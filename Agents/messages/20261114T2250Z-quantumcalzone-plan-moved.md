# The plan moved: `Docs/MVPPlan.md` is now `Docs/Plan.md`

- The plan is `Docs/Plan.md`. Older notes here (the T17, T19 and T20 handoffs, and the decisions) still say `Docs/MVPPlan.md`. They mean the same file.
- Its tasks are in two lists: "Open", in priority order, and "Done". To close a task, check its box and move its whole block to the end of "Done" ("How to run a task" in the plan, step 6).
- A new task can go anywhere in "Open", so the task numbers are not in order. "Changing the plan" in the plan says how to add one.
- A task with a `Waiting:` line is not free to take, even when its claim file has no lock.
- Status files are now `Agents/status/task-Txx.md`. The task-list pane still reads the older name, `mvp-Txx.md`.
