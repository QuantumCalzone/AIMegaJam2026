# T122 New costumes, part 2: the 8 new costumes' models, fitted to every character

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task's title line, **Model and effort**, **Depends on**, **Needs**, **Touches** and any **Waiting:** or **Blocked:** line are in its entry there, under "Open".

## Task

- **Why:** T121 draws a costume of their own for the 8 roles that play bare-headed since the costume pass of 2026-11-19 (see its Why). This task makes their models, fits them to every robot and dresses the 8 seats, so every role in every story is in costume again. It does for these costumes what T104 did for the first 13.
- **What must keep working:**
  - Each robot takes off its costume at Scene Prep and puts on its seat's costume when the scene starts (`BP_Robot.WatchShow`, `DressFromShow`, `SetHeadpiece`).
  - A cut robot keeps its costume.
  - The 13 costumes already in the game keep their fits.
  - Nothing in this task changes `BP_Show`.
- **Steps:**
  1. **Read first:** T121's results in `Docs/Art/Costumes/README.md` (the 8 costumes, their versions and colors) and `Docs/tasks/T121.md`. Then `Docs/tasks/T104.md`, its steps and findings (how the models were made, imported and fitted), and the `_readme` of `Content/NHOS/Data/costumes.json`. Read a `BP_Robot` graph only if you change it.
  2. **Generate.** Make one model per version from T121's clean views, the way T104 did. Tell me the tool and why. Before the first paid call, tell me the estimated credits and ask once. Ask again only if the cost would go over that estimate. Save each model, its thumbnail and its log in `Docs/Art/Costumes/<costume>/<version>/`. Show me a thumbnail sheet, ask for my OK, and remake what I reject.
  3. **Import.** Import into `Content/NHOS/Stage/Costumes/<costume>/` as T104 did: Nanite off, on `MI_Clay_Neutral`. A costume part off the head, if T121 has one, gets its attach point on `BP_Robot` (lock it first).
  4. **The data.** Add each costume to `costumes.json`: its meshes, a `test_color`, a fit per character and its `top`. Mind T104's findings on `HeadSocket` space and the +Y facing. Give the 8 seats in `scenes.json` their costume ids, and the colors T121 approved. Every seat has a costume again, so take out the empty-headpiece allowance in `Tools/check_prompts.py` (its docstring and `costume_problems`) and the sentence about it in `scenes.json`'s `_readme`.
  5. **Fit by eye.** With `costume_test`, take a screenshot of each new costume on all five robots at the house camera, plus a close shot of the heads. Fix each fit until nothing floats, clips into the head, or covers the eyes, the face or the name tag. Make a grid of the final shots (costumes by characters), and show it to me for my OK next to T121's fit sheets.
  6. **Check in a show.** Play a PIE show (Test for Free, 1600 × 900). Take a screenshot of the first turn of a scene whose card has a new costume, with each robot in its seat's costume. Play a free autoplay show in a game window to `NHOS: SHOW END`. Its log has no `NHOS: COSTUME ... no headpiece` line.
  7. **Record it.** Add the new models to `Docs/Assets.md`, with the tool, the generation ids and the license. Show me the edit to the design doc's Costumes section in `Docs/Design/ArtInspiration.md`: the grid, and the table without its "Its own, to come" row. Make it after my OK.
- **Done when:**
  1. Each of the 8 new costumes sits right on every character, my cube head included, in the grid I approved.
  2. Every seat in `scenes.json` names a costume, and `python Tools/check_prompts.py --offline` passes without the empty-headpiece allowance.
  3. A free autoplay show reaches `NHOS: SHOW END` with no "no headpiece" line.
  4. `Docs/Assets.md`, `Docs/Art/Costumes/` and the design doc record the new costumes.
