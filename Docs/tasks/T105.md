## Task

- **Why:** my request on 2026-11-15: after I approve the concepts, the art is generated with the Meshy API or the Hyper3D API. T97 got my approval on all 28 props, each with a clean view and its size. This task makes them into models and puts them in the game in place of T41's and T43's.
- **What must keep working:**
  - Each card's four props on the prop table (T45: `props.json`, `BP_Prop.SetProp`, `BP_StageDirector.ResetStage`).
  - A robot grabs a prop into its `HandSocket`, throws it and gives it (T62).
  - The prop names that the wants, the notes and the clue use. The ids don't change.
- **Steps:**
  1. **Read first:** T97's README and views in `Docs/Art/Props/`; `Docs/PropArt.md` (how T41 made and T43 imported the old props, with their scale and yaw in `Docs/PropArt/manifest.json`); `Content/NHOS/Data/props.json`; and the `BP_Prop` graphs you change (list them, then read only those).
  2. **Generate.** Make each prop from its view with Meshy (`Tools/art_gen.py mesh-image`, the PC's `Local/meshy_key.json`), or with Hyper3D where Meshy does a prop badly. Tell me which one and why. Before the first paid call, tell me the estimated credits and ask once (28 props at 30 credits each, with textures, is 840 credits). Ask again only if the cost would go over that. Save each model, its thumbnail and its log in `Docs/Art/Props/<card id>/<prop id>/`. Show me a thumbnail sheet per card next to its approved views, ask for my OK, and regenerate what I reject.
  3. **Import.** Import into `Content/NHOS/Stage/Props/<card id>/` over the old props, or as new assets with the references in `props.json` updated. Scale each prop to the size T97 gave, and turn it to face the camera. Write each one's scale and yaw in a manifest, `Docs/Art/Props/manifest.json`. Delete the old props and their materials once nothing uses them.
  4. **Check it in the game.** In a PIE show (Test for Free, 1600 × 900), take a screenshot of each card's prop table and of a robot holding one prop from each card. Use autoplay's random allowed actions, or `force` switches if there are any, so grabs and throws happen. Play a free autoplay show in a game window to `NHOS: SHOW END`. Judge the screenshots as the build notes say: each prop reads at the house camera, sits in the hand and is the right size next to the robots.
  5. **Record it.** In `Docs/Assets.md`, replace the prop rows with the new models: the tool, the generation ids and the license (Meshy Pro, or Hyper3D Business).
- **Done when:**
  1. All 28 props are in the game from models I approved, at their sizes, and the old props are deleted.
  2. Each card's prop table and a held prop look right in the screenshots.
  3. A free autoplay show reaches `NHOS: SHOW END`.
  4. `Docs/Assets.md`, `Docs/Art/Props/` and the manifest record every prop.
