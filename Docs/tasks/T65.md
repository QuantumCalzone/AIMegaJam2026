# T65 The speech field: a word counter, focus and @ mentions

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task's title line, **Model and effort**, **Depends on**, **Needs**, **Touches** and any **Waiting:** or **Blocked:** line are in its entry there, under "Open".

## Task

- **Why:** the input spec in [T62.md](T62.md) ("The input spec": row 3 of "Layout", and "Saying a line"). The input box becomes a speech field: I see how many words I have left, I never lose focus while I click, and I pick a name with @ instead of typing it. It takes over the @ half of T31, which was dropped on 2026-11-15.
- **Steps:**
  1. **The speech field** is the input box in the input row, with the hint "Say something (optional)...". To its right: a live word counter ("12/15"), then T64's Action!, then room for T66's timer.
  2. **The 15-word cap.** The counter counts words as `CapWords` does, so a mention is one word. It turns to `UIStyle`'s warning red at 15, and a 16th word can't be typed: when the text changes, cut it back to 15 words and keep the caret at the end. A paste is cut the same way.
  3. **Focus stays here.** The speech field holds keyboard focus for my whole turn. It takes focus when each of my turns opens, and again after any click on the builder, my card or the chat. T64 made the builder unfocusable; check that nothing else on the screen takes focus.
  4. **The @ list.** Typing "@" opens a small list above the field: the actors still in the show, by the names on my screen, in their colors, never me. Letters after the "@" filter it. Up and Down move in it; Enter, Tab or a click puts the name in; Esc closes it. While it is open, Enter picks a name and doesn't send. Catch the keys in `WBP_Stage`'s `OnPreviewKeyDown` and return Handled, because by default Tab moves the focus to the next widget (T31's note).
  5. **A mention is a chip.** A picked name shows as a chip in the actor's color. UMG's editable text can't hold a widget inline; if that proves true, insert "@Meta " as one unit (one Backspace right after it removes the whole mention) and draw it as a chip in T64's preview line. Write under "## Findings" what you built. It reaches the AIs as "@Player 5" through `ResolveMentions`, as it does now.
  6. **Speech never acts.** Nothing typed in the field changes the builder. (T63 already makes the input LLM leave actions alone.)
- **Done when:**
  - In PIE (Test for Free, autoplay off, a 1600 × 900 window), screenshots show: the hint in an empty field; the counter at 12/15; the counter red at 15, with a 16th word refused; "@" with the list of AI names (not me); "@me" filtered to Meta; the mention as a chip.
  - Typing, clicking Grab and typing on puts every letter in the field.
  - A line sent with a mention prints `NHOS: TYPED` with "@Meta", and its `NHOS: LOG` line has "@Player N".
  - A free autoplay show in a game window reaches `NHOS: SHOW END` with no `LogScript` line.
