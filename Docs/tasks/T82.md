## Task

- **Why:** T81's Hyper3D models take the place of the engine shapes that make OpenAI's, Anthropic's and SpaceXAI's head shell, bodies and arms (T79), so the three look like their concept images. Everything that moves, poses or attaches to them must keep working. T83 does Meta and my robot the same way.
- **Steps:**
  1. **Read** T81's findings and sheet, T79's findings, and the "For later tasks" notes in T47 and T75. Start from `Graphs/BP_Robot/` as pushed. Before you change anything, take a close-up of each of the three bots, for the comparison in step 7.
  2. **Import** the parts into `Content/NHOS/Stage/Cast/<bot>/` (the shared head shell into `Cast/RoundHead/`). Rename each material and texture right after its import, as T43 had to.   3. **The material.** The seat color is on the body, and on a cut every `seat` part goes gray (`ResetLook`, `SetCut`). Pick one, and write why under "## Findings": a Substrate material that shows the model's texture and has a parameter that the cut turns gray; or the model's mesh on today's `M_Clay` instances in the seat color, if the texture reads worse than the clay. Build it with Substrate nodes.
  4. **Swap the parts** in `SetPersona`: the models take the place of the engine shapes for the head shell, the body and the arms, on the same components or on new ones under the same parents (the arms under `ArmPivotL` and `ArmPivotR`, the head shell where `RHead` is). If a hat floats or sinks, change the round head's fit in `Docs/tasks/T47-build/make_costumes.py` and build `costumes.json` again.
  6. **Keep every motion:** OpenAI's hover, the idle bob, the talking bounce, the arm flap outward (T79's sign), the cheer, the fold, the hops, grab, throw and give, the shake, the cut slump with the gray, and T75's faces and gestures. Re-place the five robots in `L_Stage` if template changes don't reach them (the T17 and T46 rule).
