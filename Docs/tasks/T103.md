## Task

- **Why:** my request on 2026-11-15: after I approve the concepts, the art is generated with the Meshy API or the Hyper3D API. T95 got my approval on the five characters and their turnarounds. This task makes them into models and puts them on the robots in place of T82's and T83's.
- **What must keep working:**
  - **The sockets.** Every robot keeps `HeadSocket`, `BackSocket` and `HandSocket` (T79), placed for the new shapes. T104 fits the costumes to them, and the props ride in `HandSocket`.
  - **The face.** `Face` and its parts, and `FaceParts` and `FaceRest` updated for the new face (T75's "For later tasks"), so `Emote` still poses every emotion. T77 and T78 build on it.
  - **The seat color.** The parts that take the seat color are tagged `seat` (T82).
  - **What the show needs:** the speech bubble's anchor (`BP_Robot.Say`, `Typing` and `Hush`, from T38), the name tag and the "(passed)" marker (T57), the cut robot's fall, and its getting back up if T87 is done.
  - **My robot:** my real eyes behind the box head's slots (T80, T83).
- **Steps:**
  1. **Read first:** T95's turnarounds and README in `Docs/Art/Cast/`; `Docs/tasks/T81.md`, `T82.md` and `T83.md` (how the last cast was generated and swapped in, and their findings); "For later tasks" in `T75.md`; the `BP_Robot` graphs you change (list them, then read only those); and `Docs/CastArt/` for the old logs.
  2. **Generate.** Make each character's parts from its approved turnaround. Use Hyper3D, through the Mac's `hyper3d` MCP server or its API with a key that I put in `Local/`, or Meshy, through `Tools/art_gen.py mesh-image` with the PC's `Local/meshy_key.json`. Pick per part, as T81 did, and tell me which one and why. Split each robot into parts as T81 did, where the face, the arms or the seat-colored parts need to move or take their own material. Before the first paid call, tell me the estimated credits and ask once. Ask again only if the cost would go over that. Save each part's model, textures, thumbnail and `log.json` (tool, prompt, settings, generation id and cost) in `Docs/Art/Cast/<character>/<part>/`.
  3. **Check each model against its concept** before you import it: the shape, the proportions, and the place for the face and for the costumes. Show me a thumbnail sheet of all five next to their approved concepts, and ask for my OK. Regenerate a part I reject.
  4. **Import and swap.** Import into `Content/NHOS/Stage/Cast/<character>/` on a Substrate material (T82's `M_CastModel`, or a new one if T94's style guide calls for it). Swap the parts into `BP_Robot`, keeping everything in "What must keep working". Delete the old cast meshes and materials that nothing uses any more. If the robots' sizes changed, check their places in `L_Stage` and the house camera's frame.
  5. **Check it in the game:**
     - With `"emote_test": true`, take a screenshot of each robot in each emotion.
     - In a PIE show (Test for Free, 1600 × 900), take a screenshot of each phase: Scene Prep, a scene turn with a bubble, Free Talk with a "(passed)" marker, The Cut with a fall, and the end screen.
     - Play a free autoplay show in a game window to `NHOS: SHOW END`.
     - If T85 is done, check that its hover cards still fit the new robots.

     Judge them as the build notes say.
  6. **Record it.** In `Docs/Assets.md`, replace the cast rows with the new parts: the tool, the generation ids and the license (Hyper3D on my Business plan, or Meshy on the Pro plan, as before). Write in the findings what T104 must know about each head: the socket, the top, the size and where a costume can sit.
- **Done when:**
  1. All five robots wear their new models, which I approved against the concepts, and the old cast meshes that nothing uses are deleted.
  2. Every emotion poses on every robot, and the bubble, the name tag, the "(passed)" marker and the cut robot's fall all work, in the screenshots.
  3. A free autoplay show reaches `NHOS: SHOW END`.
  4. `Docs/Assets.md` and `Docs/Art/Cast/` record every part, with its tool, generation id, cost and license.
