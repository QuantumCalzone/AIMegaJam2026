# T95 New art, part 2: concepts for the cast, me included, approved by me

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task's title line, **Model and effort**, **Depends on**, **Needs**, **Touches** and any **Waiting:** or **Blocked:** line are in its entry there, under "Open".

## Task

- **Why:** my request on 2026-11-15: all the art goes back to concept art and waits for my approval before anything is generated. This task draws the five characters in T94's direction: the four AI robots (OpenAI, Anthropic, SpaceXAI and Meta) and my robot, a person in a cardboard costume whose head is a cube. T103 then turns the approved concepts into models.
- **What the concepts must allow:**
  - **Heads that take costumes.** T96 fits a costume for each role onto every character, so each head needs a clear place for a hat, a wig or a hood. My robot's head is a cube, so its costumes must fit a cube (T96).
  - **A face that shows emotions.** T75's `Emote` poses a face on each robot. Keep a face that can be posed: eyes and a mouth, or the screen face. T77 and T78 build on it.
  - **Sockets.** Each robot has a `HeadSocket`, a `BackSocket` and a `HandSocket` (T79), and holds up to two props.
  - **Readable at the house camera's distance,** at 1600 × 900: each robot is told apart by its silhouette and its seat color.
  - **Each bot goes by its provider.** No real company's logo, mark or product appears on a robot (my memory note `providers-not-personas`, and the brand rules in T36).
  - **Me among them.** My robot is a person in a costume who is trying to pass as a robot, and the game's joke depends on that. Its box head has eye slots that my real eyes look through.
- **Steps:**
  1. **Read first:** T94's style guide in `Docs/Art/Direction/README.md`, `Docs/BotArt.md` (how the last cast was chosen, and why), the design doc's Art Inspiration tab (the bots), "For later tasks" in `Docs/tasks/T75.md`, and T79's notes on sockets.
  2. **First round.** For each character, make three or four candidates from T94's prompt text with `art_gen.py image`, and show them to me side by side. Before the first paid call, tell me the estimated cost of this task's generations and ask once. Ask again only if the cost would go over that.
  3. **Rounds until I approve.** Change a picked image with `art_gen.py edit` (my memory note `edit-picked-images`), never by drawing it again from text. Ask me one question at a time. Approve each character on its own, then all five together in one lineup image, so they read as one cast.
  4. **Turnarounds for image-to-3D.** For each approved character, make a clean front view and a side view on a plain background, with no costume or prop, in the pose that Meshy and Hyper3D handle best. Meshy and Hyper3D build a model from these images. Show me the turnarounds, and approve them as matching the concept.
  5. **Record it.** Keep the rounds, the picks and the turnarounds in `Docs/Art/Cast/<character>/`. Write each round, my words, the prompts, the generation ids and the cost in `Docs/Art/Cast/README.md`.
  6. **The design doc.** Show me the edit to the bots section of the Art Inspiration tab: the approved lineup and a line for each character. Make it after my OK, in its file in `Docs/Design/`.
- **Done when:**
  1. I approved each of the five characters, and the lineup of all five.
  2. Each approved character has a front and side turnaround that I approved, in `Docs/Art/Cast/<character>/`.
  3. `Docs/Art/Cast/README.md` logs every round and its cost.
  4. The bots section of the Art Inspiration tab shows the approved cast, after my OK.
