# T38 Every line plays in a speech bubble, and the chat opens on a click

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task's title line, **Model and effort**, **Depends on**, **Needs**, **Touches** and any **Waiting:** or **Blocked:** line are in its entry there, under "Open".

## Task

- **Why:** my request on 2026-11-15. I want to watch the talk on the stage, not read it in a panel. Every line an actor says shows as a speech bubble over its robot. The chat history stays, so I can find a line I missed, but it is hidden until I click to open it, and another click hides it again.
- **Steps:**
  1. **A speech bubble widget,** `WBP_SpeechBubble` in `Content/NHOS/UI/`:
     - A rounded panel with a tail that points down at the speaker's head. The panel's edge is in the speaker's color (`SeatColors`), so the bubble also shows who is talking without the label.
     - The bubble holds the speech. A line that has an action but no speech shows its short action instead, in the chat's action style, so no line is lost.
     - The text is 18 pt or larger, wraps at a fixed width (about 420 px at 1600 × 900), and has 4.5:1 contrast or better on the bubble's fill.
     - The colors, sizes and padding come from T32's style Blueprint, not from values in the widget.
  2. **Each robot has a bubble.** The bubble sits above the robot's name label, which is at 228 cm.
     - Start with a `WidgetComponent` in screen space on `BP_Robot`, so the text keeps one size and faces the camera. Give `BP_Robot` two functions: `Say(Text, Color)` and `Hush()`.
     - If a bubble in screen space can't be kept inside the window or clear of the panels, have `WBP_Stage` place the bubbles instead: project each robot's head point to the screen every frame. Write why under "## Findings".
     - The bubble stays inside the window's 24 px margin and clear of the header, my card, the chat and the input row. It never covers the reveal stamp. A bubble that would run off an edge or into a panel moves sideways or down, and its tail still points at its robot.
     - A cut robot shows no bubble.
  3. **The bubble shows when its line plays.** When T30's line queue plays a line (the chat line appears, the robot lights up and acts), the queue calls `Say` on that seat's robot.
     - Every line an actor says on stage goes this way: scene turns, Free Talk, votes with their reasons, and reactions. My lines go this way too, over my robot, the one labeled "You".
     - A bubble stays up while its line is the newest. When the next line plays, the old bubble fades out over about 0.3 s, so at most one bubble is full and one is fading. The last line's bubble stays up until the next line plays or the phase changes.
     - Print `NHOS: BUBBLE <seat id>` each time a bubble shows.
     - Game lines are not talk. They stay on T29's banner and in the header.
  4. **The typing line becomes a bubble.** T30's "<name> is typing..." shows as a small bubble with three dots over that robot, so I still see it while the chat is hidden. The real line's bubble replaces it.
  5. **The chat opens on a click.**
     - The chat (`ChatSize` and the log in `LowerPanel`) is hidden when each show starts. The input row with its hint, Send, Pass, the vote row and "See the results" stay at the bottom left, with nothing above them.
     - Add a button to the input row, in T32's shared button style, that reads "Show chat" when the chat is hidden and "Hide chat" when it is shown. One click shows the chat above the input row, newest line at the bottom, as T27 laid it out. Another click hides it.
     - The chat stays as I set it through every phase, until the next show starts.
     - The chat still gets every line while it is hidden, so it is complete when I open it.
     - On my turn, the focus goes back to the input box after the click, so I can type at once.
     - Print `NHOS: CHAT shown` or `NHOS: CHAT hidden` on each click.
  6. **Only my screen changes.** The shared log the AIs read, `game_messages` and every `NHOS: LOG` line stay as they are. `ShowLine` stays the one place where a line reaches my screen.
  7. **The design doc follows the game.** Show me the matching edit to the doc's UI direction (`Docs/UIStyle.md` links it): in the layout, the chat is behind a button, and the speech bubble is a new component with its states. If the turns read differently, show me an edit to the Rules tab too. Make the edits after my OK, as the build notes say.
- **Done when:**
  - Screenshots at 1600 × 900 of a scene turn, Free Talk, the vote and the reactions after a cut show the newest line in a bubble over its speaker, with the chat hidden.
  - A screenshot of the same scene turn with the chat shown has every line so far.
  - A long AI line's bubble wraps and stays inside the window. The bubble of a robot at Right stays clear of my card. My own line's bubble shows over my robot.
  - A free autoplay show reaches `NHOS: SHOW END` with no new `LogScript` warning. It has one `NHOS: BUBBLE` line for each `NHOS: LINE` line of a seat still on stage, and its `NHOS: LOG` lines have the same form as before.
  - I play one show in a game window and say I can follow the talk from the bubbles, and that the chat opens and hides on a click.
