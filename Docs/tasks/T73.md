# T73 Lines type out, one voice sound per syllable

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task's title line, **Model and effort**, **Depends on**, **Needs**, **Touches** and any **Waiting:** or **Blocked:** line are in its entry there, under "Open".

## Task

- **Why:** my request on 2026-11-15. A line's words type out on screen like a typewriter, and the speaker's voice (T72) babbles one sound for each syllable as the words appear, as in Animal Crossing. Today a whole line appears at once, with one pluck (T20, T30).
- **Steps:**
  1. **The speech types out.**
     - When the line queue plays a line (`BPC_LinePlayer.PlayCur`), its chat line shows the name and the action at once. Then the speech types out letter by letter, at `type_cps` letters a second. `type_cps` is a new value in `Content/NHOS/Data/pacing.json`; start at about 30. When `fast_lines` is on, `fast_scale` scales the typing like every other wait.
     - Every line types out this way: scene turns, Free Talk, votes with their reasons, reactions and my own lines.
     - The chat line has its final size from the first letter, so the chat doesn't jump while a line types. For example, lay out the whole text and hide the part that isn't typed yet.
  2. **One sound for each syllable.**
     - Split each word into syllables. Each run of vowels (a, e, i, o, u and y) is one syllable, a silent "e" at the end of a word is not, and every word has at least one.
     - When the typing reaches a syllable's first letter, call `BPC_ShowAudio.Syllable(Name, IsMe)` (T72) for the speaker.
     - Punctuation adds a short pause and no sound: about 0.15 s after a comma, and about 0.3 s after a period, question mark or exclamation mark.
     - Play at most about 12 sounds a second, so it stays babble and doesn't turn into a buzz.
  3. **The hold comes after the typing.** The next line waits until the typing is done, then the line holds for reading as T30 made it.
     - Retune `line_base`, `line_per_word`, `line_min` and `line_max` in `pacing.json` so a scene takes about as long as before.
     - Time the first scene of a free autoplay show without `fast_lines`, before and after the change, and write both times under "## Findings".
  4. **The pluck goes.** Remove T20's call that plays one blip per line from `PlayCur`. Delete `BP_Show.LineBlip` and `BPC_ShowAudio.Blip` too if this task holds those locks. If it doesn't, leave them unused and say so under "## Findings".
  5. **"Is typing..." stays** as T30 made it, before an AI line in Free Talk and in the reactions.
  6. **Only my screen and my speakers change.** The shared log, the `NHOS: LOG` lines and what the AIs read stay as they are. The `NHOS: LINE` line gets two new fields: `syl=<syllables>` and `type=<seconds of typing>`.
  7. **The design doc follows the game.** If the doc's Rules tab or UI direction says how a line appears, show me the matching edit, and make it after my OK.
- **Done when:**
  - A free autoplay show with `fast_lines` reaches `NHOS: SHOW END` with no new `LogScript` warning. Every `NHOS: LINE` line has `syl=` and `type=`, and `syl` is above 0 on every line that has speech.
  - Claude plays one show by hand in PIE (Test for Free, 1600 × 900). It takes screenshots of a line half typed in a scene turn, a line half typed in Free Talk, and my own line typing. From them, it checks that the chat doesn't jump while a line types and that the lines can still be read in time.
  - The first scene's time before and after the change is under "## Findings".
  - Claude can't hear the result. Under "## Findings", it writes that the sound and the pace of the babble are not checked.
