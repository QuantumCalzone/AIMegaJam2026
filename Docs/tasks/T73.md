# T73 Lines type out, one voice sound per syllable

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task's title line, **Model and effort**, **Depends on**, **Needs**, **Touches** and any **Waiting:** or **Blocked:** line are in its entry there, under "Open".

## Task

- **Why:** my request on 2026-11-15. A line's words type out on screen like a typewriter, and the speaker's voice (T72) babbles one sound for each syllable as the words appear, as in Animal Crossing. Today a whole line appears at once, with one pluck (T20, T30).
- **Steps:**
  1. **The speech types out.**
     - When the line queue plays a line (`BPC_LinePlayer.PlayCur`), its chat line shows the name and the action at once. Then the speech types out letter by letter, at `type_cps` letters a second. `type_cps` is a new value in `Content/NHOS/Data/pacing.json`; start at about 30. When `fast_lines` is on, `fast_scale` scales the typing like every other wait.
     - Every line types out this way: scene turns, Free Talk, votes with their reasons, reactions and my own lines.
     - The chat line has its final size from the first letter, so the chat doesn't jump while a line types. For example, lay out the whole text and hide the part that isn't typed yet.
  2. **One sound for each syllable.**
     - Split each word into syllables. Each run of vowels (a, e, i, o, u and y) is one syllable, a silent "e" at the end of a word is not, and every word has at least one.
     - When the typing reaches a syllable's first letter, call `BPC_ShowAudio.Syllable(Name, IsMe)` (T72) for the speaker.
     - Punctuation adds a short pause and no sound: about 0.15 s after a comma, and about 0.3 s after a period, question mark or exclamation mark.
     - Play at most about 12 sounds a second, so it stays babble and doesn't turn into a buzz.
  3. **The hold comes after the typing.** The next line waits until the typing is done, then the line holds for reading as T30 made it.
     - Retune `line_base`, `line_per_word`, `line_min` and `line_max` in `pacing.json` so a scene takes about as long as before.
     - Time the first scene of a free autoplay show without `fast_lines`, before and after the change, and write both times under "## Findings".
  4. **The pluck goes.** Remove T20's call that plays one blip per line from `PlayCur`. Delete `BP_Show.LineBlip` and `BPC_ShowAudio.Blip` too if this task holds those locks. If it doesn't, leave them unused and say so under "## Findings".
  5. **"Is typing..." stays** as T30 made it, before an AI line in Free Talk and in the reactions.
  6. **Only my screen and my speakers change.** The shared log, the `NHOS: LOG` lines and what the AIs read stay as they are. The `NHOS: LINE` line gets two new fields: `syl=<syllables>` and `type=<seconds of typing>`.
  7. **The design doc follows the game.** If the doc's Rules tab or UI direction says how a line appears, show me the matching edit, and make it after my OK.
- **Done when:**
  - A free autoplay show with `fast_lines` reaches `NHOS: SHOW END` with no new `LogScript` warning. Every `NHOS: LINE` line has `syl=` and `type=`, and `syl` is above 0 on every line that has speech.
  - Claude plays one show by hand in PIE (Test for Free, 1600 × 900). It takes screenshots of a line half typed in a scene turn, a line half typed in Free Talk, and my own line typing. From them, it checks that the chat doesn't jump while a line types and that the lines can still be read in time.
  - The first scene's time before and after the change is under "## Findings".
  - Claude can't hear the result. Under "## Findings", it writes that the sound and the pace of the babble are not checked.

## Findings

The sheet's re-checks held: since the sheet's commit, only T71 touched these sources, and only other `BPC_ShowAudio` functions (`Syllable` is still `Syllable(Name, IsMe)`; nothing but `PlayCur` called `LineBlip`, and nothing but `LineBlip` called `Blip`, which a byte search of every `.uasset` under `Content/` confirmed). `pacing.json` had T30's numbers.

### What was built

- **`WBP_ChatLine`:** `SpeechText` now sits in a new Overlay, `SpeechStack`, with a new TextBlock, `SpeechShown`, on top of it (same font, wrap, shadow and justification; Collapsed until a line types). Three new functions: `TypeBegin(Speech)` hides `SpeechText` at render opacity 0 (it keeps its full size) and shows `SpeechShown`; `TypeTo(N)` shows the first N letters; `TypeEnd()` brings `SpeechText` back and collapses `SpeechShown`. `SetActor` is unchanged, so the cast rows that reuse the widget are unchanged.
- **`WBP_Stage.LastChatLine()`:** the last child of `LogScroll`, cast to `WBP_ChatLine` (`AddChatLine` adds there and nothing else does).
- **`BPC_LinePlayer`:** `PlayCur` plans the line (`PlanType` with `PlanChar`, `WordStart`, `SylAt`, `EndWord`, `PauseAt`), prints `NHOS: LINE <id> hold=<s> syl=<n> type=<s>`, adds the chat line and starts the typing (`StartType`). A looping 1/60 s timer (`TypeTick`, `TypeStep`, `TickChar`, `TickHit`) shows every letter whose time has come by the real clock, plays one syllable through `BPC_ShowAudio.Syllable` (found by `FindAudio` on the owner) when a new letter starts one and the last sound is at least 1/12 s old, and ends with `EndType`, which starts T30's hold. `Clear` stops a line that is still typing (`StopType`). `LoadPacing` reads the four new values through `PaceF` (a value or its default) and prints `type_cps` on the `NHOS: PACING` line. "<name> is typing..." is unchanged.
- **The blip is gone:** `PlayCur` no longer calls it, and `BP_Show.LineBlip` and `BPC_ShowAudio.Blip` are deleted (T73 held both locks), with their `Graphs/` files. `Sfx`'s `blip` branch and `S_TalkBlip` stay, unused (the sheet's decision 12; deleting the asset would also mean its row in `Docs/Assets.md`).
- **`pacing.json`:** `type_cps` 30, `type_pause_comma` 0.15, `type_pause_stop` 0.3, `syl_max_rate` 12, and the hold retuned to `line_base` 0.6, `line_per_word` 0.12, `line_min` 1.5, `line_max` 2.5, as the sheet worked out.
- **Decisions:** the sheet's 14 "Decisions made for my person" all stand as written there; please look them over. Four things differ from the sheet:
  1. **The typing plans the speech as the chat line shows it.** `AddChatLine` runs the speech through `WBP_Stage.CleanText` (trims it, folds double spaces, drops emoji) before `SetActor`, which the sheet didn't know. Typing the raw `CurSpeech` could have put the typed part out of step with the hidden full copy, so `PlayCur` plans and types `CleanText(CurSpeech)`. The hold still counts the words of `CurSpeech`.
  2. **The 4 px gap above the speech moved inside the overlay.** `WrapWidgets` gave `SpeechStack` the old slot's top padding of 4, but an overlay whose children are all collapsed still keeps its slot padding, so an action-only line, a pass or a cast row with no speech would have grown 4 px. The padding is now on the two overlay slots (top 4, fill, top-aligned) and `SpeechStack`'s slot has none, so such lines look as before.
  3. **`TypeStep` walks only the letters not shown yet** (`(range TypePos TypeLen)`), not the whole line each tick.
  4. **The curly apostrophe counts as part of a word,** as the sheet suggested if the DSL took it: it reads back as `’` in the word set, so "don’t" is one word.

### Unreal MCP rules found in this task

- **`ObjectTools` input names:** `list_properties(instance)`, `get_properties(instance, properties)` and `set_properties(instance, values)`, where `values` is a JSON string, not an object.
- **An Overlay whose children are all collapsed keeps its own slot padding** in a VerticalBox, while a collapsed TextBlock in that slot took none. Put padding inside the overlay when the wrapped widget can collapse.
- **`read_graph_dsl` leaves out pins at their default** (`GetSubstring`'s `Length 1` reads back as `(GetSubstring _text I)`), and `(print ...)` reads back as `PrintString` with print-to-screen off.
- **`(for _i (range start stop) ...)` works,** with variables for both ends.
- **This session's Bash tool rejected long heredocs** that wrote several files at once ("unexpected EOF while looking for matching `''"); the same text went through the Write tool.
