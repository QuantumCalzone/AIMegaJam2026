# T108 Meta's seat color becomes mustard, in the UI and on the robot

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task's title line, **Model and effort**, **Depends on**, **Needs**, **Touches** and any **Waiting:** or **Blocked:** line are in its entry there, under "Open".

## Task

- **Why:** in T95 I redesigned Meta as a nod to Data: a black and mustard top and black pants, with no blue left on it. I chose "Black & mustard, seat goes mustard", so Meta's seat color changes from #5B8CFF blue to mustard. The seat color is the body color and the UI's color for that bot (its name, chat bar, name tag and ballot ticket), so the two must change together. The bots section of `Docs/Design/ArtInspiration.md` already says mustard, with the hex left for this task.
- **Where Meta's blue is now:**
  - `UIStyle.SeatColor` (`Content/NHOS/UI/Style/UIStyle.uasset`; source in `Graphs/UIStyle/SeatColor.txt`): the Meta branch returns linear (0.1046, 0.2623, 1.0). Every widget that colors a seat calls it (`WBP_Chip`, `WBP_ChatLine`, `WBP_Stage`, `WBP_Keys`, `WBP_EndCard`, `WBP_SpeechBubble`, `WBP_ActionBuilder`, and `BP_Show`'s `StagePrep` and `ShowLine`), so those need no change.
  - `BP_Robot.SetPersona` (`Graphs/BP_Robot/SetPersona.txt`): its own copy, the `_p` value (0.105, 0.262, 1.0) in `SetBaseColor`, which colors every part tagged `seat`.
  - The docs: the bots table and the UI section's "Palette and type" line in `Docs/Design/ArtInspiration.md`, and the palette table in `Docs/Art/Direction/README.md` (with its rule that OpenAI and Meta stay apart by lightness).
- **What to watch:** mustard sits close to the UI's gold, which means act now (#C9A04A, and "nothing else in the play area is gold"), to Anthropic's orange (#E8894A), and to the audience's mustard body (#C9A43F in T94's palette, which T98 may use). `Docs/Art/Cast/README.md` ("For later tasks") has the background.
- **Steps:**
  1. **Read first:** `Docs/Art/Cast/README.md` ("Decisions my person made" and "For later tasks"), the UI section's "Palette and type" in `Docs/Design/ArtInspiration.md`, and `Graphs/README.md`.
  2. **My pick.** Make a free swatch sheet on the Mac (a Swift script, as `Docs/Art/Cast/` did) with two or three mustard candidates beside the other four seat colors, the UI gold, Anthropic's orange and the audience's mustard, each as a chat bar with a name on it and as a patch of clay body color. Recommend the one that stays clearest of the gold and the orange, and ask me to pick.
  3. **The game.** Lock `UIStyle.uasset` and `BP_Robot.uasset`. Change the Meta branch of `SeatColor` and the `_p` value in `SetPersona` to the pick's linear values (converted from sRGB, as the other seats are), starting from their `Graphs/` files and keeping them in step. Search the other `Graphs/` files and any asset they don't cover for Meta's old blue, and change what you find.
  4. **Check.** Play a free show in PIE (Test for Free): Meta's body, name tag, chat bar and ballot ticket are all the new mustard, the other seats are unchanged, and nothing gold in the play area is confused with it. Take a screenshot for the task file.
  5. **The docs, after my OK:** the hex in the bots table and the UI palette line of `ArtInspiration.md`, and the palette table in `Docs/Art/Direction/README.md`, with its OpenAI and Meta rule brought up to date.
- **Done when:**
  1. I picked the hex.
  2. In a free PIE show, Meta's body and all its UI show the new mustard, and the other seats are unchanged.
  3. `Graphs/UIStyle/SeatColor.txt` and `Graphs/BP_Robot/SetPersona.txt` match what is in the assets.
  4. The design doc and the style guide list the new hex, after my OK.
