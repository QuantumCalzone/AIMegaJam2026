- **The rules.** The rules for a costume are T96's, in `Docs/tasks/T96.md`:
  - It is at least a headpiece. A part off the head, such as a collar, needs my OK (given for the leader's collar), and T122 gives it an attach point.
  - It never hides the face, the mouth, the seat color or the name tag.
  - It is a homemade parody, never a film's real costume.

  Two rules are new. A new costume belongs to its own story: no role on another card wears it. Two cards share a costume only if it fits both stories and I say so. A role's color may change with its costume, if I approve.
- **Tools on the PC.** T96 ran on the Mac, and its scripts run only there: `gen.py` locks `log.json` with `fcntl` and converts each take with `sips`, and `fit.py` builds every canvas and sheet with `canvas.swift`. On the PC, first port those steps to Pillow (Pillow 13 is installed with Python 3.13 there) and keep the Mac path working, or run the task on the Mac. `Tools/art_gen.py` is standard library only and runs on both.
