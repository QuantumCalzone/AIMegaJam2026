# T66 The turn timer, the auto-send and the other phases

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). Done on 2026-11-16 on the PC, in the chain's editor, from [T66-build.md](T66-build.md).

## Task

- **Model and effort:** Opus 5.5, high. A timer that sends whatever state the builder is in, and the input row's shape in four phases.
- **Depends on:** T65 (the speech row), T57 (the Pass button as it leaves it), T58 (the last word)
- **Needs:** editor + Unreal MCP running, Test for Free for the PIE shows.
- **Touches:** `Content/NHOS/Core/BP_Show.uasset` (the timeouts, each phase's input), `Content/NHOS/UI/WBP_Stage.uasset` (the timer, the input row per phase), `Content/NHOS/Data/input.json` (`timers`), `Tools/check_prompts.py`, `Graphs/` files for the graphs it writes, `Docs/tasks/T66.md`.

- **Why:** the input spec in [T62.md](T62.md) ("The input spec": "Submitting" and "Other phases"). A turn can't hang on me: a timer sends what I have built. Free Talk, the last word and the reactions have no actions, so they get only the speech field. It takes over T24, which was dropped on 2026-11-15.
- **Steps:**
  1. **The timers are data.** `Content/NHOS/Data/input.json` (T63 made it) gets `timers`, in seconds: `scene` 30, `last_word` and `reaction` their own (start each at 15, T24's value for a reaction), and `free_talk` 0, which means no timer, because the spec gives Free Talk none. `Tools/check_prompts.py` checks the new fields.
  2. **The timer** shows at the right end of the speech row ("0:21"), styled from `UIStyle`. It starts when my turn opens on screen, after the lines before it have played (T30's queue), not when the show's logic gets there.
  3. **When it runs out in a scene turn,** the turn sends itself through T63's `SendHumanTurn`: a finished action as built; an unfinished one (an action picked, its chips incomplete) as a look around, the fallback a bot gets; and the typed text as it is. Print `NHOS: TIMEOUT scene <finished|unfinished|none>`.
  4. **Free Talk:** the builder and its preview line hide. The speech field, Action! and the Pass button stay (Pass is there now: `SetPassEnabled`). No timer.
  5. **The last word (T58) and my reactions after the Cut:** the speech field only, with their own timers. When one runs out, what's typed is sent. An empty field sends nothing, the same as a pass. Print `NHOS: TIMEOUT last_word` or `NHOS: TIMEOUT reaction`.
  6. **The Cut's vote** keeps its own row; the builder stays hidden there.
  7. **No times anywhere the AIs read:** not in the shared log, not in `game_messages`.
  8. **Autoplay** sends at once and never waits for a timer.
  9. **The doc.** Check the design doc's Rules tab (T62's edit) against what this task built, and propose a fix if they differ.
- **Done when:**
  - In PIE (Test for Free, autoplay off, a 1600 × 900 window), screenshots show the timer counting down in a scene turn, Free Talk with the field and Pass but no builder, and a reaction with only the field and its timer.
  - Letting the scene timer run out with an unfinished Throw prints `NHOS: TIMEOUT scene unfinished`; my robot looks around, and my typed words reach the log.
  - A finished action left to time out plays as built.
  - A reaction left to time out with an empty field sends nothing.
  - A free autoplay show in a game window reaches `NHOS: SHOW END` with no `LogScript` line.
- **Result:** done. Each scene turn has 30 seconds, counted from when my input opens on screen and shown as "0:21" at the right end of the speech row (gold for the last 5); at 0 the turn sends itself, a finished action as built, an unfinished one as a look around, with the typed words. A last word and a reaction have 15 seconds and send what's typed, or a pass; Free Talk and the vote have none; Pass hides in scene turns; the lower panel widens while a timer shows. The seconds are `input.json` `timers` (checked by `Tools/check_prompts.py`), autoplay never waits, and no time reaches the AIs. A hand-played PIE show hit every timeout kind, a free autoplay show reached `SHOW END` clean, and on my OK ("Yes, edit the doc") the design doc's Rules tab (rev 52) and Turn Input tab (rev 7) say the same.

### Design review, as a critical first-time player

- **The timer is easy to find** at the end of the row I'm typing in, quiet lavender in the mono face, and the turn line already says it is my move. The mono font's colon has wide side bearings, so it reads "0 : 19"; it is legible, and T34 can tighten it.
- **Gold in the last 5 seconds reads as "hurry"** but is calm: no flash and no sound. It doesn't alarm, which suits a turn that sends itself anyway.
- **A turn that timed out looks like any other** in the chat and the log, as the spec wants (nothing tells the AIs). A first-time player whose half-built Throw became "looks around" gets no on-screen reason; T61's stagehand can say it once.
- **The wide panel at a reaction or a last word** draws the eye to my turn and gives the field room. It stays wide until the next header, so when another last word follows mine the panel keeps its width; nothing jumps at my send.
- **Pass is gone in scene turns,** which removes T64's odd disabled Pass beside a greyed Action!; No action is the scene turn's "say nothing".
- **Not checked:** sound (T66 adds none).
