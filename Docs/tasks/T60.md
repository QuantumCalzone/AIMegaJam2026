## Task

- **Why:** design change 11 of 12 (2026-11-15). The win screen shows each AI's reasoning, then reveals their rivals. The lose screen shows each AI's reasoning and names the main reason I was caught, with an instant restart. Today's end screen (T10) lists each AI's votes with their reasons and a closing line, and has the Restart button. Naming the top reason turns a loss into a lesson I can use in the next show. The rule is in the design doc's Rules tab (the paragraph after "The Cut", and "Onboarding").
- **Steps:**
  1. **Ask me first: how the top reason is found.** The Rules tab says the lose screen "names the reason that got the most votes", for example "They caught you copying late." Two ways, and I'd expect the first:
     - Each vote names its tell: a new field in the reply shape (`llm.json` `reply_schema`), one of a short list such as `note`, `late_copy`, `silence`, `polish`, `human_slip`, `no_persona`, `other`. The lose screen counts the tells of the votes that cut me and shows the top one's line from a data file. It changes the one reply shape for all four providers, so it needs one paid check run, with my OK.
     - No new field: match keywords in the reasons. No cost, but it can name the wrong reason.
  2. **The lose screen:** each AI's reasoning about me, as now, then the top reason in one large line, then Restart. Restart must start a new show in one click, as T10 built it.
  3. **The win screen:** each AI's reasoning, then the rivals revealed: each bot's rival by the name I see ("OpenAI's rival was Anthropic"), read from `rival` in `personas.json` (T56). Show the pairs for the bots in this show only. The subtitle keeps T54's two kinds of win.
  4. **Build on T33's layout** of `WBP_EndScreen` and `WBP_EndCard`, in T32's style. Change `ShowEndScreen` and `StoreClosing` in `BP_Show`; read only those graphs and what they call.
  5. **Check:** a free autoplay show that I lose shows the reasoning and one top reason; a show with `spare_human` on shows the reasoning and the rivals. Screenshots of both. Print `NHOS: TOP REASON <tell> votes=<n>` on a loss and `NHOS: RIVALS <id>=<id> ...` on a win. Restart from each screen reaches a second `NHOS: SHOW END`.
- **Done when:** the check passes, and Claude plays by hand (with `spare_human` and `force_tie` as needed) until it has seen one win and one loss, and checks that the top reason matches what the AIs said.
