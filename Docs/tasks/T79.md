Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task's title line, **Model and effort**, **Depends on**, **Needs**, **Touches** and any **Waiting:** or **Blocked:** line are in its entry there, under "Open".

## Task

- **Why:** on 2026-11-15 I chose a new cast to replace T46's clay pill bots: four polished bots and me in a cardboard costume. [Docs/BotArt.md](../BotArt.md) has the concept images, what each bot wears and the rules the build keeps. This task builds the round head that three of the bots share, the sockets that props and headpieces attach to, and those three bots: OpenAI, Anthropic and SpaceXAI. T80 builds Meta's android and my costume. T75 then poses every face for the emotions, and T47 puts the headpieces on.
- **Steps:**
  1. **The design doc.** Show me the edit to the "Art Inspiration" tab's section on the bots: the new cast, from `Docs/BotArt.md`, with no real person's name. After my OK, make it with the Claude Docs connector. If the tab has the pill bots' pictures, T74 replaces them later; leave them.
  2. **The shared round head** in `BP_Robot`: about 70 cm across, like T46's dome, so T43's headpieces keep the size T47 gives them. A dark screen face with T46's LED eyes and mouth (the mouth still stretches while a bot talks), two small cream ear pods and a short gold collar to the body. Keep the screen in the same place on all three, because T47's shades and T75's faces sit on it.
  3. **Sockets, not fixed heights.** Give every robot a `HeadSocket` (the top of its head), a `HandSocket` (the right mitten) and a `BackSocket`. `Receive` attaches a prop to the socket for its slot instead of T46's fixed heights (the hand at the arm pivot minus 44 cm, the head at 160 cm, the back at 106 cm). Meta and my robot get theirs in T80; until then they use the old heights.
  4. **The three bodies,** picked in `SetPersona` by persona id, each about 1.6 m tall to the top of the head, so the labels (228 cm), the prop slots and T38's bubbles keep their places. The seat color is on the body, because a wig or hood hides most of the head:
     - **`visionary` (OpenAI), [openai.png](../BotArt/openai.png):** a pale blue teardrop body that hovers a little over a glowing ring on the floor (the idle bob becomes a slow hover), a gray knit sweater over the top half (a clay collar band and sleeves), hands pressed together at rest, the halo floating above the head.
     - **`worrier` (Anthropic), [anthropic.png](../BotArt/anthropic.png):** a round orange body with a cream belly and a few buttons, small round feet, perfectly circular thin gold glasses over the screen, and the amber warning light on a thin antenna.
     - **`overclocker` (SpaceXAI), [spacexai.png](../BotArt/spacexai.png):** a round lime body with a cream belly, coiled spring arms and legs, chunky boots, a long straight nose sculpted under the visor, a toothy grin and the lightning bolt.
     - `copycat` (Meta) and my robot keep T46's body until T80.
  5. **Parts.** Engine shapes and `M_Clay` instances first, smooth like the concept art (lower `ThumbDepth` than T46 if it reads rough). A part engine shapes can't make well (the circle glasses, the springs, the nose) may be a Meshy model made with `Tools/art_gen.py`, recorded in `Docs/Assets.md`. No hair and no wires (my call, 2026-11-15). No brand marks anywhere.
  6. **Keep every motion:** the idle bob (a hover for OpenAI), the talking bounce, the hops, grab, throw, give, the shake, the cheer and the cut slump. On the cut the seat color goes gray, as before. A grabbed prop still sits in the hand, now through the socket.
  7. **Re-place** the five robots in `L_Stage` if template changes don't reach them (the T17 and T46 rule), with their seat, fall direction and preview persona.
  8. **Check:** PIE screenshots from the house camera in Scene Prep and in a scene turn; a close-up of each new bot beside its concept image; each of the three holding a prop; a free autoplay show in a game window reaches `NHOS: SHOW END` with no `LogScript` line.
- **Done when:** the check passes, and Claude judges from the screenshots, as a critical first-time player would, that the three read as their concept images at the house camera's size, and that they are told apart by body and color, not only by the topper.
