## Findings

### What was built

- **`WBP_Stage`'s tree:** `PrepSize` (SizeBox, a variable, `WidthOverride` 660, `MaxDesiredHeight` 520, `Collapsed` at design time) > `PrepPanel` (Border, a variable, padding 24 / 18) > `PrepPanelBox` (VerticalBox), third in `RootBox` (after `TopLeftStack` and `LowerSize`, so the card, `AtPop` and `FacePop` draw over it). `SceneTitle`, `SceneText`, `SetTitle`, `PlacesText`, `PropsText` and `StartButton` moved into it with `UMGToolSet.MoveWidget`, which keeps each widget object, its variable and `StartButton`'s bound `OnClicked`: the click chain (`Graphs/WBP_Stage/EventGraph.StartButton.txt`) wasn't rewritten. `StartButton`'s slot is `HAlign_Center`, top 14, its old left padding of 10 gone. `SceneTitle` and `SetTitle` were not variables; they are now (`PrepInit` styles them). `PrepBox` on the card keeps only `CastTitle` and `CastBox`.
- **Where it sits:** slot `HAlign_Center`, `VAlign_Bottom`, padding left 110 and bottom 24. An Overlay slot's left padding moves a centered child right by the full padding (measured: 220 moved it 220), so the panel is centered on `width/2 + 110`, which is the middle of the gap between the 640 chat column (its right edge at 664, plus 24) and the card (its left edge at `width − 444`, less 24) at any 16:9 width. Measured gaps at 1600 × 900: about 50 px to the 640 chat column and 50 px to the card; at 1280 × 720, about 35 px each. One spot in every scene, as the sheet's decision 2 says.
- **`PrepInit`** (new, `Graphs/WBP_Stage/PrepInit.txt`; not in the Touches line because it is new): once (`PrepReady`), `StylePanel "Ink"` on `PrepPanel`, the scene's title in `CardTitle` and old gold, its text in `Body`, the stage's title in `Name` and old gold, the places and props in `Caption`, both texts in `UIColor "Text"`. Nothing else sets these fonts at run time (the card's texts keep their design-time fonts), so once is enough.
- **`SetPrepMode`** (`Graphs/WBP_Stage/SetPrepMode.txt`): the sheet's part 3 draft, with two changes. The scene number is bound inside the prep branch (`Class|BPShow|GetSceneNum` on `ShowRef`, no cast needed). `LowerSize` is given back `Visible`, its design-time value (the tree dump lists no visibility for it), not the sheet's guess `SelfHitTestInvisible`. No other graph in `WBP_Stage` or `BP_Show` sets `LowerSize`'s visibility (a `find_nodes` sweep of every graph of both).
- **New variables:** `LowerPrep` (float, 640), `PrepReady` (bool).
- **`PrepPrompt`** (`Graphs/BP_Show/PrepPrompt.txt`): the turn line is "Read the scene in the middle, then your role and the note's clue on the right. Press Start the scene when you are ready."; the hint is "Read the scene first." (the sheet's decision 5).

### The Scene Prep screen, as a critical first-time player

- **The reading order works.** The eye goes to the gold "THE SCENE" in the lower middle, reads the premise, then the stage, then lands on the gold Start the scene right under it. The turn line in the header says the same. My part and the clue are on the right, where they stay for the whole scene, so the move from prep to play doesn't relocate them. Start the scene is now next to what I read instead of in the bottom-left corner.
- **Scene 1 looks open, not empty.** With no lower panel, the bottom left shows the audience and the floor; nothing reads as missing, because the chat has nothing in it yet. In scenes 2 on, the slim 640 row (Show chat and the greyed "Read the scene first.") sits beside the panel and reads as the same input row, just quiet.
- **Crowded: the card.** My role, my want, the clue and five cast entries make the card long; with a long clue (Pig Latin, four lines) the cast starts below the fold. T85's hover cards fix this.
- **The panel sits right of the stage's center** (about 90 px at 1600 × 900), because it stays in the gap between the chat column and the card in every scene. In scene 1, with no chat column, a player may notice it isn't centered under the robots. It reads fine, and centering it only in scene 1 would make it jump between scenes.
- **Two small things for T34:** the scene line reads "Scene 1: What's in the Glowing Briefcase?." (the title's own "?" plus the "." that `ShowPrep` adds); and the header box shrinks with a short turn line and doesn't grow back, so scene 2's Scene Prep shows the turn line in five narrow lines (T32's row 4, "header width jumps", which T34-build 2.3 fixes with a fixed width).
