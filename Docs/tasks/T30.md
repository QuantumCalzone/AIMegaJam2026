# T30 The lines play one at a time

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task is done.

## Task

- **Model and effort:** Opus 5.5, high. Timing across the show loop, the chat and the stage.
- **Depends on:** T19 (the stage acts out each line), T29 (only actor lines and the banner are left to pace)
- **Needs:** editor + Unreal MCP running, Test for Free for the PIE shows.
- **Touches:** new `Content/NHOS/Core/BPC_LinePlayer.uasset`, `Content/NHOS/Core/BP_Show.uasset` (`ShowLine`, `ActorLine`, the waits), `Content/NHOS/UI/WBP_Stage.uasset` (the typing line), a data file in `Content/NHOS/Data/`, `Docs/switches.example.json`, `Graphs/` files for the graphs it writes, the design doc.
- **Why:** my playtest on 2026-11-14. The AIs' lines all happen at once, and that is hard to follow. A scene turn drops five lines into the chat in one frame while five robots act at the same time.
- **Steps:**
  1. **A line queue in its own component** (`BPC_LinePlayer` on `BP_Show`, as the build notes say). `ShowLine` adds each line to the queue instead of putting it on screen. The queue plays one line at a time: the chat line appears, its robot lights up and bounces (`SetLit`), and its stage actions play. Move the `BP_StageDirector.PlayLine` call from `ActorLine` to this moment, so the stage and the chat match. Then the queue waits a time that grows with the line's length (for example 1.2 s plus 0.25 s per word, about 2 to 5 s) and plays the next line. Print `NHOS: LINE <seat id>` as each line plays.
  2. **Free Talk and reactions feel like a chat:** before each AI line, "<name> is typing..." shows for a short random time. This is T24's typing delay, moved here.
  3. **The show waits for the queue.** The asks for the next step may go out while lines play, which hides the wait for the LLMs. Anything that changes the screen next (my turn, a new phase in the header, the vote, a banner, the end screen) waits until the queue is empty.
  4. **The values live in a data file** (a `pacing` block in `Content/NHOS/Data/`). A `fast_lines` switch in `Local/switches.json` cuts every wait to a tenth for autoplay checks; add it to `Docs/switches.example.json`.
  5. The shared log the AIs read doesn't change: it still gets each turn's lines at once, in random order. `ShowLine` stays the one place a line reaches my screen, so T20's talk blip can hook in there.
  6. Propose the matching edit to the design doc's Rules tab (The Scene & acting: the turns play out one at a time, in random order), as the build notes say.
- **Done when:** a free autoplay show with `fast_lines` reaches `NHOS: SHOW END`; a free autoplay show without it plays a scene turn's lines at least 1 s apart (the times on its `NHOS: LINE` lines); a screenshot shows one robot acting while its line is the newest in the chat; Claude plays one show by hand and checks from screenshots that the lines are easy to follow.
- **Result:** done 2026-11-15 (`172fb76`, closed after my answer on the doc edit). Every line on my screen plays one at a time through `BPC_LinePlayer`, "<name> is typing..." comes before AI lines in Free Talk and the reactions, and the header, banners, vote row, cut, Scene Prep, end screen and my turn wait for the lines in front of them. I said not to edit the design doc, so the Rules tab stays as it is. See "T30: the line queue" in Findings.

### T30: the line queue (2026-11-15)

Built on the Windows PC through Unreal MCP, from `Docs/tasks/T30-build.md`, on top of T29, T32 and T56 as pushed.

- **How it works now.** `BP_Show` runs the show's logic as before: it sends the asks at once, and the shared log still gets each turn's lines at once, in random order. Only what reaches my screen goes through the new component `BPC_LinePlayer` (`/Game/NHOS/Core/BPC_LinePlayer`, the `LinePlayer` component on `BP_Show`), one item at a time:
  - **A line:** `ShowLine` (still the one place a line reaches my screen) calls `LinePlayer.Push`. When its turn comes, the queue shows "<name> is typing..." first if it is an AI line in Free Talk or the reactions (`LineInfo`), then `PlayCur` adds the chat line, lights the robot (`LightSeat`) and plays its stage actions (`StageLine`, the director call that moved out of `ActorLine`). It prints `NHOS: LINE <seat id> hold=<s>` and holds for `line_base + line_per_word × words`, kept between `line_min` and `line_max`.
  - **A cue:** a screen change that waits for the lines in front of it. `RunCue` runs it and the queue prints `NHOS: CUE <kind> <text>`. Kinds: `phase` (the header), `banner` (T29's banner), `votes` (`show` runs `ShowVotePick`, empty hides the row), `cut` (all of `CutActor`, so the robot falls and the stamp shows after the vote lines), `prep` (all of `ScenePrep`) and `end` (all of `ShowEndScreen`). A banner holds the queue 2 s and a cut 3 s (`cue_hold`), so the next line doesn't start under them.
  - Waits use `SetTimerbyFunctionName` on the component, never under 0.01 s. The queue plays an item at once when it is idle, so a path with nothing in front of it works as before.
- **My turn waits for the queue.** `UpdatePrompt` calls the new `LinePrompt` after `PrepPrompt`: while lines play, the turn line says "Watch the stage." (or "Watch the stage. Your turn comes next." when the logic has already reached my turn), the hint says "Wait for the lines to finish.", and the input, Send and Pass stay disabled. The first build only changed the turn line on my turn; the first autoplay screenshot then showed "Counting the votes..." under the turn 1 header, because the logic runs ahead in free mode, so now it changes whenever lines play. `WBP_Stage` has a new `PassOn`: `SetPassEnabled` stores it, and Pass is enabled only when `PassOn` and the input are both on (`SetPrompt` applies it again when the input turns on).
- **Swapped in `BP_Show`** (node swaps; these graphs have no sources): the header in `StartTurn`, `StartTalkRound`, `EndFreeTalk`, `StartCut`, `StartReactions`, `EndShow` → `CueHeader`; the banners in `EndScene`, `Tally` (2), `DirectorCut` (2), `EndReactions`, `EndShow` (3) → `CueBanner`; `StartCut`'s two `ShowVotePick` calls → `CueVotes("show")`; `Tally`'s row hide → `CueVotes("")`; `CutActor` in `Tally` and `DirectorCut` (2) → `CueCut`; `EndReactions`' `ScenePrep` → `CuePrep`; `StoreClosing`'s `ShowEndScreen` → `CueEnd`. Left immediate on purpose: `ShowTitle`, `SetUnavailable` and `HandleSend`'s error banners, the stamp and vote-row clears in `StartScene`, `StartCut` and `RestartShow`, and the calls inside the deferred functions (`ShowPrep`, `CutActor`, `ShowEndScreen`), which run at their cue's time anyway. `RestartShow` doesn't clear the queue: it runs only after the `end` cue, when the queue is empty.
- **New sources:** `Graphs/BPC_LinePlayer/` (every graph), `Graphs/BP_Show/` (`StageLine`, `LineInfo`, `RunCue`, the six `Cue*`, `LinePrompt`, `ActorLine`, and `ShowLine`, now a full source), `Graphs/WBP_Stage/` (`SetTyping`, `SetPassEnabled`, `SetPrompt`).
