## Findings

- **Cost:** `lucid-origin` at 1024 × 1024 costs 17 credits for two images (8.5 an image, half of T94's 2 MP rate). A `gemini-2.5-flash-image` edit costs 26 an image. The replies' `apiCreditCost` stays empty; measure the balance on `GET /v1/me` around each call, and expect odd numbers when other sessions spend from the same balance in the same minute.
- **The shared balance runs out.** T95, T97 and T98 started together on 1,202 credits; it fell to 54 after T97's card 4, and my person topped it up. Concept tasks that run at once should agree a split, or check the balance before each card.
- **Draw the candidate as the model view.** With the style guide's reference ending on every prompt, the picked candidate is already a clean view for image-to-3D, so step 4 cost nothing for 25 of 28 props. Only the props with glow (the briefcase) or a fix (the watch, the spoon) needed another image.
- **Edits can't change a shape or a hinge.** Four briefcase edits (flatter, half as deep, lid slightly open, hinged at the back) all failed: the case stayed deep, then the lid floated and the handle doubled. A text redraw that describes the shape in plain words (round 5, round 8) got it at once. Edits did well at removing things (the watch's numbers, the briefcase's beam).
- **Bends are hard for the image model.** Five spoon takes from text came out straight or as a soft ladle. A short Swift script (`Docs/Art/Props/bend_spoon.swift`) bent a straight spoon cleanly on the plain background: cut at the narrowest point of the neck, rotate about its centre, repaint the background per row from the pixels beside the handle (the background isn't even from side to side), and round the joint with a disc shaded from the neck's own row.
- **My person shows a reference when words fail.** Both references (a stock photo of an attaché case, a "there is no spoon" sticker) were described in the prompt, never uploaded: one carried a watermark and both are someone else's art.
- **What the image model ignored:** "no numbers" on the watch, "not glowing" on the staff's crystal (it reads as an egg, near card 3's dino egg; my person kept it), "roasted brown" on the drumstick (red, kept), "two shades of red/blue" on the pills (half cream, which reads better), "matte" on the tin can (shiny metal; T105 makes it matte clay).
- **Palette after T95:** Meta's seat color is now mustard (T95, 2026-11-18; the hex comes in T108). The rolled-up rug's diamonds are mustard; T105 darkens them once T108 sets the color. The oranges are deep tangerine, darker than Anthropic's orange.
- **Things for T105** are in the table in `Docs/Art/Props/README.md`: which parts glow (the glow-stick blade, the staff's crystal, the briefcase's lining and its beam), the phone's cord left off, the envelope's seal left plain, the briefcase's lid as its own part.
- Leonardo still serves JPEG data under a `.png` name; `gen.py` converts every take with `sips` (T94's lesson).
