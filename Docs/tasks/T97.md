## Task

- **Why:** my request on 2026-11-15: all the art goes back to concept art and waits for my approval before anything is generated. This task draws every story prop in T94's direction. Each of the seven cards brings four props to the prop table, so there are 28 in all (T44, T45). T105 makes the models.
- **What the props must allow:**
  - **A robot can hold it** in its `HandSocket`, throw it and give it (T62). It reads at the house camera's distance, on the prop table and in a robot's hand.
  - **It is what the card says.** Each prop's name and look come from `Content/NHOS/Data/props.json` and its card in `scenes.json`. The wants, the notes and the clue (T88) name these props, so a prop can't change what it is. Only its look changes.
  - **No real brand,** logo or product on any prop.
- **Steps:**
  1. **Read first:** T94's style guide, `Content/NHOS/Data/props.json`, the cards in `scenes.json`, `Docs/PropArt.md` (how the old props were made, and their sizes in `Docs/PropArt/manifest.json`), and the design doc's "Props and backdrops" section.
  2. **One card at a time.** For each card, make two or three candidates for each of its four props with `art_gen.py image`, and show me the card's props together, so they read as a set. Before the first paid call, tell me the estimated cost of this task's generations and ask once. Ask again only if the cost would go over that.
  3. **My picks.** Ask one question at a time. Change a picked prop with `art_gen.py edit` (my memory note `edit-picked-images`).
  4. **Views for the model.** For each approved prop, make one clean view on a plain background, at the three-quarter angle that image-to-3D handles best, with nothing else in the frame. Write each prop's real size in centimeters next to it, because T105 scales the model from it.
  5. **Record it.** Keep the candidates, the picks and the model views in `Docs/Art/Props/<card id>/<prop id>/`. Write each round, my words, the prompts, the generation ids and the cost in `Docs/Art/Props/README.md`.
  6. **The design doc.** Show me the edit to the props part of the "Props and backdrops" section of the Art Inspiration tab, and to the props strip under each story's "Props:" line on the Stories tab. Make it after my OK, in its files in `Docs/Design/`.
- **Done when:**
  1. I approved all 28 props, and each card's four as a set.
  2. Each approved prop has a clean model view and its size, in `Docs/Art/Props/`.
  3. `Docs/Art/Props/README.md` logs every round and its cost.
  4. The design doc's props pictures show the approved props, after my OK.
