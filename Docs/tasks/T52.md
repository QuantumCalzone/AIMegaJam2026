### What changed in the game

- **`BP_Show.TieCut`** (new, with `Graphs/BP_Show/TieCut.txt`): builds the tie message from `Tally`'s own `TiedSeats`, `TiedNames` and `MaxVotes` (the sheet's version counted the votes again; `Tally` already has them), sets `TieMsg`, adds it to `GameMsgs`, sets `CutSeat` to -1, calls `RecordVotes` (the end screen's vote history), cues the banner, prints `NHOS: TIE <message> tied=<ids> cut=none` and queues the `"tie"` cue. New String-array variable `TieIds`. The sheet's `TieTop`, `TieText` and `TieNames` were not needed.
- **`BP_Show.Tally`** (node edit, no file): the `Branch` on "exactly one top seat" keeps its `then` (`CueCut`); its `else` now runs one `TieCut` call. Deleted: the old tie branch's `Set Tiebreak`, `JoinNames`, `Set TieMsg`, the `GameMsgs` add, the `DisplayText` and `CueBanner` (" The Director breaks the tie."), the `TIE` print, `Set DirTries` and the `AskDirector` call, and the pure nodes that fed only them. `Tally`'s count still fills `TiedSeats` and `TiedNames`, and `TieCut` reads them.
- **`BP_Show.RunCue`** (`Graphs/BP_Show/RunCue.txt`): the `"tie"` cue, as the sheet gives it. `StopDrum` and `Sfx` resolved from `BP_Show`.
- **`BP_Show.StartReactions`** (node edits, no file), two places that read `CutSeat`:
  - "The human is caught" tested `SeatHuman[CutSeat]`; it now tests `CutSeat == HumanSeat` (an `Equal (==)` node; the `SeatHuman` get was deleted). Same meaning, and seat -1 no longer reads out of range.
  - A new `Branch` on `CutSeat >= 0` sits between `Shuffle` and the `Add CutSeat to ReactOrder`; its `else` goes straight to the loop that adds the other AIs. After a tie, `ReactOrder` is the AIs in random order, then me.
- **`BP_Show.EndReactions`** (node edit, no file): the "my seat is cut, so I lose" test was `SeatHuman[CutSeat]` too, and the sheet missed it; it is now `CutSeat == HumanSeat`. Without these three edits a tie would log `Attempted to access index -1` warnings (the result was already right, since an out-of-range get returns false).
- **Data and docs:** `prompts.json` (The Cut's line, the doc's tie line word for word under it, `asks.reaction`, a tie example in `_readme`), `Tools/check_prompts.py` (decision 10, and a docstring paragraph), `Docs/switches.example.json` (`force_tie` now gives a scene with no cut), `Docs/ItchPage.md` (one sentence: "When the vote ties, nobody is cut, but the scene still counts.").

### Unreal MCP rules found in this task

- **A banner's old text stays in the Slate tree after it fades,** so `SlateInspectorToolset.WaitFor` on a banner's text can be true long after it left the screen. To catch a banner, read its `NHOS: BANNER` time in the log and take a burst of `Screenshot`s (every ~1 s, named with the UTC time). The frame that showed a banner came about 2 s after its log line here.
- **`AssetTools.write_file` takes only `.csv`, `.html`, `.json`, `.md`, `.py` or `.txt`.** Write a `Screenshot`'s base64 to a `.txt` and decode it outside the editor.
- **`ObjectTools.get_properties` takes `instance` and `properties`; `set_properties` takes `instance` and `values` (a JSON string).** `AssetTools.save_assets` takes `asset_paths` (package paths).
- **The comparison operators to create** are `Utilities|Operators|Equal(==)`, `GreaterEqual(>=)` and the like. They are wildcards that take the type of the first pin you connect; `set_pin_value` on `B` then sets a literal.
- **`Tools/dslpp.py` needs a `;;; GRAPH <Name>` line** before the source, or it writes an empty file.
