# T102 New audio, part 3: import the picks and set a quieter mix

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task's title line, **Model and effort**, **Depends on**, **Needs**, **Touches** and any **Waiting:** or **Blocked:** line are in its entry there, under "Open".

## Task

- **Why:** my request on 2026-11-15: the new music must match the scenes, be quieter, and sit more in the background. T100 and T101 got my picks. This task puts them in the game and sets a mix in which the music sits under the talk.
- **Claude can't hear.** Claude sets the mix by measured loudness, then records a short piece of each phase from the game and sends it to me. I approve the mix by listening.
- **Steps:**
  1. **Read first:** T100's and T101's picks in `Docs/AudioShortlist.md`; how `BPC_ShowAudio` plays the music (`TrackFor`, `PhaseKey`, `PlayPhase`, `DuckMusic`, `EndMusic`: `Docs/tasks/T71.md`), the show sounds (`Docs/tasks/T20.md`) and the syllables (`Syllable`: `Docs/tasks/T72.md`, `T73.md`); and the sound classes `SC_Music`, `SC_Effects` and `SC_Voice`.
  2. **Even loudness.** Measure every pick's loudness with a short script (or `ffmpeg`'s `loudnorm`, if it's on the machine). Normalize all the music loops to one target. Normalize each group of sounds to its own target. Write the targets down. As a start, put the music about 10 dB under the voices, and the effects between the two.
  3. **Import in place.** Reimport each pick over the asset it replaces, under the same name (`Content/NHOS/Audio/...`), so every reference stays. Check that each music loop is set to loop and keeps its sound class. Delete any old asset that T101 left out, `S_TalkBlip` included if nothing plays it.
  4. **The mix.** Set the volumes on `SC_Music`, `SC_Effects` and `SC_Voice` (and on `BPC_ShowAudio`'s own levels, if it has them) so the talk is always on top. Keep T71's duck under the drum roll and its 1 s cross-fade, and duck the music a little more while a line types out if it still competes. If I chose a theater ambience bed in T100, play it quietly under everything from the title on, from `BPC_ShowAudio`. Keep `BP_Show` out of it if you can. If it can't be done without `BP_Show`, the task moves to the chain machine: tell me first.
  5. **Record the game for me.** In a free autoplay show in a game window, record the game's own audio output with the audio mixer's recording: the `StartRecordingOutput` and `FinishRecordingOutput` nodes, or the `AudioMixer` console commands. Record about 30 s each of Scene Prep, a scene turn with lines typing out, Free Talk, The Cut with its drum roll and reveal, and the end screen. Send me the recordings, or put them on a private Artifact page, and ask me for my OK on the mix. Change the levels until I approve them.
  6. **Record it.** In `Docs/Assets.md`, replace the audio rows with the new ones: what each is, the source, the license and the credit line. Put the credit lines in `Docs/ItchPage.md`, and in the game's credits if it shows any. Write the loudness targets and the final levels in `Docs/AudioShortlist.md`.
- **Done when:**
  1. Every pick from T100 and T101 plays in the game under its old asset name, and my own voice clips are unchanged.
  2. All the music loops share one loudness target, and the levels put the music under the talk.
  3. I approved the mix from the recordings of each phase.
  4. A free autoplay show in a game window reaches `NHOS: SHOW END`, and the log has no audio errors.
  5. `Docs/Assets.md`, `Docs/AudioShortlist.md` and `Docs/ItchPage.md` have the new sources, licenses and credits.
