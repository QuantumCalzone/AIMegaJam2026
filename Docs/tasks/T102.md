# T102 New audio, part 3: import the picks and set a quieter mix

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task's title line, **Model and effort**, **Depends on**, **Needs**, **Touches** and any **Waiting:** or **Blocked:** line are in its entry there, under "Open".

## Task

- **Why:** my request on 2026-11-15: the new music must match the scenes, be quieter, and sit more in the background. T100 and T101 got my picks. This task puts them in the game and sets a mix in which the music sits under the talk. T100 picked 16 music loops: one for each of T71's 13 slots, and one each for three phases that had no track of their own, Scene Prep, Last Word and Reactions. There's no ambience bed.
- **Claude can't hear.** Claude sets the mix by measured loudness, then records a short piece of each phase from the game and sends it to me. I approve the mix by listening.
- **Steps:**
  1. **Read first:** T100's and T101's picks in `Docs/AudioShortlist.md`; T100's findings under "For T102" in `Docs/tasks/T100.md` (the files, their loudness and the seams to listen to first); how `BPC_ShowAudio` plays the music (`TrackFor`, `PhaseKey`, `PlayPhase`, `DuckMusic`, `EndMusic`: `Docs/tasks/T71.md`), the show sounds (`Docs/tasks/T20.md`) and the syllables (`Syllable`: `Docs/tasks/T72.md`, `T73.md`); and the sound classes `SC_Music`, `SC_Effects` and `SC_Voice`.
  2. **Even loudness.** Measure every pick's loudness with a short script (or `ffmpeg`'s `loudnorm`, if it's on the machine). Normalize all the music loops to one target. Normalize each group of sounds to its own target. Write the targets down. As a start, put the music about 10 dB under the voices, and the effects between the two.
  3. **Import in place.** Reimport each pick over the asset it replaces, under the same name (`Content/NHOS/Audio/...`), so every reference stays. T100's picks are `SourceAssets/Audio/music/t100/<NN>_<key>.flac`. Thirteen of them replace the loops in `Content/NHOS/Audio/Music/`:
     - `title` replaces `S_MusicTitle`, and `show` replaces `S_MusicShow`.
     - Each card id replaces its card's loop: `space_plans` replaces `S_MusicSpacePlans`, and so on for the seven cards.
     - `free_talk`, `cut`, `win` and `lose` replace `S_MusicFreeTalk`, `S_MusicCut`, `S_MusicWin` and `S_MusicLose`.
     - The other three are new assets beside them: `S_MusicScenePrep`, `S_MusicLastWord` and `S_MusicReactions`.

     If the importer refuses FLAC, make WAVs first; T100's findings have the command. Check that each music loop, the new ones too, is set to loop and has the sound class `SC_Music`. Delete any old asset that T101 left out, `S_TalkBlip` included if nothing plays it.
  4. **The three new music keys.** Each phase prints its own `NHOS: SOUND music <key>`, and the swap is T71's 1 s cross-fade.
     - **`BPC_ShowAudio.PhaseKey`:** `LAST WORD` gives `last_word`, and `REACTIONS` gives `reactions`. `SCENE PREP` gives `scene_prep`, though no `phase` cue carries that header today (T71). `THE SCENE` still gives `card`.
     - **`BPC_ShowAudio.TrackFor`:** the three keys return the three new assets, so the cook packs them.
     - **`BP_Show.OpenPrep`, one pin:** its `PlayMusic` asks for `"scene_prep"` in place of `CardId`. The card's loop then starts at the first `THE SCENE` cue, through `PlayPhase`, which already handles it. My call on 2026-11-17: this pin is the only change to `BP_Show`. Lock `BP_Show.uasset` first, and change the pin and the comment in `Graphs/BP_Show/OpenPrep.txt` to match.
     - **Graph files:** `PhaseKey` and `TrackFor` have no splices, so you can write them again from their files in `Graphs/BPC_ShowAudio/`. `SwapMusic`, `StopMusic` and `DuckMusic` do have splices. Leave those three alone, or redo their splices (T71).
     - **Check in the log** that each phase gets its key: `scene_prep` at Scene Prep, the card id at The Scene, then `free_talk`, `last_word`, `cut` and `reactions`.
       - The Last Word comes only when a line in the final Free Talk round names someone with @. Otherwise Free Talk goes straight to The Cut, and `last_word` never plays.
       - The `REACTIONS` cue plays after `Reveal`, which stops the music so that nothing plays under the stinger (T71). If the reactions loop starts under the stinger or the crowd, start it later, from `BPC_ShowAudio`.
       - A tie stops the music in `RunCue`, and `REACTIONS` then starts the reactions loop.
       - At the end, `EndCheer` stops the music first, so `EndMusic` still plays the win or lose loop 3 s later.
  5. **The mix.** Set the volumes on `SC_Music`, `SC_Effects` and `SC_Voice` (and on `BPC_ShowAudio`'s own levels, if it has them) so the talk is always on top. Keep T71's duck under the drum roll and its 1 s cross-fade, and duck the music a little more while a line types out if it still competes. Apart from `OpenPrep`'s pin (step 4), keep `BP_Show` out of it. If the mix can't be done without another change to `BP_Show`, tell me first.
  6. **Record the game for me.** In a free autoplay show in a game window, record the game's own audio output with the audio mixer's recording: the `StartRecordingOutput` and `FinishRecordingOutput` nodes, or the `AudioMixer` console commands. Record about 30 s each of Scene Prep, a scene turn with lines typing out, Free Talk, the Last Word if a show has one, The Cut with its drum roll and reveal, Reactions, and the end screen. Send me the recordings, or put them on a private Artifact page, and ask me for my OK on the mix. Change the levels until I approve them.
  7. **Record it.** In `Docs/Assets.md`, replace the audio rows with the new ones: what each is, the source, the license and the credit line. Put the credit lines in `Docs/ItchPage.md`, and in the game's credits if it shows any. Write the loudness targets and the final levels in `Docs/AudioShortlist.md`.
- **Done when:**
  1. Every pick from T100 and T101 plays in the game under its old asset name, and my own voice clips are unchanged. T100's three new loops play at Scene Prep, Last Word and Reactions, and each card's loop starts at The Scene.
  2. All the music loops share one loudness target, and the levels put the music under the talk.
  3. I approved the mix from the recordings of each phase.
  4. A free autoplay show in a game window reaches `NHOS: SHOW END`, and the log has no audio errors.
  5. `Docs/Assets.md`, `Docs/AudioShortlist.md` and `Docs/ItchPage.md` have the new sources, licenses and credits.
