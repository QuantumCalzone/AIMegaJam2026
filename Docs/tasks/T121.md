- **The rules.** The rules for a costume are T96's, in `Docs/tasks/T96.md`:
  - It is at least a headpiece. A part off the head, such as a collar, needs my OK, and T122 gives it an attach point.
  - It never hides the face, the mouth, the seat color or the name tag.
  - It is a homemade parody, never a film's real costume.

  Two rules are new. A new costume belongs to its own story: no role on another card wears it. Two cards share a costume only if it fits both stories and I say so. A role's color may change with its costume, if I approve.
- **Steps:**
  1. **Read first:** `Docs/Art/Costumes/README.md` (how T96 made the costumes, its lessons and the role table), `Docs/Art/Direction/README.md`, `Docs/Art/Cast/README.md`, "## Findings" in `Docs/tasks/T96.md`, and the 8 seats in `scenes.json`.
  2. **An idea for each role.** For each of the 8 roles, give me two or three costume ideas that fit its story and its role, in one question per role, your pick first.
  3. **Candidates.** For each picked idea, make two candidates the way T96 did (`Docs/Art/Costumes/gen.py cand`, with Leonardo). Before the first paid call, tell me the estimated cost of this task's generations and ask once. Ask again only if the cost would go over that estimate. T96 used about 180 Leonardo credits per costume, fit sheets included.
  4. **My pick for each.** Show me the candidates and ask one question at a time. Change a picked costume with `art_gen.py edit` (my memory note `edit-picked-images`).
  5. **Fit it to every character.** Put each costume on all five characters with `fit.py` (`art_gen.py edit`, one call per character, as T96 learned). Give the cube its own version of a soft piece. A hat with a crown and brim may sit flat on the lid. Make one sheet per costume, and approve each with me.
  6. **Views for the model.** For each costume, make one clean view per version on plain light gray, as T96's `view_cube` folders are. Write down which characters share a version, because T122 makes one model per version.
  7. **Record it.** Keep everything in `Docs/Art/Costumes/<costume>/`. Add each round, my words, the prompts, the generation ids and the cost to `Docs/Art/Costumes/README.md`. Fill in the 8 "none yet" rows of its role table with the costume id and its color.
  8. **The design doc.** Show me the edit to the Costumes section of `Docs/Design/ArtInspiration.md` (the fit sheets, or one overview, and the table) and to the 8 Headpiece cells in `Docs/Design/Stories.md`. Make it after my OK.
- **Done when:**
  1. Each of the 8 roles has a costume that I approved, and no role on another card wears it.
  2. Each new costume has a fit sheet on all five characters that I approved, and its cube version is made for a cube.
  3. Each version that T122 needs has a clean view.
  4. `Docs/Art/Costumes/README.md` records the rounds, the cost and the 8 rows.
  5. The design doc shows the new costumes, after my OK.
