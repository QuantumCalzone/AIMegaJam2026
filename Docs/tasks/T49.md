# T49 The clay opera-house stage

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). Done on 2026-11-15; its entry there is cut down to its title line and this link.

## Task

- **Model and effort:** Opus 5.5, high. Set dressing and materials across the whole level, framed for the house camera.
- **Depends on:** T42 and T43 (the set pieces and `M_Clay`), T46 (the audience bots' body and the lock on `L_Stage`)
- **Needs:** editor + Unreal MCP running, Test for Free for the PIE shows.
- **Touches:** `Content/NHOS/Maps/L_Stage.umap`, `Content/NHOS/Stage/Materials/` (`M_Set` and its instances), `Content/NHOS/Stage/Set/`, `Docs/tasks/T49.md`.
- **Why:** the clay art direction's stage is a grand 19th-century opera house sculpted in clay (the design doc's "Art Inspiration" tab, The stage). Today's improv theater (T17) is glossy and plain.
- **Steps:**
  1. **Clay surfaces:** make the theater matte clay. Either give `M_Set` a faint thumbprint normal and raise its roughness, or move the set's instances onto `M_Clay`; keep each color.
  2. **Opera dressing,** with T42's models: cherubs on the gold proscenium, the chandelier over the house (seen at the top of the frame, never in front of a robot), and the horseshoe boxes down both sides at the frame's edges, holding small capsule bots in top hats (T46's body at a small scale, T40's top hat).
  3. **Keep the play area clear:** the places, the robots, the prop table, the Riser and the backdrop stay in view from the house camera, which doesn't move. Keep `StagePost`'s exposure, and check the lights once the materials change.
  4. **Check:** screenshots from the house camera in Scene Prep and in a scene turn, and one close up of the proscenium; a free autoplay show reaches `NHOS: SHOW END`.
- **Done when:** the check passes, and Claude checks from screenshots of one free show in a game window that the stage looks right.
- **Result:** done. The theater is matte clay, a gold chandelier hangs at the top of the frame, gold cherubs blow trumpets at the apron's corners, two tiers of horseshoe boxes sit at both edges and a row of stalls fills the bottom of the frame, all with small top-hat capsule bots. A free autoplay show in a game window reached `NHOS: SHOW END result=win scenes=3`, and the screenshots of Scene Prep, scene turns and a reaction after a cut show every robot, place, the prop table, the Riser and the backdrop in view.

## Findings

### What was built

- **Clay surfaces (step 1, the first option).** `M_Set` (it lives at `Content/NHOS/Stage/M_Set.uasset`, not in `Materials/`) gained a thumbprint normal into its Slab's `Normal`: two Noise nodes (copied from `M_Clay`'s settings) on `WorldPosition × ThumbScale`, times `ThumbDepth`, appended to 1 and normalized. `ThumbDepth` defaults to 0, so the props, toppers, backdrop vignettes and everything else on `M_Set`'s 45 old instances look exactly as before. World position, not `M_Clay`'s local position, because the set's cubes are scaled up to 40 times and local-space noise stretches with them.
- **New theater instances** in `Stage/Materials/`, each keeping its old instance's color: `MI_Theater_Gold` (metal 0.3, rough 0.6, thumbs 0.2 at scale 0.08), `MI_Theater_Velvet`, `MI_Theater_Black`, `MI_Theater_Wood` (planks kept), `MI_Theater_WoodDark`, `MI_Theater_Cloth`, `MI_Theater_Gunmetal`. The level's static mesh actors under `Stage/` swapped `MI_Gold`, `MI_Velvet`, `MI_Black`, `MI_Wood`, `MI_WoodDark`, `MI_Cloth` and `MI_Gunmetal` for them (53 slots: floor, walls, proscenium, curtains, valance, footlight hoods, truss and spot cans, the Riser and the prop table). The old instances are untouched, because the robots and props use them too.
- **Chandelier:** `SM_Chandelier` at (-500, 0, 411), scale 0.948, yaw 90, casting no shadow (it sits in `FrontWash`'s beam and would shade center stage). `M_Chandelier` now tints its texture gold (× (2.3, 1.9, 1.1), saturated), makes the brightest texels (the candles) glow warm, and sets metallic 0.35 and roughness 0.5: T42's bronze read as a dark tangle.
- **Cherubs:** `SM_Cherub` at scale 0.45 on gold cube plinths at the apron's front corners, (-243, ±495), turned 20° toward center. The proscenium's arch is above the house camera's frame (its header starts at z 580; the frame's top at the proscenium is about z 460), and the boxes cover the columns' feet, so the apron corners are the only proscenium spot that shows.
- **Boxes:** four `SM_TheaterBox` at scale 1.0, two tiers (centers at z 152 and 342) on each side at (-450, ±600), turned 25° from the side wall toward the house so their openings face the camera, on a dark-wood wall with a gold trim strip so the lower tier doesn't float.
- **Audience:** new `Stage/Set/BP_AudienceBot`, an actor of engine shapes copied from T46's `BP_Robot` (cream bottom, seat-color top, seam, dark screen, glowing eyes, feet) with T40's `SM_TopHat` in charcoal and two gold opera-glass cylinders (hidden by default). No graph; each placed copy sets its own top color and glasses on its components. Two per box at scale 0.4, facing out of the box, a third of them with opera glasses. New colors on `M_Clay`: `MI_Clay_AudRose`, `AudPlum`, `AudMustard`, `AudBrick`, `AudLilac` (none is a seat color); screen and eyes are `MI_Aud_Screen` and `MI_Aud_Eye` on `M_Robot`.
- **Stalls (not in the steps):** the bottom third of the frame was bare black floor, so one row of 13 crimson clay seat backs runs across the house at x -470 with a gold rail, and seven audience bots sit in it, only their seat-color domes and top hats above the backs. It sits below the stage's front edge on screen, so it hides nothing on stage.
- Everything new is in the outliner under `Stage/Theater/Opera/` (`Boxes`, `Cherubs`, `Audience`, `Stalls`). No collision on any of it. `StagePost` and every light are unchanged.
- No new outside asset: the models are T40's and T42's, already in `Docs/Assets.md`.

### Unreal MCP and editor rules found in this task

- **A Slate inspector click in the PIE window lands after `Windows` `select` brings that window to the front.** The first `Click` on Test for Free did nothing; after `select` index 1 it worked. This may be why T46 found clicks never land.
- **Keystrokes can't reach a game window:** `osascript` System Events is refused ("not allowed to send keystrokes"), so a game window can't leave the title screen without autoplay.
- **`CaptureViewport` shows less height than the game.** The camera keeps its 90° horizontal FOV, and the editor viewport here is 2.4:1, so a capture misses about 7° at the top and the bottom of the 16:9 game frame. Pitching the capture up 6.9° (to -13.1) puts the capture's top edge on the game's top edge. `AutomationLibrary.take_high_res_screenshot` with a camera made nothing in the editor.
- **Editor Python through the console box** (T46's way: `SlateInspector.Type` of `py "<file>.py"` into the `Cmd` textbox) did all the asset work: `MaterialEditingLibrary.create_material_expression` and `connect_material_expressions` (the Noise input is `World Position`, the Normalize input `VectorInput`, the Slab input `Normal`), `connect_material_property` for a legacy material, `MaterialInstanceConstantFactoryNew` with `set_material_instance_*_parameter_value`, and a new Blueprint by `BlueprintFactory` plus `SubobjectDataSubsystem`. Collision on a component template is set with `set_collision_profile_name`, not `set_editor_property`.
- The layout was placed by a re-runnable script (`Saved/T49/layout.py` reading `layout.json`): it destroys everything in `Stage/Theater/Opera/` and places it again, so each try was one call.
