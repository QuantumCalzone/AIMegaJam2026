## Task

- **Why:** my request on 2026-11-15: when I win, I want to see how every AI reacts, the ones that were cut and the ones still on stage. Today a win goes from the last scene's reactions to `EndShow(true)`. That sends every AI seat, cut or not, the closing ask (who it suspected most, for the end screen's cards), and then the end screen waits behind See the results (T10, T14, T54, T60). The AIs learn that a human fooled them only through `game_messages`, and nothing on stage shows it. The cut robots still lie gray and tipped over (T11's `SetCut`), and a cut robot can't emote (T75). The whole show builds toward the reveal that a human fooled them, and today nobody on stage reacts to it.
- **Steps:**
  1. **Read first.** In `BP_Show`, read `EndShow`, `AskClosing`, `StoreClosing`, `ShowEndScreen` and `RevealEnd`, and only what they call. In `BP_Robot`, read `SetCut`, `ResetLook`, `Emote` and `Say` (T11, T75, T38, and the Hyper3D cast from T81 to T83). Also read how `BPC_LinePlayer` plays a line with its bubble (T30, T73, T38), and `BP_Show.StageLine`, where T77 makes the seat's robot `Emote` as its line starts to type. Decision 17 in T77's build sheet ([T77-build.md](T77-build.md)) already suggests giving `Emote` an `EvenCut` input like `Say`'s. Start each graph from its `Graphs/` file.
  2. **A new ask, `reaction_win`,** in `prompts.json`. Send it only on a win, to every AI seat in the show, cut or not, in the same batch as the closing ask, so a win waits no longer than it does now. The text, roughly: "The show is over, and the human fooled you all (see game_messages). React to the reveal in character, in one line to the cast, whether you were cut earlier or are still on stage. Fill: speech. No actions." Every reply carries its `emotion` too (T76). The bots learn who the human was, and which of the two wins it was, from T54's line in `game_messages`. A separate ask leaves the closing ask and its free-mode phase as they are, and costs one cheap call per AI on a win. Show me the Bot Prompts tab edit first, and after my OK, copy it into `prompts.json` word for word. The legibility rules and the 15-word cap (T59) apply.
  3. **Free mode.** Add a `reaction_win` phase to `free_mode.json`. Its `ask_contains` is a phrase that only this ask has. Give it six or more pre-written lines: stunned, embarrassed, a sore loser, a good sport, "I knew it", and a bot that blames its rival. Give it an emotion list that leans surprised, embarrassed, angry and sad, with a little happy for the good sport. The relay bundles this file, so a push redeploys the relay: ask me first. Check that the relay's free mode answers the new ask (`--relay --free`), and change `Relay/src/index.js` only if it doesn't.
  4. **The cut robots get back up.** When the reveal starts, each cut robot rises where it fell: its tip eases back to upright, the reverse of the way `SetCut` tipped it. Its name label comes back, its face lights again, and it can emote and show a bubble. It keeps its gray body, so I can still tell who was cut. If the screenshots show that gray hides the face, give it back its colors and mark the cut another way, for example a small "CUT" under its name. Put this in `BP_Robot`, for example as `Rise()`, and keep `RestartShow`'s reset working.
  5. **My robot.** As the reveal starts, my robot cheers once (T19's `Cheer`), and its label shows the `HUMAN` stamp, so the cast's reactions point at someone. If T19's look act makes it easy, each robot looks at my robot while it speaks.
  6. **Play the reactions one at a time** through the line player, the way the reactions after a cut play. The chat gets a header such as "THE REVEAL". The cut robots go first, in the order they were cut, then the robots still on stage, so the ones fooled to the end speak last. Each line gets its bubble (T38) and its face (T77). A failed or empty reply plays nothing, and the next robot goes. These lines don't go in the shared log, because no ask reads it after the show. After the last line, the end goes on as it does now: the log line "Read the last reactions, then press See the results.", then See the results. With autoplay on, the reactions play and the end screen follows, as it does now. Play the reactions before `ShowEndScreen` prints `SHOW END`, and before T61's stagehand speaks on the end screen (`RunCue`'s `"end"`), so the stagehand speaks after them. A loss plays as it does now.
  7. **Check lines.** Print `NHOS: WIN REACT <id> cut=<0|1> emotion=<id> | <line>` for each line, and `NHOS: WIN REACTIONS n=<n>` before `NHOS: SHOW END`.
  8. **The checker.** In the offline checks of `Tools/check_prompts.py`, add the new ask: its text, its free-mode phase, its lines' legibility and the 15-word cap, and every emotion id it uses in `emotes.json`.
