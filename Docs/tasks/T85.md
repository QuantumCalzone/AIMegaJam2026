## Findings

- **The names in Scene Prep (step 1).** `StageSetup` (`FindRobots`, then each robot's `Setup` with its seat label and color and its `SetPersona`) ran only from scene 1's `StartScene`. It now runs from `OpenPrep` when `SceneNum` is 1, and `StartScene` no longer calls it (its node is deleted, `Resize` wires straight to `LightSeat`). Nothing else starts early and no robot moves: `StageSetup` only fills `Robots`, names and colors the tags and calls `ResetLook`. T104's dressing still comes when `Phase` leaves "scene prep" (`BP_Robot.WatchShow`), so no `BP_Robot` change was needed. Shot: `prep1_names.png`.
- **How the robot under the mouse is found (step 5).** The first way in the task: a screen test in `WBP_Stage`, no collision box. Each frame `TipPick` reads the mouse (`GetMousePositionOnViewport`), the camera's location and right vector (`GetAxes` of the camera rotation; there is no `Rotator|GetRightVector` node), then `TipTry` tests each robot slot. `RobotRect` projects three points with `ProjectWorldLocationToWidgetPosition` (viewport units, the same units as the mouse and `LocalToViewport`): the feet (the actor's location), the top of the name tag plus `TipTopZ` 30 cm, and the feet plus `TipHalfW` 50 cm along the camera's right (T103's heads are up to 98 cm wide with their pods). The box runs from the tag's top to the feet, that half-width either side of the line between them. When boxes overlap, the robot nearest the camera wins. No card shows while the mouse is over a panel (the header, the clapperboard, my card, the lower panel, the banner, the stamp, the face list, the @ list, T84's middle panel).
- **Where the card sits.** Beside the robot, on the side toward the screen's middle, its top at the tag's top, a gap of `S3` from the box. Then T38's fit rule (`TipFit`, `WBP_SpeechBubble.Fit` without the tail) moves it inside `Edge` and clear of the panels above. If that puts it over its own robot, it takes the other side when that side is clear. It is moved with its render translation in `TipLayer` (the last child of `RootBox`, `HitTestInvisible`), so it never takes a click. At 1600 × 900 and 1280 × 720 every card was clear of the screen edge, the header, the card on the right and the middle panel (`prep1_*.png`, `p12_left_meta.png`, `p12_right_openai.png`, `p12_center_openai.png`).

- **As a critical first-time player.** The card reads well: the name is in the same color as its tag and its square, the role is short and quiet, the want is the line that matters, and it is the card's biggest text. Once I have read the hint, the cast is easy to find, and it is easier to match to the robots than THE CAST was, because the card sits on the robot. But nothing on the stage says the robots can be pointed at: the cursor doesn't change and a robot doesn't light up under the mouse. A player who skips the header in Scene Prep, where the hint is the only mention, may never find the cards. For T34: a highlight on the robot under the mouse (or a glow on its tag) would show it, and the hint could come back in the scene's first turn line.
- **The keyboard (for T34).** The cards can't be reached by keyboard, and since THE CAST left the card on the right, a player who plays by keyboard only can't see the other actors' roles and wants anywhere. That matters for T34's keyboard-only show: either a key that steps through the robots (Tab is the input's), or the cast back in a collapsible part of the card.
