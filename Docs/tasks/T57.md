# T57 Silence and polish are tells: a pass shows as "passed"

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task's title line, **Model and effort**, **Depends on**, **Needs**, **Touches** and any **Waiting:** or **Blocked:** line are in its entry there, under "Open".

## Task

- **Why:** design change 8 of 12 (2026-11-15). When an actor passes in Free Talk, "passed" shows next to them, and the bots see the pass. The bots treat both silence and lines that are too polished as evidence, so speaking up or staying quiet becomes a real choice for me. The rule is in the design doc's Rules tab ("Free Talk") and Bot Prompts tab ("Opening prompt: game rules").
- **Steps:**
  1. **`prompts.json` `game`:** copy the doc's line "Silence is evidence, and so is a line that sounds too polished. Notice who passes and who sounds unlike the rest." word for word. It reverses part of an old line in "Spotting the human": "How a line is typed is never evidence. Ignore punctuation, capitals, spelling and emoji...". Show me a new wording for that line before the commit: typos and capitals still mean nothing (the input converter fixes spelling), but how polished a line sounds now counts. In `asks.free_talk`, "Pass only if you truly have nothing to add" may need to say that a pass is seen.
  2. **The bots see the pass.** They already do: `PlayTalk` logs a pass as `does` "passes" (T08). Check that this still holds after T30, and that a failed AI reply, which is also logged as a pass, doesn't make that bot a suspect too often in free mode (`pass_chance` in `free_mode.json`).
  3. **"passed" on screen:** in `PlayTalk`, a pass puts a "passed" marker next to that actor, where I read the cast during Free Talk: by its name over the robot, or in the bubble's place if T38 is in by then. It stays until the actor's next line or the end of that Free Talk, and it clears at the start of each Free Talk. It shows for every actor, me included. Follow T32's style Blueprint.
  4. **Check:** `--offline` and `--relay --free` pass. A free autoplay show, whose canned round 2 line is "pass", shows "passed" next to my robot and next to any bot that passes (a screenshot), and the marker is gone in the next scene's Free Talk. Print `NHOS: PASSED <id> scene=<n> round=<r>`.
- **Done when:** the check passes, and I look at one free show in a game window and say the marker reads well.
