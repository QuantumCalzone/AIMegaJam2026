## Task

- **Model and effort:** Opus 5.5, high. A prompt change that reverses an old rule ("how a line is typed is never evidence"), plus a marker on the in-show screen.
- **Depends on:** T30 (the line queue plays each Free Talk line), T50 (the rule lines it sits beside), T55 (the lock on `BP_Show`), T73 (the locks on `BP_Show` and `WBP_Stage`: the audio tasks go first)
- **Needs:** editor + Unreal MCP running, Test for Free for the PIE shows, the relay for a `--relay --free` check.
- **Touches:** `Content/NHOS/Data/prompts.json` (`game`, `asks.free_talk`), `Tools/check_prompts.py`, `Content/NHOS/Core/BP_Show.uasset` (`PlayTalk`, the start of each Free Talk), `Content/NHOS/UI/WBP_Stage.uasset` or `Content/NHOS/Stage/BP_Robot.uasset` (the "passed" marker), `Graphs/` files for the graphs it writes, `Docs/tasks/T57.md`.
- **Why:** design change 8 of 12 (2026-11-15). When an actor passes in Free Talk, "passed" shows next to them, and the bots see the pass. The bots treat both silence and lines that are too polished as evidence, so speaking up or staying quiet becomes a real choice for me. The rule is in the design doc's Rules tab ("Free Talk") and Bot Prompts tab ("Opening prompt: game rules").
- **Steps:**
  1. **`prompts.json` `game`:** copy the doc's line "Silence is evidence, and so is a line that sounds too polished. Notice who passes and who sounds unlike the rest." word for word. It reverses part of an old line in "Spotting the human": "How a line is typed is never evidence. Ignore punctuation, capitals, spelling and emoji...". Show me a new wording for that line before the commit: typos and capitals still mean nothing (the input converter fixes spelling), but how polished a line sounds now counts. In `asks.free_talk`, "Pass only if you truly have nothing to add" may need to say that a pass is seen.
  2. **The bots see the pass.** They already do: `PlayTalk` logs a pass as `does` "passes" (T08). Check that this still holds after T30, and that a failed AI reply, which is also logged as a pass, doesn't make that bot a suspect too often in free mode (`pass_chance` in `free_mode.json`).
  3. **"passed" on screen:** in `PlayTalk`, a pass puts a "passed" marker next to that actor, where I read the cast during Free Talk: by its name over the robot, or in the bubble's place if T38 is in by then. It stays until the actor's next line or the end of that Free Talk, and it clears at the start of each Free Talk. It shows for every actor, me included. Follow T32's style Blueprint.
  4. **Check:** `--offline` and `--relay --free` pass. A free autoplay show, whose canned round 2 line is "pass", shows "passed" next to my robot and next to any bot that passes (a screenshot), and the marker is gone in the next scene's Free Talk. Print `NHOS: PASSED <id> scene=<n> round=<r>`.
- **Done when:** the check passes, and Claude checks from screenshots of one free show in a game window that the marker reads well.
- **Result:** done on 2026-11-15. A Free Talk pass puts "(passed)" over that actor's name on its robot, in `UIStyle`'s `Quiet` lavender, for me and for every bot; the actor's next line takes it off, and every marker comes off at the end of each Free Talk and the start of the next (`BP_Robot.SetPassed`, `BP_Show.PassCue`, `PassClear`, `MarkPass`, two new `RunCue` cue kinds). The log prints `NHOS: PASSED <id> scene=<n> round=<r>` when a marker shows. The bots' rules now say silence and too-polished lines are evidence (the doc's line, word for word) and that typing is not, and the Free Talk ask says everyone sees a pass. `--offline`, `--relay --free` and three free autoplay shows passed; the screenshots show the marker reads at 1600 × 900 and 1280 × 720.

## Findings

### Decided for the person (to review)

The build sheet's list ([T57-build.md](T57-build.md), "Decisions made for my person"), made by the Mac's prep session without my person. Each was used as written; where the build changed something, it says so.

1. **The marker is part of the name over the robot**, in `Quiet` (#C9C3DA) instead of white. No new component: template components don't reach the five robots already placed in `L_Stage` until they are destroyed and placed again (T17, T46), and `L_Stage` is outside T57's Touches. *Changed in the build:* the sheet's one-line "OpenAI (passed)" ran two neighbors' labels into each other when both passed ("Anthropic (pa…)XAI (passed)", `Saved/T57/shots/b/173546_548.png`), so "(passed)" now goes on its own line above the name (TextRender's `<br>`). The label is bottom-aligned, so the name stays where it was, and the label is no wider than the name. `Quiet` read well on every backdrop the runs showed, so the white fallback wasn't needed.
2. **When it shows:** a `"passed"` cue goes into the line queue just before the pass line, so the marker shows as that line comes up. For an AI pass that is just before "<name> is typing..." and then "<name> passes"; for my pass, the marker and the line show together. A cue after the line would show the marker about 2 s late, when the next speaker's typing line starts.
3. **When it goes:** the actor's next spoken line takes it off (an `"unpass"` cue just before that line), and every marker comes off at the end of each Free Talk (`EndFreeTalk`) and again at the start of the next (`StartFreeTalk`). Both are cues, so a marker never changes before the lines in front of it have played. A second pass by the same actor shows the marker again and prints a second `PASSED` line.
4. **`PlayTalk` isn't edited** (it is in T57's Touches): `ShowLine`, which has a file, does the work in Free Talk. `StartFreeTalk` and `EndFreeTalk` get one call node each. *In the build:* `StartFreeTalk`'s `PassClear` goes right after the function entry, not just before `StartTalkRound`, because two exec wires lead into `StartTalkRound` (with and without autoplay's `LoadCannedTalk`). Nothing between queues a cue, so the `unpass` cue still plays before the round-1 header.
5. **The new wording** of the old "How a line is typed is never evidence" line: "Typing is never evidence: the converter fixes the human's spelling, so ignore typos, punctuation, capitals and emoji. Judge what each actor says and does, and how it sounds." Capitals and emoji stay ignored, as the task says, even though the converter keeps them. **My person picked this wording in this session** (asked with the line it replaces, before the commit).
6. **The doc's line goes right after it**, in "Spotting the human", as a top-level bullet like the line it sits beside. `game`'s Free Talk line ("One line each per round, or a pass.") doesn't change: the bots already see a pass in the log, and T58 edits that line next.
7. **`asks.free_talk`:** "Pass only if you truly have nothing to add." becomes "Pass only if you truly have nothing to add: everyone sees a pass, and silence is evidence." Not in the doc (the doc doesn't hold the asks).
8. **`pass_chance` stays 0.1** (step 2): free-mode votes pick a random name and read no log, so a free-mode pass never makes a bot a suspect. A 3-scene free show has about 18 AI Free Talk lines, so about 2 AI passes, enough to see AI markers.
9. **A failed AI reply still logs a pass** (T08's rule, unchanged), and now shows "passed" too. The bots may hold that silence against the bot, but replies rarely fail (none in T08's, T20's or T36's runs). If failures turn up in real shows, log a failed reply as "stands still" instead; that would be its own small task.
10. **`NHOS: PASSED` prints when the marker shows** (the cue), with the scene and round of the pass. `PassCue` builds the text when the pass is logged, since the logic runs ahead of the screen (T30).
11. **The checker** gets the round-2 Free Talk ask after a pass (4 more calls per full run), the file checks, and the `--log` check (part 5).

### Unreal MCP and editor rules found in this task

- **A `TextRenderComponent` breaks a line at `<br>`** in its text. With `VerticalAlignment` `TextBottom` (the robots' labels), the new line goes above, so put the extra line first to keep the last line in place.
- **`AssetTools.read_file` reads only under `Content/` and `Saved/`** (and the engine's plugin content), and only `.csv`, `.html`, `.json`, `.md`, `.py` or `.txt`. To batch `write_graph_dsl` from files in one script, copy the sources to `Saved/<task>/src/*.txt` first.
- **`find_nodes` matches a variable node's title without spaces** (`SetLine`, not "Set Line"), so a search for a variable's readers uses its exact name.
- **`Local/switches.json` is back** to autoplay, `spare_human` and `fast_lines` off, and every game window is closed.
