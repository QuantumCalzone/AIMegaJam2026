## Task

- **Model and effort:** Opus 5.5, high. A costume for every role, each drawn on all five approved characters, with a version made for my cube head.
- **Depends on:** T95 (the approved characters, whose heads the costumes fit). It needs no editor and changes no asset, so it can run on either machine, beside the chain.
- **Needs:** `Local/leonardo_key.json`; my OK on the estimated cost before the first paid call; my pick for each costume and my approval of each fit sheet; my OK on the design doc edit.
- **Touches:** new `Docs/Art/Costumes/` (with the role table that T104 writes the data from), the design doc's Art Inspiration tab (Costumes), `Docs/tasks/T96.md`.
- **Why:** my request on 2026-11-15: the costumes must actually fit the characters, mine included, and they are part of the new art. Today each role in `scenes.json` names a headpiece and a color, and `costumes.json` gives each of the 14 headpieces one mesh and one fit (scale and offset), which every robot uses (T47). One fit can't suit a round head, Meta's doll head and my cube head all at once, so the headpieces float or clip. This task draws each role's costume on each of the five approved characters from T95. T104 makes the models.
- **What a costume is.** At least its headpiece. It may be more, such as a collar, a cape or a sash, if that helps the role read and I approve it. A costume is worn on top of the robot and never hides its face, its seat color or its name tag. A cut robot keeps the costume it wore (T47).
- **The cube head.** My robot's head is a cube. Its version of each costume is made for a cube: a hat that sits on a flat top, a wig that hangs from square edges. Don't stretch a round-head version to fit.

## Findings

- **Fit:** the edit model (`gemini-2.5-flash-image`) dresses only one character per call. On the five-character lineup it dressed three, on four it dressed two, and on a pair it dressed one. So each fit sheet is five edits, each on a canvas that holds one character's lineup panel and a plain gray half. My person raised the cap from 1,200 to 2,500 for this after the bob wig test, choosing all five fits over one per head shape.
- **The cube:** the cube versions followed the prompt for soft pieces (wigs, hoods, the beanie, the cap, the dino hood, the pan), which came out boxy. Hats with a crown and brim stayed round and sat flat on the lid, which reads well, so those share the round model.
- **Open for T104** (in the README's table): the dwarf's beard covers the mouth on SpaceXAI, Meta and the cube in the approved sheet, so T104 makes the face gap taller; the cube hood's view lost its cape; the dwarf's cube view is poor, so T104 builds it from the fit sheet's cube panel; the cube cap's peak points sideways in the sheet but forward in its view, and the view is right; the tiara came out as a full crown ring on every head, and my person approved it so.
- **Meta's seat color:** T108 made Meta mustard (#C8AC12) while this task ran. The role colors are T47's, unchanged; the nearest is the farm kid's straw hat (#E3C770), paler. If it reads as Meta's color at the house camera, T104 can pale it further.
- **Lessons** for later art tasks are in the README: one character per edit, the `[character | gray]` canvas, `sips -c` ignoring its crop offset, edits copying head parts from one robot to another, and the beard that keeps going over the mouth.
