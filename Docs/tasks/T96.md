# T96 New art, part 3: concepts for a costume for each role, fitted to every character, approved by me

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task's title line, **Model and effort**, **Depends on**, **Needs**, **Touches** and any **Waiting:** or **Blocked:** line are in its entry there, under "Open".

## Task

- **Why:** my request on 2026-11-15: the costumes must actually fit the characters, mine included, and they are part of the new art. Today each role in `scenes.json` names a headpiece and a color, and `costumes.json` gives each of the 14 headpieces one mesh and one fit (scale and offset), which every robot uses (T47). One fit can't suit a round head, Meta's doll head and my cube head all at once, so the headpieces float or clip. This task draws each role's costume on each of the five approved characters from T95. T104 makes the models.
- **What a costume is.** At least its headpiece. It may be more, such as a collar, a cape or a sash, if that helps the role read and I approve it. A costume is worn on top of the robot and never hides its face, its seat color or its name tag. A cut robot keeps the costume it wore (T47).
- **The cube head.** My robot's head is a cube. Its version of each costume is made for a cube: a hat that sits on a flat top, a wig that hangs from square edges. Don't stretch a round-head version to fit.
- **Steps:**
  1. **Read first:**
     - T94's style guide
     - T95's approved cast and turnarounds in `Docs/Art/Cast/`
     - the roles: each card's seats in `Content/NHOS/Data/scenes.json`, with their headpiece and color
     - `Content/NHOS/Data/costumes.json`
     - T47's notes in `Docs/tasks/T47.md`
     - the design doc's Costumes section
  2. **One costume per role.** List every role on every card. Roles that share a headpiece today may keep sharing one costume, if that still fits the story. For each costume, make two or three candidates on the character it suits best, with `art_gen.py image` or `edit` (with `--ref` for the approved character). Before the first paid call, tell me the estimated cost of this task's generations and ask once. Ask again only if the cost would go over that.
  3. **My pick for each.** Show me the candidates and ask one question at a time. Change a picked costume with `art_gen.py edit` (my memory note `edit-picked-images`).
  4. **Fit it to every character.** Put each approved costume on all five characters with `art_gen.py edit`, from the approved character and costume images. Make one sheet per costume with its five fits side by side. Change each head shape's fit as its shape needs, the cube most of all. Approve each sheet with me.
  5. **Views for the model.** For each approved costume, make one clean view per version that the models will need (as a rule, one for the round heads, one for Meta's head and one for the cube), on a plain background, with nothing else in the frame. Write down which characters can share a version, because T104 makes one model per version.
  6. **Record it.** Keep the candidates, the picks, the fit sheets and the model views in `Docs/Art/Costumes/<costume>/`. Write each round, my words, the prompts, the generation ids and the cost in `Docs/Art/Costumes/README.md`. Add a table there with one row per role (card and seat), its costume and its color. T104 writes `costumes.json` and `scenes.json` from that table.
  7. **The design doc.** Show me the edit to the Costumes section of the Art Inspiration tab: a fit sheet for each costume, or one overview. Make it after my OK, in its file in `Docs/Design/`.
- **Done when:**
  1. Every role on every card has a costume that I approved.
  2. Each costume has a fit sheet on all five characters that I approved, and the cube version is made for a cube.
  3. Each version that a model needs has a clean view, and the README says which characters share each version.
  4. The README's role table is complete, and every round is logged with its cost.
  5. The Costumes section of the Art Inspiration tab shows the approved costumes, after my OK.
