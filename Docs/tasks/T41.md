# T41 Generate the story props

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task's title line, **Model and effort**, **Depends on**, **Needs**, **Touches** and any **Waiting:** or **Blocked:** line are in its entry there, under "Open".

## Task

- **Why:** each story card brings four clay props to the Prop Table (the design doc's "Stories" tab). The plush horse head is done; this task makes the other 21 core props, and the 6 spares if credits are left.
- **Steps:**
  1. `python Tools/art_gen.py batch-images --kind prop`, core props first, then `pick` each one as T40 does. A prop needs a bold outline at about 30 px tall: one or two strong colors, nothing thin that vanishes.
  2. `python Tools/art_gen.py batch-meshes --kind prop --max-credits 620`, with a texture. Plain shapes run on `meshy-6-lite` (15 credits), detailed ones on `meshy-7.1` (30 credits), as the manifest says.
  3. `sheet --kind prop`, and check every thumbnail against its story: it reads as the prop it names, in clay. Redo at most four that fail.
  4. If at least 150 credits are left after T42's 90 are set aside, make the spares the same way. If not, skip them and say so: a spare is never needed by a want.
  5. Add a row for each prop to `Docs/Assets.md`, and show me the sheet.
- **Done when:** every core prop has its FBX, log and `Assets.md` row, and I say the props look right.

## Findings

- **Run (2026-11-15).** 20 core props made (the plush horse head and the red pill were already done): 13 on `meshy-7.1` and 7 on `meshy-6-lite`, all with a texture, 495 credits. Then 5 of the 6 spares: piggy bank, dino plush and cheeseburger on `meshy-7.1`, frying pan and milk carton moved to `meshy-6-lite` (plain shapes), 120 credits. 615 credits in all, balance 765 to 150, which leaves T42's 90 and 60 for its retries. The bowl of oranges is the one spare not made: it is the one engine shapes build best (spheres in a bowl), and a want never needs a spare. Its reference image is picked (`ref.png`), so `batch-meshes --ids bowl_of_oranges` makes it for 30 credits once credits allow.
- **Picks:** tin_can_robot 2, glow_stick_sword 1, toy_blaster 3, cursed_ring 3 (second prompt), glowing_staff 4, water_bucket 4, dino_egg 4, meaty_drumstick 1, safety_waiver 1, cannoli_tray 1, wedding_envelope 2, glowing_briefcase 3, gold_pocket_watch 3, giant_milkshake 4, rolled_up_rug 1, bowling_ball 3, ransom_bag 3, blue_pill 3, ringing_phone 1, bent_spoon 4 (fourth prompt); spares piggy_bank 2, frying_pan 4, dino_plush 1, bowl_of_oranges 2, giant_cheeseburger 2, milk_carton 2. 18 of 20 core props had a good candidate in the first batch. Top-down views (the cannoli trays) were passed over for the one three-quarter view, and a candidate with lettering ("fety forme" on a waiver) was rejected.
- **Two prompts reworded (in the manifest).** The cursed ring "lying flat, with a faint red glow" gave four clay donuts in a red halo: Meshy would have made the halo a red disc. "Standing upright on its edge, a thick band of bright metallic gold, one small deep red gem on top, no glow" reads as a ring; a red glow, if wanted, is an emissive or light in the engine. "A teaspoon bent in the middle at a sharp angle" gave straight spoons; "bent into a V like a trick spoon" gave two spoons; "seen from the side, bent backwards into a hook" gave a ring of metal. What worked: "a big chunky silver spoon lying on a table with a kink in its handle: halfway along, the thick handle bends upward ... while the round bowl rests flat. One spoon, in one piece." Name the pose by what rests on the table and what rises.
- **Sheet check.** All 25 new models read as the props they name, in clay; none was redone. Notes for T43: the gold pocket watch's texture came out tan, not gold, and the cursed ring is chunky matte yellow, so both may want a metallic tint. The glow-stick sword and the staff are long and thin, so at 30 px they read as lines; the sword's glow and the staff's crystal need an emissive to show. The safety waiver and the wedding envelope are flat slabs, which read from above on the table but not from the side. The bowling ball shows one finger hole from the thumbnail's angle. The ringing phone sits on its cradle; the "ringing" is for the engine to show (a shake or a sound).
- **Contact sheets.** As T40 found, a small Swift script (`swiftc`, AppKit) tiles PNGs with labels; sheets of 4 assets × 4 candidates made the picks quick. The labeled sheet of every prop model is `Local/art/prop_sheet.png` (not in git), beside `Local/art/sheet_prop.html`.
