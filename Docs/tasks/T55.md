# T55 Remove the Director AI

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task's title line, **Model and effort**, **Depends on**, **Needs**, **Touches** and any **Waiting:** or **Blocked:** line are in its entry there, under "Open".

## Task

- **Why:** design change 6 of 12 (2026-11-15). No AI outside the cast ever breaks a tie. Since T52 a tie cuts nobody, so the Director's tiebreak is never asked for. This task takes the Director AI out of the game, so that nothing loads it, pays for it or mentions it.
- **Steps:**
  1. **`prompts.json`:** delete `director` and `asks.tiebreak`, and every mention of the Director in `game` and `_readme`. The director's note keeps its name: the note is from the show's director, who is not an AI in the game.
  2. **`llm.json`:** delete `roles.director`, and its mentions in both `_readme` texts.
  3. **`free_mode.json`:** delete the `tiebreak` phase. The relay bundles this file, so a deploy is needed for the relay's free mode to match; ask me before you deploy. Check `Relay/` for any other mention of the Director or the tiebreak.
  4. **`Tools/check_prompts.py`:** remove `tiebreak` from `ALL_ASKS`, the `director` role, `tiebreak_state` and the tiebreak jobs.
  5. **`BP_Show`:** delete `AskDirector`, `PlayTiebreak`, `DirectorCut` and `DirectorState`, the "tiebreak" branch of `HandleReply`, the `Tiebreak` and `TiedNames` state if nothing else reads them, and the Director's line on the banner. List the graphs first; read only these and their callers. Delete their files in `Graphs/` if any exist.
  6. **`BPC_LLMClient`:** check whether it reads `roles.director` or builds a free-mode tiebreak reply; remove what it has.
  7. **`Docs/switches.example.json` and `Docs/ItchPage.md`:** no Director. The itch page's "Director's Cut" paragraph says the Director breaks ties, and its keys section says the Director runs on Anthropic; reword both. The theme still holds through the director's note and the cut.
  8. **Check:** `--offline` and `--relay --free` pass. With `force_tie` on, a free autoplay show reaches `NHOS: SHOW END` and its log has no `NHOS: DIRECTOR` line and no `LogScript` warning.
- **Done when:** the check passes, and `grep -riE "tiebreak|AskDirector|DirectorCut" Content/NHOS/Data Tools Graphs Docs/switches.example.json` finds nothing.
