## Findings

### Decisions (mine, in my person's place)

1. **Meta keeps T80's engine head; the Hyper3D head is imported but unused.** Side by side on the model body (`Saved/T83/cmp_ab.png`), the Rodin head reads as a realistic bald mannequin: closed eyes, lips, a tall oval skull and a thin neck, with T80's glasses floating 8 cm in front of its flat face. meta.png's head is a smooth, wide toy ball with a small nose and a line mouth, which is what T80's sphere already is. On the Hyper3D body, T80's head reads closer to meta.png than either full version did. `SM_MetaHead` and its material and textures stay in `Cast/meta/`, referenced by nothing, so they don't cook. **If my person wants Meta's head from Hyper3D too, it needs a new generation** (a toy-like ball head with no eyes or mouth, ears as round pods, no neck), which I didn't make: no paid call without asking.
2. **My box head's paint takes a new `M_CastModel` instance, `MI_YouHeadPaint`,** with a flat 8 × 8 teal texture (`T_YouHead_Paint`, from `Docs/CastArt/you/head/paint_teal.png`, sRGB (44, 116, 109), the mean of the body texture's teal). The importer's `M_YouHead_Teal_cardboard_paint` has no `Color` parameter, so the cut couldn't gray it, and its teal (48, 99, 87) was greener and darker than the body box. The interior and tape slots keep the importer's materials (they don't gray; the inside is dark anyway).
3. **The whole model part grays on the cut** (Meta's skin-colored arms, my jeans and sneakers), as T82 chose: from the house camera it reads as "this one is out".
4. **`SlotL`/`SlotR` kept as cardboard backing** (`M_YouHead_Unpainted_cardboard_interior`, at x 16, behind my eyes, 16 × 19 cm): the model's box is hollow, so the hood's inside showed red through the eye slots. With the plates, the slots stay cardboard brown under every headpiece.
5. **The dark shades on my box:** `on: {"human": {"x": -4.4, "z": -24.6}}`. The model box is shallower (front at x 23.4, not 28) and its slots are lower (z 134.7, not 138), so at the shared fit the shades sat on my forehead with my eyes showing under them. Every other headpiece fits my box at T80's socket, (−2, 0, 162) at 1.1.
6. **Shoulders:** Meta 27 at z 82, not the prep's 30 at 86: the tee is 25.4 cm half-wide at its sleeves (z 76) and narrows to 21 at z 86, so at 86 the arm's blue sleeve cap floated above the shoulder. Me 34 at z 94: the body box is 30 cm half-wide.
7. **Eyes stay 10 cm** in my 12.3 × 15.7 cm slots: bigger eyes would poke through the box front when an emotion widens them (`EyeMaxW` 1.3).
