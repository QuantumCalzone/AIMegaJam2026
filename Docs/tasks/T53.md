# T53 Scene clock: one scene fewer than the cast, on a clapperboard

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task's title line, **Model and effort**, **Depends on**, **Needs**, **Touches** and any **Waiting:** or **Blocked:** line are in its entry there, under "Open".

## Task

- **Why:** design change 4 of 12 (2026-11-15). The number of scenes is the cast size minus one: 4 scenes with 5 actors, 3 with 4. That includes one spare scene, which a tie uses up (T52). The show ends when two actors remain or after the last scene, and a clapperboard on screen shows "SCENE n / total". Today `SceneCount` is the seat count minus 2, and a show ends only when I am cut or two actors remain. The rule is in the design doc's Rules tab ("Cast & personas", "The Cut" step 6).
- **Steps:**
  1. **`BP_Show`:** `SceneCount` = the cast size − 1, where it is set in `SetupShow`. `show.scenes` in the state the AIs read follows it. Update the scene banner's "Scene n of m" text if it needs it.
  2. **The end check:** after a scene's last step (the reactions, or a tie once T52 is in), end the show when two actors remain or when `SceneNum` equals `SceneCount`. I am still on stage in both cases, so both call `EndShow` as a win. Until T52 lands, every scene cuts one actor, so the last scene is never reached; T52's check plays it.
  3. **The clapperboard in `WBP_Stage`:** a small clapperboard in a corner that shows "SCENE 2 / 4" in every phase from Scene Prep to the reactions. Follow T32's style Blueprint. Give it its own function, for example `SetClapper(Scene, Total)`, called where the scene number changes.
  4. **`prompts.json` `game`:** "How a show works" says how many scenes a show has and when it ends. `Tools/check_prompts.py` builds `show.scenes` the same way.
  5. **Check:** a free autoplay show with 5 actors prints `scenes=4` in its `SETUP` line (add the field if it is missing) and sends `"scenes": 4` in the state; one with 4 actors gives 3. Screenshots of the clapperboard in Scene Prep and in a scene turn.
- **Done when:** the check passes and both shows reach `NHOS: SHOW END`.
