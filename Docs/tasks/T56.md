## Task

- **Why:** design change 7 of 12 (2026-11-15). Each bot also wants to reach the final two itself. Each persona has a rival and leans toward cutting that rival when it can't decide between two suspects. The pairs are Sam Botman and Dario Amobotei (`visionary` and `worrier`), and Elon Muskatron and Mark Zuckerbot (`overclocker` and `copycat`). It gives me rivalries to stir up. The rule and the four rival lines are in the design doc's Bot Prompts tab ("Opening prompt: game rules" and "Rivals").
- **Steps:** no editor. An actor's system text is `game`, then `base`, then its persona's `prompt`, so this is a change to the data files only.
  1. **`personas.json`:** add each persona's rival line from the doc's Rivals table as the last line of its `prompt`, word for word. The line describes the rival by its traits and never by name, because the AIs see only Player names. Add a `rival` field with the rival's persona id; no AI reads it, and T60 uses it for the reveal on the win screen. Update `_readme`.
  2. **`prompts.json` `game`:** copy the doc's two lines, "You also want to reach the final two yourself." and "Your persona names a rival. When you can't decide between two suspects, lean toward your rival.", word for word. Goal 2 ("Don't get cut yourself") must agree with them. Finding the human stays goal 1.
  3. **If the rival isn't on stage,** the bot has no one to lean toward. The game needs no logic for that, but check that a bot whose rival was cut doesn't talk as if the rival were still there.
  4. **`Tools/check_prompts.py`:** every persona has a `rival` that is another persona's id, the pairs are mutual, and the rival line holds no persona name, provider name or Player name.
  5. **Check:** `python Tools/check_prompts.py --offline`, then `--relay --free`. A run that calls the providers costs money: do one only if I ask, and then read 10 votes to see that no bot votes for its rival against the evidence.
- **Done when:** both check runs pass, and `personas.json` and `prompts.json` match the doc word for word.
