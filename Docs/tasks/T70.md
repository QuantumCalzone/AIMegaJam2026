## Task

- **Why:** the itch.io page is the first thing a judge sees, and the submission needs a 30 to 60 second gameplay video. `Docs/ItchPage.md` and the shot list in `Docs/Submission.md` were drafted on 2026-11-14, before most of the game was built, so the text is out of date and nothing has been shot yet. This task makes everything the page needs from the finished game, ready to paste and upload: the description, the screenshots, the main screenshot (the cover image) and the video. The upload stays mine: T23 was dropped because I make the submission myself.
- **Steps:** this task is larger than most. If it runs long, finish steps 1 to 5, push them, and tell me, so that the video (steps 6 and 7) becomes a task of its own.
  1. **The description.** Read how the game plays now: the design doc's Rules tab, `Docs/DevLog.md` from 2026-11-14 on, and the files of the tasks done since. Rewrite `Docs/ItchPage.md` to match, both the page settings and the page text: how a show works, how to win, the controls (the action builder, the speech field, @ mentions, the timer), the three ways to play, the install steps for the packaged build, the known limits, and the credits rebuilt from `Docs/Assets.md`. Settle every **[Txx]** marker and delete it. Leave the **[YOU]** blanks, and list the ones still open at the top of the file. Each AI goes by its provider's name.
  2. **A copy to paste.** itch.io's description editor takes rich text, not Markdown, so also write the page text as `Docs/ItchMedia/description.html`: plain tags (`h2`, `h3`, `p`, `ul`, `ol`, `table`, `strong`, `code`), with no styles or scripts. Show me the page text before the commit.
  3. **Set up the shoot.** Package a build with T22's command (or use the game window, as `CLAUDE.md` shows it) at 1920 × 1080. In `Local/switches.json`, set `"autoplay": false` and `"mode": "relay"`. Install ffmpeg after my OK (`winget install Gyan.FFmpeg`). Record a 10-second test clip of the game window and check that it has both the picture and the game's sound. ffmpeg's `gdigrab` takes no sound, so record the sound from a loopback device (such as Stereo Mix) or with Unreal's submix recording, and lay it under the picture. Raw recordings stay out of git: keep them in `Saved/ItchMedia/`.
  4. **Play and record.** After my OK for the cost, play two or three whole shows by hand on Play via Relay, and record each one (see "Claude does the playing" under "Build notes for every task" in the plan). Aim for one show where the AIs cut an AI, and one where they catch me, so the video has both endings.
  5. **The screenshots and the main one.** Take 5 to 8 screenshots at 1920 × 1080 with the UI in them, live or as frames from the recordings, as PNG files. Pick from: the theater mid-scene with all five robots, Scene Prep with the card and the backdrop, my turn in the action builder, Free Talk with speech bubbles, The Cut with the votes, the reveal stamp, the end screen, and the title screen with the three ways to play. Name them in page order: `Docs/ItchMedia/screenshots/01-<shot>.png`, and so on. The main screenshot, `Docs/ItchMedia/cover.png` (630 × 500, itch.io's cover size), is made already: key art from the cast's concept images, with the game's name (my request, 2026-11-15; `Docs/ItchMedia/README.md` has how). Check that it still matches the cast as the game shows it. If it doesn't, make it the old way: crop it from the strongest frame (the draft's pick is the theater mid-scene), and put the game's name on it only if the name still reads at 315 × 250, the size of itch.io's game grid. Update the screenshot list in the page settings table.
  6. **The video.** Bring the shot list in `Docs/Submission.md` up to date with the game. Then cut the video from the recordings with ffmpeg: 45 to 60 seconds, gameplay in the first five seconds, no waits for replies, the game's sound, and each shot's caption burned in, large enough to read on a phone. Export it as `Docs/ItchMedia/gameplay.mp4`: 1920 × 1080, H.264 video and AAC sound, under 100 MB.
  7. **Check the video.** `ffprobe` says it is 30 to 60 seconds long and has a sound stream. Pull one frame every 3 seconds and look at each one: no loading screen, black frame, editor window or desktop shows, every caption reads, and no key, key screen text or personal detail is on screen.
  8. **Hand-off.** Update "Left to do" in `Docs/Submission.md` with what this task made and what is left for me: upload the video and put its link in the form, upload the cover and the screenshots, paste the description, and fill in the **[YOU]** blanks.
- **Done when:** `Docs/ItchMedia/` holds `cover.png` (630 × 500), 5 to 8 screenshots at 1920 × 1080, `gameplay.mp4` (30 to 60 seconds, with sound and captions) and `description.html`; `Docs/ItchPage.md` has no **[Txx]** marker left and matches the game; Claude has looked at every image and at a frame from every shot of the video, and checked that each one shows the game as it is now; and I have approved the page text.
