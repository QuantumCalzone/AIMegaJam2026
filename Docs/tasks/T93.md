## Findings

### T93: what was built (2026-11-17, on the Mac)

- **The relay (`Relay/src/index.js`, `relay.config.json` `health`).** Each real call through the relay records its provider's last outcome (2xx = success; an error status or no reply = failure) in the `Limiter` (`health:<id>`, kept across the UTC day change). When a provider's last outcome is older than **300 s (5 minutes)**, the next `GET /health` claims a check for it (one claim per provider for all players together, held 30 s) and makes the smallest call: the first model in its `models`, `max_tokens` 1, prompt `Hi`, its `force` fields. `openai` answers a 1-token cap with a 400 ("Could not finish the message because max_tokens ... was reached"), so it gets `check_max_tokens` 16 (8 also works). `/health` lists a provider only when its last outcome succeeded and its `daily_tokens` aren't used up; the new `down` field gives the others and why, for logs. Checks never count as calls or against limits; their tokens are logged and counted in `check_tokens:<id>`, not in `daily_tokens`. `HEALTH_STALE_SECONDS` overrides the 300 s.
- **Checks per day and their cost** (in `Relay/README.md`): at most 288 per provider (1,152 in all), only while someone has the title open and no real call refreshed that provider in 5 minutes. Measured tokens per check: `anthropic` 12, `openai` 17 to 18, `xai` 186, `meta` 10. Worst case about 65,000 tokens and about $0.14 a day at output prices, $0.13 of it `xai`.
- **The game.** `WBP_Title`: the button reads **Play**. When the title opens it calls `CheckRelay`; Play is greyed out with "Checking the AI actors..." until the answer, then turns on (3 or more providers and budget left) or stays greyed out with `BPC_LLMClient.TitleLine` under it in red. It checks again every 60 s of real time (`CheckAt`, `GetRealTimeSeconds`) and on the click; the click reuses a check already in flight. A greyed-out button can't be clicked or focused, so focus starts on Test for Free and moves to Play the first time Play turns on. New function `SetPlay(On, Line, Quiet)`.
- **`BPC_LLMClient`.** `TitleLine` (new) picks `errors.json`'s `title_` case from `HealthKind` (an unreadable reply counts as `no_reply`). `HealthFail` keeps the kind and sends the analytics `error` only when the kind changes (`HealthSent`), because the title checks every minute. `OnHealth` drops the `health_test` providers and names `BP_Show.DrawCast` next to its floor of 3. `CanSeat` in a relay show seats only the providers of the last good check, so `DrawCast` follows the same answer as the button; with no good check (an autoplay relay run skips the title) it falls back to `llm.json`'s providers as before. `LoadConfig` reads `health_test` (`HealthTest`). The "needs the relay address" message now says Play.
- **Lines** (OKed on 2026-11-17): `title_too_few_labs`, `title_budget_used_up`, `title_no_reply` in `errors.json`, two each; `Tools/check_prompts.py` knows the three cases (no placeholders).
- **Design doc** (OKed and saved on 2026-11-17, in the Claude Doc, since T90 isn't done): Art Inspiration's Title row ("Play, on our relay. It stays greyed out, with a short line on why, until at least 3 AIs are working and today's budget isn't used up.") and Tech & Build's relay bullet (the title checks when it opens and every minute; the unavailable screen after you click Play).

### Unreal MCP rules found in this task

- **`write_graph_dsl` drops a `(return x)` when the function has no output parameter.** Add it first with `add_function_param` (`input_param` false); then the return node and its value come through.
- **`AssetTools.read_file` reads only under `Content/` and `Saved/`** (and engine plugin content), not the session's scratchpad. Put expanded DSL in `Saved/<task>/`.
- **`SlateInspectorToolset.Windows` with `select` takes keyboard focus away from the game's widgets,** so the next `PressKey` goes nowhere. Find the PIE window's ref with `Snapshot` at `maxDepth` 1 (the window whose title has "Preview") and pass it to `Screenshot`; focus stays where the game put it.
- **A widget's `InDeltaTime` is capped per frame** (about 0.125 s): with the editor in the background at about 3 fps, a 60 s timer summed from it took 160 s. `Utilities|Time|GetRealTimeSeconds` isn't capped.
- **`python3` on this Mac is an x86_64 build under Rosetta,** so a `wrangler dev` started from a Python script picks the wrong `workerd` binary and fails. Start it with `arch -arm64 npx wrangler dev`.
