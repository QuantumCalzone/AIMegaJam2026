# T93 The title's Play button replaces Play via Relay and is greyed out when fewer than three AIs work

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task's title line, **Model and effort**, **Depends on**, **Needs**, **Touches** and any **Waiting:** or **Blocked:** line are in its entry there, under "Open".

## Task

- **Why:** my request on 2026-11-15. The title screen's **Play via Relay** button becomes a plain **Play** button. It is greyed out, and can't be clicked, when two or more of the four AI providers aren't working.
- **Why the line is two.** A show needs at least four actors: me and three AIs. With all four providers working, a show has five actors and four scenes. With three working, it has four actors and three scenes (T53, and the `ai_seats` test switch). With only two, it would have three actors, and `BP_Show.DrawCast` already refuses that ("fewer than 3 AI players can take a seat"). So Play needs at least three working providers, which is the same rule as `DrawCast`'s.
- **What is wrong today:**
  - The game checks the relay only after I click Play via Relay. If the check fails, I land on the "AI service unavailable" screen (T21).
  - The relay's `GET /health` lists a provider when the relay has its key and its models (`providerReady` in `Relay/src/index.js`), not when it actually answers. A provider that is down, out of credit, or whose model has gone away still counts as working.

  This task fixes both. The Play button knows before I click, and "working" means the provider answered recently.
- **What stays the same:**
  - Test for Free and Play with my own API keys don't change.
  - The mode's id stays `relay`, in `BPC_LLMClient.SetMode`, the analytics `mode` event and the switches. Only the label changes.
  - The "AI service unavailable" screen still appears in the middle of a show, and on a click if the relay's answer changed since the last check.
