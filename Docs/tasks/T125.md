# T125 The web, part 3: a mobile look for the stage

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task's title line, **Model and effort**, **Depends on**, **Needs**, **Touches** and any **Waiting:** or **Blocked:** line are in its entry there, under "Open".

## Task

- **Why:** the web runs the stage on the mobile renderer. T112 found that most of the look survives it, with three exceptions: the near stalls go much darker without Lumen's bounce light, the robots cast no floor shadow (the stage lights are Stationary, and with static lighting off they cast no shadow on mobile), and 100 of the 117 textures are 2048 × 2048, 8 to 16 times what the house camera shows. This task fixes them on the mobile renderer only; the desktop game keeps its look exactly.
- **Steps:**
  1. **Read first.** `Docs/Mobile/README.md` ("What breaks or changes" and "What a mobile look would take"), T106's and T86's findings on the house's lights, the art direction's "Lighting it assumes", and T123's findings on the web tiers.
  2. **See it.** Use the mobile preview in a game window, as T112.md's "Ways of working" says, or the web build if T123 left a way to run it. Keep static lighting off.
  3. **The house fill.** Light the near rows on mobile only: a dim warm sky light, or a stronger `SM_HouseGlow`, switched by the feature level (or a device profile cvar) so desktop is unchanged. The near rows should read as on desktop: red seat backs, domes and hat brims.
  4. **Robot shadows.** Try both: the key spot (`KeyLight`) Movable with `r.Mobile.EnableMovableSpotlightsShadow=1` on mobile, and a soft clay-dark blob decal under each robot (in `BP_Robot`, shown only on mobile). Show me a sheet of both and use the one I pick.
  5. **Textures.** Cap the World texture group at 1024 for the web and mobile device profiles (`TextureLODGroups` or a LOD bias), with no change to any texture asset.
  6. **Check it.** Sheets of the title, Scene Prep, a scene turn, Free Talk, The Cut and the end screen: desktop before, desktop after (it must look the same), mobile before, mobile after. A free autoplay show on desktop reaches `NHOS: SHOW END`.
- **Done when:** I OK the mobile sheet; the desktop sheets show no change; a free autoplay show reaches `NHOS: SHOW END`; the art direction's lighting notes say what the mobile look adds.
