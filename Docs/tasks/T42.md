## Task

- **Why:** the clay stage is a grand opera house (the design doc's "Art Inspiration" tab, The stage). Engine shapes already make its frame; three pieces need models: the chandelier over the house, a chubby cherub for the gold proscenium (used several times), and one horseshoe theater box with its balustrade (repeated down both sides). The audience in the boxes are small capsule bots in top hats, made from engine shapes and T40's top hat, so they need no model here.
- **Steps:**
  1. `python Tools/art_gen.py batch-images --kind set`, then `pick` each one. A set piece is seen whole and from the front at three-quarters, gold and crimson clay with cream, as the doc says.
  2. `python Tools/art_gen.py batch-meshes --kind set --max-credits 100`, with a texture, on `meshy-7.1` (30 credits each).
  3. `sheet --kind set`, check each thumbnail, redo at most one.
  4. Add a row for each to `Docs/Assets.md`, and show me the sheet.
- **Done when:** the three FBX files, logs and `Assets.md` rows are in, and I say they look right.

## Findings

- **Run (2026-11-15).** One Leonardo batch of four candidates each, then Meshy 7.1 image to 3D with a 2K texture: 3 models, 90 credits, balance 150 to 60. No redo was needed, so 60 credits are left (two models' worth) for T43 or a spare.
- **Picks:** chandelier 2 (the gold one; 1 was dark bronze, 3 and 4 orange clay), cherub 3 (gold, sitting, trumpet, wings, three-quarter view), theater_box 3 (cream frame, crimson curtains and floor, gold balustrade; 1 and 2 were brown, 4 all red). Every prompt gave a usable candidate in the first batch.
- **Sheet check.** All three read as what they name, in clay. Notes for T43: the chandelier's texture came out bronze more than gold, and its arms and crystal drops are thin, so at a distance it reads as a dark tangle; a metallic gold tint and an emissive on the candles will help. The cherub's wings are small and pale. The theater box's balustrade posts came out pinkish red, with a gold top rail; its opening is a deep hollow, so the capsule audience bots can sit inside it. The box is closed at the back and sides, so it can be repeated side by side.
