## Task

- **Why:** on 2026-11-15 the design doc got 20 pictures, each next to the part that defines it. In the "Art Inspiration" tab they sit under The bots, The stage, Costumes, and Props and backdrops. In the "Stories" tab a props strip sits under each story's "Props:" line. The in-engine shots (the house view, the five bots and the stage close-ups) were taken before T45, T47 and T48, so they show the old castle backdrop, the old shared prop table and bare-headed bots. I asked for a re-shoot once those three land (2026-11-15). That is my OK for this doc edit.
- **Steps:**
  1. **Use an editor nobody else drives.** If another session drives the PC's editor, open a second one from your own worktree with `-ModelContextProtocolPort=8011 -NoLiveCoding`. Then post MCP calls straight to `http://127.0.0.1:8011/mcp`, not through `unreal-mcp`, which reaches the other editor. Run editor Python by typing `py "<file>"` into the console box (the textbox after `text "Cmd"` in a `SlateInspectorToolset.Snapshot`). Write `Agents/status/task-T74.md` first, because other sessions restart the editor by killing every `UnrealEditor.exe`. Save nothing. At the end, close only your own editor, by its PID.
  2. **Dress the stage for each card in the editor, unsaved.** For each of the seven cards in `scenes.json`, call these on the level's actors through editor Python:
     - the card's backdrop (`BP_Backdrop.ShowBackdrop`);
     - the card's props on the prop table (the call T45 added to `BP_StageDirector`);
     - one role's headpiece and color from that card on each robot (`BP_Robot.SetHeadpiece`, from T47).

     Hide each robot's `Label` text, which reads "ROBOT" in the editor. If one of these calls can't run outside PIE, play a free autoplay show in PIE instead, and capture a scene turn of each card that comes up.
  3. **Capture with `EditorAppToolset.CaptureViewport`** and its `captureTransform`. `HighResShot` stops firing once the editor's window is in the background. The viewport isn't 16:9, so widen the FOV (`LevelEditorSubsystem.set_level_viewport_fov`) and crop the center to the frame you want. Take two kinds of shot:
     - the house view of each card: camera at (-1050, 0, 330), pitch -20, FOV 90;
     - the five bots front on and in costume, for one card, in seat order (OpenAI, Anthropic, SpaceXAI, Meta, You), each labeled with its provider.
  4. **Replace the pictures in the doc** with the Claude Docs connector:
     - Art Inspiration: the house shot under The stage and the cast under The bots. Rewrite their captions: the lines about the old backdrop and the old prop table go.
     - Stories: each story's house shot, under its "Backdrop (…)" line.

     Upload PNGs to the doc's artifact, not JPEGs: the docs `picture` read shows only PNG and GIF, so a JPEG can't be checked after upload. Create a blob for each upload, then replace the old picture's block. Keep every other word in the doc. The prop and headpiece renders stay unless T45 or T47 changed a model.
- **Done when:** the doc's stage and cast pictures show the painted backdrop, the card's props and the bots in costume; every story in the Stories tab has its house shot; you looked at each picture in the doc; and no asset was saved.
