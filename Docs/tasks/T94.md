## Findings

- **My person's design calls in this task:**
  - OpenAI has no halo and no floor ring (round 4).
  - The audience bots have no antennas. They have smooth dome heads with cream ear pods, and most wear top hats or bowler hats (rounds 4 and 5).

  The style guide carries both. The bots and stage sections of `ArtInspiration.md` only point at them in their "being redrawn" lines, because T95 and T98 own those sections. T95 and T98 should bring the text in line.
- **The audience's hats depend on the headpieces.** `BP_AudienceBot` wears `SM_TopHat`, `SM_Beanie` and `SM_Fedora` from `Stage/Headpieces/`, which T104 deletes. T104 or T106 must keep or replace them. This is in `Docs/Art/README.md`, section 2.
- **Hidden leftovers the import tasks may delete:** `SM_MetaHead` (imported, unused), T19's eight shape props inside `BP_Prop`, T17's cardboard cutouts inside `BP_Backdrop`, and the materials that only they use. Material users were grouped by name, not checked. The `.uasset` files weren't opened. Check the Reference Viewer before deleting anything.
- **The game today versus the design doc:** the name tags are still T11's 3D TextRender, not the seat-colored pill (T34 is open). The Cut's trapdoor and dust puff were never built: the Cut is a gray-out, a slump and a tip-over.
- **Leonardo, from this task's 9 calls** (more detail under "Lessons" in `Docs/Art/Direction/README.md`):
  - A text prompt can't hold five set characters. All 12 board takes mixed up the cast.
  - An edit with a reference of another shape returns that shape: the 3500 × 1152 cast sheet gave a 1792 × 576 output.
  - Collage, then repaint, works.
  - Edits drop things they aren't told to keep, and fail to move things.
  - Each pass softens the image a little.
  - `lucid-origin` returned 1920 × 1072 for 1920 × 1080, at 66 credits per four. An edited image costs 26 credits. The replies' `apiCreditCost` is empty: measure the balance with `GET /v1/me` (`apiPaidTokens`).
  - Leonardo serves JPEG data under `.png` names, so convert with `sips -s format png`.
- **Running `art_gen.py` from a worktree:** the repo folder's copy finds `Local/`, so the worktree's copy can't run. Its logs give paths relative to the repo folder, so they began `.claude/worktrees/<name>/`. That prefix was removed from the committed `edit.json` files.
- **Not checked:**
  - The palette's world colors are albedo targets, chosen to match the board and the design doc. They were not measured, because the board's pixels are lit, and much darker.
  - "A bot is about 125 px tall at 1600 × 900" was measured on the design doc's house shot at 1920 × 1080 and scaled, not in the game.
  - My person's memory note `edit-picked-images`, which step 1 names, isn't on this Mac. Its rule (change a picked image with `edit`, never by redrawing from text) is in `Docs/BotArt.md` and was followed.
