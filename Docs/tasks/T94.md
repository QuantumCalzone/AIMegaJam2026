## Task

- **Why:** my request on 2026-11-15: replace all the art in the game. All of it goes back to concept art, and nothing is generated as a model or imported until I approve its concept. Generation then uses the Meshy API or the Hyper3D API. On 2026-11-16 I chose the scope: **everything**. That means the cast and their costumes, the story props, the theater with its set pieces and audience, the painted backdrops, and the UI art (the stagehand's portrait, and the burning bills with their provider badges). This task sets the one direction that all of that follows. The tasks after it draw each group in that direction.
- **The new art tasks, in order:**
  - T94: the direction
  - concepts, each approved by me: T95 the cast, T96 the costumes, T97 the props, T98 the theater
  - T99: the 2D art, generated and imported
  - T103 to T106: the 3D models, generated from the approved concepts and imported

  Each group's old assets stay in the game until its import task replaces them, so the game stays playable throughout.
- **Steps:**
  1. **Read first:**
     - the design doc's Art Inspiration tab (in `Docs/Design/` since T90): style, the bots, costumes, the stage, the UI
     - `Docs/Assets.md`
     - `Docs/BotArt.md`, to see how the last cast was chosen in concept rounds
     - `Tools/art_gen.py`'s docstring
     - my memory note `edit-picked-images`: change a picked image with `art_gen.py edit`, never by drawing it again from text

     Don't open the old models or images in the editor. Look at their thumbnails in `Docs/` instead.
  2. **The list.** Write `Docs/Art/README.md` with every art asset the game shows. Group them as T95 to T99 split them. For each asset, give its path in `Content/`, the files it came from and the task that replaces it. Check the list against `Content/NHOS/Stage/`, `Content/NHOS/UI/` and `Docs/Assets.md`, so nothing is missed. The engine-shape parts count too: the audience bots, the theater and the face parts on the robots.
  3. **Direction boards.** Make three direction boards, each a single image of the same scene: the five robots on stage, my cardboard-box robot among them, a prop or two, the theater around them, and a scrap of the UI. One board keeps the current clay opera-house look, done better. The other two are new directions that still fit the game: a cast of AI robots putting on an improv show, with me hiding among them in a box costume. Use `art_gen.py image` (Leonardo). Before the first paid call, tell me the estimated cost of this task's generations and ask once. Ask again only if the cost would go over that.
  4. **My pick.** Show me the boards (send the images, or a private Artifact page with all three). Ask me with one question which direction to take. Then refine the picked board with `art_gen.py edit`, round by round, until I approve it. Write down each round, my words and the generation ids in `Docs/Art/Direction/README.md`. Keep the picked board and its rounds in `Docs/Art/Direction/`.
  5. **The style guide.** In `Docs/Art/Direction/README.md`, write the rules that the later tasks follow:
     - the palette (with the seat colors, which the UI uses too: check `UIStyle`)
     - the materials and surfaces
     - how much detail, at the house camera's distance
     - the shapes: round versus square, and proportions
     - the lighting it assumes (Lumen, with no baked light)
     - what to avoid
     - the prompt text that every later generation starts from

     The rules must suit image-to-3D in Meshy and Hyper3D. Prefer simple, readable forms with clean silhouettes, because fine detail turns to mush in a model.
  6. **The design doc.** Show me the edit to the Art Inspiration tab: the new direction, the style guide's main rules, and the approved board in place of the old mood images. Make it after my OK, in its file in `Docs/Design/`. Leave the sections on the bots, costumes, stage and UI to their tasks (T95 to T99), but add a line to each saying that the section is being redrawn.
