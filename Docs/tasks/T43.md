# T43 Import the generated models and make the clay material

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task's title line, **Model and effort**, **Depends on**, **Needs**, **Touches** and any **Waiting:** or **Blocked:** line are in its entry there, under "Open".

## Task

- **Why:** T40 to T42 leave FBX files in `Docs/PropArt/`. This task makes them assets the stage can use, and makes the one clay material that colors the headpieces and anything built from engine shapes. It places nothing in the game: the tasks that hook the art in (see "Not planned yet" in the plan) use these assets.
- **Steps:** follow "Import and look" and "Test result" in [Docs/PropArt.md](../PropArt.md). Unreal MCP calls run one at a time; split the work into short calls.
  1. **Import each model** in the manifest that has an `fbx` and no asset yet, with `StaticMeshTools.import_file`:
     - props into `/Game/NHOS/Stage/Props/<card id>/`, headpieces into `/Game/NHOS/Stage/Headpieces/`, set pieces into `/Game/NHOS/Stage/Set/`, each as `SM_<Name>`.
     - Props and set pieces with materials and textures; rename `Material_001` and `texture_0` to `M_<Name>` and `T_<Name>_BaseColor` straight after each import, or the next one collides.
     - Headpieces without materials.
     - Move each `<name>.fbm/` folder the importer leaves beside an FBX into `Local/art/props/<id>/`.
  2. **Record the fit** in the manifest for each model: its bounds, the scale that gives its `size_cm` (Meshy scales everything to about 1.9 m), and the yaw that turns its face to the house (the horse head needed 90).
  3. **Make `M_Clay`** in `/Game/NHOS/Stage/Materials/`: Substrate, matte (roughness about 0.85), a `Color` parameter and a faint noise in the normal for thumbprints. Put an instance in a neutral color on every headpiece.
  4. **Look at them:** place every model in a row on the stage as temporary actors at its recorded scale and yaw, take a screenshot from the house camera and one close up, then delete the actors. Don't save `L_Stage`.
  5. Save the new assets. They are all new, so nothing needs a lock.
  6. In `Docs/Submission.md`, add Leonardo.ai and Meshy to "Tools you used", and rewrite the lines that say no AI image or 3D tools were used and that there is no AI-generated art.
- **Done when:** every generated model is an asset with the right names, the manifest has the scale and yaw of each, `M_Clay` exists, and the two screenshots show them all at a sensible size.
