# T126 The web, part 4: fewer triangles on the stage

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task's title line, **Model and effort**, **Depends on**, **Needs**, **Touches** and any **Waiting:** or **Blocked:** line are in its entry there, under "Open".

## Task

- **Why:** T112 measured 2.9 M triangles a frame on the mobile renderer in a scene turn (`stat rhi`), from 1,412 mesh parts and 3.5 M triangles at full detail in `L_Stage`. None of the 79 meshes has a lower LOD, because the Hyper3D models came in at up to 15,000 triangles each. The audience alone is 1.5 M: 49 bots, 54 seats and 49 hats, seen at about 40 px. Phones and laptops in a browser want well under 1 M.
- **Steps:**
  1. **Read first.** `Docs/Mobile/README.md` ("Draw calls and triangles", "Triangles"), T106's findings (the audience and the set), T103's and T105's (the cast and the props), and T123's frame rate in a browser.
  2. **Measure** the triangles and draws a frame with `stat rhi` in the mobile preview (T112.md's "Ways of working") in a scene turn, and on desktop.
  3. **Reduce.** Pick, with numbers: lower LODs from the engine's mesh reduction for the 79 meshes (for example LOD 1 at 25% of the triangles, switched by screen size), or one light audience bot, seat and hat (2,000 to 3,000 triangles each) for the house, or both. Keep each mesh's silhouette and textures; nothing new is generated.
  4. **Check it.** Under 1 M triangles a frame in the mobile preview's scene turn. Sheets from the house camera at 1600 × 900, before and after, on desktop and on mobile: no change a player would see. A free autoplay show reaches `NHOS: SHOW END`.
- **Done when:** the mobile preview shows under 1 M triangles a frame in a scene turn; the before and after sheets show no visible change; a free autoplay show reaches `NHOS: SHOW END`.
