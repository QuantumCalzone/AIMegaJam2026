## Findings

- **Import (open questions 1 and 8):** Interchange brings a GLB in at ×100 with the model's width on mesh X, its depth on Y (its front on +Y) and its height on Z, as T103 found. Yaw 90 faces the house, yaw 270 the stage; actor scale is (width, depth, height). A check against the chandelier's candle tips read 0.03 cm as expected against 3.5 and 1.6 for the mirrored axes. The importer already sets each normal map to `TC_NORMALMAP`, sRGB off and **flip green on**, so glTF's OpenGL normals come in right with no change. Every GLB is one mesh, one material.
- **The palette shift (open question 7)** reads on every piece from the house camera, the box too: dark wood fronts, bright gold mouldings, crimson drapes. The capitals keep a faint green in their deepest scrolls, which reads as old gilt. No fallback needed. It is a `Custom` HLSL node in sRGB space with a Gaussian falloff (width 0.25) per key; the keys and targets are sRGB values in the vector parameters `KeyA`..`KeyD` and `ToA`..`ToD`.
- **The old pieces' bounds (open question 3):** the old chandelier's bottom was z 321 (the new one hangs from the same bottom, pivot z 421); the prop table's top z 82 (the new table's top is at 82, height scale 0.989); the cherub plinths and footlight hoods stood on the stage floor (z 0); the pleats were x −228 to −182, y ±444 to ±623; the valance hung at z 480 to 570, above the frame's top, so it never showed. The stage floor's front edge is x −260 and the house floor's top z −110, as recorded.
- **The camera** is at (−1050, 0, 330), pitch −20, 90° (T17's numbers in the build sheet were older). The frame's top at the proscenium (x −280) is about z 456 at 1600 × 900 and z 494 at 1440 × 900.
