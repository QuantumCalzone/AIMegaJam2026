## Task

- **Why:** the two seats in [Docs/BotArt.md](../BotArt.md) whose heads differ from T79's shared round head. Meta is a doll-like android, and my robot is a person in a homemade cardboard costume: four polished bots and one human pretending is the game's premise in one picture.
- **Steps:**
  1. **`copycat` (Meta), [meta.png](../BotArt/meta.png):** a doll-like android. A pale gold-white round head with small ears and a short nose, glowing yellow-gold eyes behind thick black wayfarer-style glasses with a tiny camera dot in each outer corner, and a small stiff smile. Jointed pale gold-white arms and hands, a royal blue tee and pants (the seat color), cream shoes and a chunky gold chain. The headset topper goes. The eyes and mouth must be able to change for T75's emotions: the eyes behind the glasses, the mouth below them.
  2. **My robot (the human seat), [you.png](../BotArt/you.png):** a person in a homemade robot costume. A teal-painted cardboard box over the head with two eye slots and eyes behind them that can move and blink (for T75), a smile drawn on the box, and a ping-pong ball on a wire for the antenna, in place of the bent antenna. The red and cream knitted scarf stays. A bigger teal box worn as the body, with strips of duct tape and bottle-cap buttons; striped sleeves, garden gloves, jeans and sneakers. Next to the four polished bots it should read as a human in a costume.
  3. **Sockets and the headpiece fit.** Each of the two gets T79's sockets. Place and scale its `HeadSocket` so T47's headpieces, sized for the shared round head, sit right on it. Write each socket's transform under "## Findings" for T47. Try the top hat, the bob wig and the dark shades on both, placed by hand in the editor without saving, as `Docs/BotArt.md`'s costume check did in 2D. The shades hide Meta's own glasses, and a wig on the box may stick out at the corners, which can pass as part of the costume.
  4. **Keep every motion** and the height (about 1.6 m). Re-place the robots in `L_Stage` if template changes don't reach them. Engine shapes first; a Meshy model only for a part they can't make, recorded in `Docs/Assets.md`. No hair, no wires and no brand marks.
  5. **Check:** a house-camera screenshot of all five robots side by side, next to [cast.png](../BotArt/cast.png); the three headpieces on both new heads; a free autoplay show in a game window reaches `NHOS: SHOW END` with no `LogScript` line.
- **Done when:** the check passes, and Claude judges from the screenshots, as a critical first-time player would, that the five read as the cast sheet from the house camera and that my robot reads as a person in a costume.
