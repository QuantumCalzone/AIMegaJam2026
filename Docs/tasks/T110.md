# T110 Each scene's props come in from the wing, and the last scene's props go out

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task's title line, **Model and effort**, **Depends on**, **Needs**, **Touches** and any **Waiting:** or **Blocked:** line are in its entry there, under "Open".

## Task

- **Why:** my ask on 2026-11-18: the props on stage should be only the props of the scene they belong to. At each scene change, the old props go away and the new ones come in, so I see the stage change hands. My pick for how: through the wing beside the Prop Table, like a crew swapping props between scenes.
- **How it works now (T45, T62):** the rules already use only the card in play's props. At each Scene Prep, `BP_Show.StagePrep` calls `BP_StageDirector.ResetStage`, then `SetScene` with the card's props, and the state's `set.props`, the action builder's chips (`ItemsFor`) and free mode's `{prop}` all read that list. But on stage, `L_Stage` has four `BP_Prop` actors that stay all show. `ResetStage` sends every robot home, swaps each actor's model in place with `BP_Prop.SetProp` (in a robot's hand or wherever it lies), and flies it back to the table with `GoTable` (0.9 s). So the last scene's props turn into the new scene's props where they are, and nothing leaves or arrives.
- **What it should look like:**
  - At each Scene Prep from scene 2 on, the last card's props leave first: each one flies off the table (or out of a robot's hand, or from wherever it was thrown) into the wing beside the Prop Table, out of the camera's frame.
  - Then the new card's props fly in from that wing, one after another, and land in their places on the table.
  - No prop changes its model where I can see it. A model changes only while its actor is out of the frame.
  - Before scene 1, the table is empty. Scene 1's props come in from the wing, and nothing leaves.
  - The instant restart after a loss works the same way: the last scene's props go out, and scene 1's props come in.
- **What must keep working:**
  - The rules' state for each scene (`SetScene`): nobody holds a prop, and only the card's props can be grabbed, thrown, given or looked at.
  - A grab, hold, throw and give in the first turn of every scene. A robot must never walk to the table for a prop that hasn't landed.
  - Autoplay shows, where Scene Prep may be short.
  - T45's places on the table, scales and grips from `props.json`.
