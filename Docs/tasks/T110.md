## Findings

- **What it does now.** `BP_StageDirector.ResetStage` (called by `BP_Show.StagePrep` at every Scene Prep and at the restart) still sends every robot home first and still runs before `SetScene`, but instead of renaming each prop in place and flying it home (`SetProp` + `GoTable`), it calls the new `BP_Prop.SwapTo` on each prop with the card's name ("" past the card's list), the wing point and a start time. Each prop flies out to the wing at once (0.6 s, arc 80) from the table, a hand or wherever it lies; takes its new model there (`SetProp`, in `TickSwap`); and flies in to its own place on the table (`HomeLoc`, 0.6 s, arc 60) at 0.9 s + 0.25 s × its index. So the table is empty for about 0.3 s, the props land one after another at about 1.5, 1.75, 2.0 and 2.25 s, and the whole swap takes about 2.25 s. A prop with no name in the new card stays in the wing. `NHOS: ACT reset … swap=wing` marks the start and `NHOS: ACT prop in <name>` each landing.
- **The table starts empty.** The director's `BeginPlay` sends every prop to the wing (`BP_Prop.ToWing`). `BP_Prop.InitHome` reads the prop's place on the table once, whichever `BeginPlay` runs first, so `HomeLoc` stays the table place from `L_Stage` (T45's places, scales and grips are unchanged). Scene 1's props, and the restart's, skip the fly out and come in at the same times.
- **The wing point** is one variable, `BP_StageDirector.WingLoc`, default (300, 710, 90): straight behind the Prop Table at (300, 300), in the right wing between the right side curtain and `WingWallR` (T106's notes). From the house camera it is hidden behind the right side curtain (and the card on the right), so no model changes in view: in the shots the old props fly off to the right and vanish behind the curtain's edge, and nothing shows at the wing while the props wait there (`run1b/r2_03`, `r2_04`). The path from the table runs along +y behind every robot's place; no prop passed through a robot or a set piece in the shots. `L_Stage` is unchanged.
- **Names change at once, models in the wing.** `SwapTo` sets `PropName` and `Wear` (the new `WearFor`, from `props.json`) at the start, so the rules (`FindProp`) and a grab during the swap find the new card's prop by name and pick the right socket. Only the model waits for the wing.
- **No robot gets a prop that hasn't landed, without a change to `BP_Show`.** In this game a grab doesn't walk to the table: the prop flies to the robot (`BP_Robot.DoGrab` → `Receive` → `BP_Prop.FlyToComp`). In autoplay, and in fast shows, the scene's first actions come 0.3 to 1.2 s after Scene Prep opens, before the props land. So while a prop swaps (`Swap` 1 to 3), `FlyToComp` holds the grab: the prop counts as held at once (`Holder`, `Slot`, so the robot and the rules agree), logs `NHOS: ACT prop waits <name>`, and flies to the hand when it has landed (`RunPend`). A throw that comes during the swap (`ThrowTo`) waits too, and plays after the grab reaches the hand (`Swap` 4). A `GoTable` during the swap (the scene's end tidying props back) drops what waits, since the prop is on its way to the table anyway. At a normal pace (`fast_lines` off) the first action played 12 s or more after Scene Prep, well after the props landed.
