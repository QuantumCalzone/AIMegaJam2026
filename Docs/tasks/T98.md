## Findings

The full log, the piece table and the lessons are in [Docs/Art/Set/README.md](../Art/Set/README.md). In short:

- **The house concepts started from the real frame.** The house camera's frame must stay, so `Docs/Art/Set/house/collage.swift` pasted the old cast sheet's robots onto the design doc's 2026-11-15 house-camera capture, and the edit model repainted it. A plain repaint kept the old render (round 1, 156 credits, not shown). With T94's board as a same-shape style reference it turned to clay, but two of three takes also took the board's low camera (round 1b). Restyling the one frame-true take gave three concepts in the frame (round 1c).
- **Edits fixed colors, not shapes or places.** Rounds 2 and 3 (the Riser in place, the dark audience heads, the red blocks the cast sheet's curtain left behind each robot) failed, and the red blocks' outlines came back as wire frames. Both artifacts were removed by hand with small Swift scripts in `Docs/Art/Set/house/` (free). Round 4 (darker walls, brighter gold) worked first time.
- **The house view leaves some things to the pieces:** the robots and backdrop are placeholders (T95, T99), the Riser doesn't show, the cherubs, chandelier and audience changed in the piece round, and the front rows are drawn bigger than T86's real rows, which T106 keeps. The README lists them.
- **Pieces as sheets.** Four objects on one 1920 × 1080 `lucid-origin` image cost 16.5 credits per take, and `sips -c` cut them apart. The chandelier came out as a standing candelabra until the prompt said "hanging" and the negative said "standing candelabra".
- **The swatches are drawn by a script** (`Docs/Art/Set/swatches/swatches.swift`) from the style guide's albedos, with my person's brighter gold (#D9B24E in place of #C9A04A) and the dark wood for the walls they asked to be darker.
- **The shared Leonardo balance ran dry mid-task** (120 credits left after T95 and T97's spending). My person said to spend what was left, then topped it up. The balance is shared, so each cost was counted per generation, checked against the account's generation list.
- **For T106:** the audience gets its own top hat and bowler here, so T104 can delete `Stage/Headpieces/` (the dependency in `Docs/Art/README.md`). Glows, screens, chains, ropes and fringes are engine parts or are left out, as the style guide says.
- **Not checked:** the 1440 × 900 frame (only 16:9 was drawn); T106 checks it in the level.
