# T61 Stagehand onboarding: earpiece tips, a tips toggle and How to play

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task's title line, **Model and effort**, **Depends on**, **Needs**, **Touches** and any **Waiting:** or **Blocked:** line are in its entry there, under "Open".

## Task

- **Why:** design change 12 of 12 (2026-11-15). A human stagehand in my earpiece teaches each rule once, at the moment it first matters, on the first show only. The tips appear only to me and never enter the shared log. A settings toggle brings them back, and a How to play button shows the full rules. Judges play one or two shows, so the first show has to teach the game. The lines and their moments are in the design doc's Rules tab ("Onboarding": the table "What the player sees, and when", and "Supporting pieces").
- **Steps:** this task is larger than most. If it runs long, finish steps 1 to 4, push them, and tell me, so that steps 5 and 6 become a task of their own.
  1. **`Content/NHOS/Data/stagehand.json`:** one entry per moment, with the earpiece line from the doc's table, word for word: before the show, Scene Prep, scene turn 1, Free Talk, The Cut, the lose screen and the win screen. Add the first-show style tips from "Supporting pieces", one idea per line across the first scene (plain sentences, no caps or emoji, 15 words at most, don't go silent, don't over-polish), and the three opening lines shown before the show.
  2. **A new component, `BPC_Stagehand`,** owns the tips: it loads the file, knows which moments it has already shown in this show, and shows a line on a new widget, `WBP_Earpiece`. `BP_Show` calls it from one node per moment, for example `Stagehand.Say("scene_prep")`. The lines are static text. They never go to an LLM, the shared log or `game_messages`.
  3. **`WBP_Earpiece`:** the stagehand's line in its own color, on its own channel on the in-show screen, clearly not chat and not the banner. It holds long enough to read, then fades. Follow T32's style Blueprint, and keep it clear of the card, the clapperboard and the input row.
  4. **First show only:** the tips show until the first show ends. Save that in a small file in `Local/` (for example `Local/progress.json`), which the key screen already shows can be written in a packaged build. A missing file means a first show. Later shows keep the scene card, the clapperboard and the "passed" markers, and show no tips. With autoplay on, tips are off unless a switch asks for them.
  5. **The tips toggle:** the game has no settings screen, so put a "Stagehand tips" toggle on the title screen (`WBP_Title`). On brings the tips back for the next show.
  6. **How to play:** a button on the title screen opens `WBP_HowToPlay`, the full rules in six bullets, with a Close button. It is never forced. Write the six bullets from the Rules tab and show them to me before the commit.
  7. **`Tools/check_prompts.py --offline`** checks `stagehand.json`: every moment has a line, and no line is over 25 words. `Docs/ItchPage.md` carries the same three opening lines.
  8. **Check:** a first free show in PIE (no `Local/progress.json`) shows each earpiece line once at its moment (screenshots of Scene Prep, turn 1, Free Talk and The Cut), prints `NHOS: TIP <moment>` for each, and no tip text is in any state sent to an AI (search the log's request bodies). A second show prints no `NHOS: TIP`. With the toggle on, the tips are back. The How to play screen opens and closes from the title.
- **Done when:** the check passes, and I play a first show and then a second one, and say that the tips taught me the rules without getting in the way.
