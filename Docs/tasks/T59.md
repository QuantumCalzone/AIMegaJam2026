- **Steps:**
  1. **`prompts.json`:** `input`: "Cap speech at 15 words by dropping words from the end." `game`: the two lines that say the game cuts at 25 words ("Reading the state" and the `speech` field under "How to reply") say 15.
  2. **`BP_Show`:** `CapWords` cuts speech and reasons to 15 words. It still counts a Player name as one word (T13). If the limit is a literal in the graph, make it one variable.
  3. **`llm.json` `_readme`, `free_mode.json`** (`_readme`, and the `echo_typed` cut of my typed text) **and `BPC_LLMClient.FreeReply`,** if it holds the 25. Check `Relay/` for the same cut in its free mode; a change there needs a deploy, so ask me first.
  4. **`Tools/check_prompts.py`:** `MAX_WORDS = 15`, and the comments and messages that say 25.
  5. **`Docs/ItchPage.md`:** "Every line is capped at 15 words."
  6. **The input row,** if its hint names a number. A live word count by the input box would help me stay under the cap; it is not part of this task, so tell me if you think it is needed.
  7. **Check:** `--offline` and `--relay --free` pass. In a free show, a typed line of 20 words reaches the log with 15 (PIE, Test for Free, autoplay off, one turn by hand through the Slate inspector), and a free autoplay show reaches `NHOS: SHOW END`.

### Unreal MCP rules found in this task

- **`set_pin_value` doesn't mark the package dirty,** and `AssetTools.save_assets` then skips it but still returns true. Check `AssetTools.is_dirty` after a pin edit; moving a node with `set_node_position` (it takes `pos`, not `position`) dirties it. `write_graph_dsl` dirties the package on its own.
- `BlueprintTools.add_variable` takes `blueprint`, `name` and `type_name` (`int`). Set its default with `ObjectTools.set_properties` on the Blueprint (`{"MaxWords": 15}`), then compile.
- `ProgrammaticToolset.execute_tool_script` has no `call_tool` function; I made the calls one by one instead.
