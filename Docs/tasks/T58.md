# T58 Last word: a reply for anyone @-mentioned in the final round

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task's title line, **Model and effort**, **Depends on**, **Needs**, **Touches** and any **Waiting:** or **Blocked:** line are in its entry there, under "Open".

## Task

- **Why:** design change 9 of 12 (2026-11-15). Anyone who is @-mentioned in the final Free Talk round gets one reply before the vote. Today an actor accused in the last round goes to the vote with no answer. The rule is in the design doc's Rules tab ("Free Talk", Last word).
- **Steps:**
  1. **Who gets a last word:** every actor on stage whose Player name follows an @ in a line of the final round, by someone else. One reply each, in a random order. A mention inside a last word gives no further last word.
  2. **`BP_Show`:** after the final round's last speaker (`NextTalker`, before `EndFreeTalk`), run the last words one speaker at a time, like a Free Talk round, through the line queue. Put the new logic in its own functions, for example `StartLastWords` and `PlayLastWord`. Each reply goes in the shared log with `when` "scene n, last word". With nobody mentioned, the step is skipped. Print `NHOS: LASTWORD <id> scene=<n>` for each, and `NHOS: LASTWORDS DONE scene=<n> entries=<n>`.
  3. **My last word:** if I was mentioned, the input row asks me for one line (`ComputePrompt`: "You were named. One last line before the vote."), with Pass on. It goes through the input LLM as a Free Talk line. With autoplay on, it uses a new `last_word` list in `autoplay.json`.
  4. **`prompts.json`:** a new ask, `asks.last_word`: answer the actor who named you, one line, no actions, a pass allowed. `game`'s "How a show works" names the last word. Update `_readme` (`when` values).
  5. **`free_mode.json`:** a `last_word` phase, placed before `free_talk` in the match order, with a few lines. The relay bundles this file, so ask me before you deploy it again.
  6. **`Tools/check_prompts.py`:** `last_word` joins `ACTOR_ASKS`, with a sample state.
  7. **Check:** `--offline` and `--relay --free` pass. Give a few `free_talk` lines in `free_mode.json` an @ mention (`@{actor}`), so free shows have mentions in the final round. In a free autoplay show, every actor mentioned in the final round gets one last word before the vote (`NHOS: TALLY`), and a scene whose final round has no mention skips the step. My canned final-round line stays "pass", because T57's check uses it.
- **Done when:** the check passes, and Claude plays one show by hand, gets named in the final round and gets its reply before the vote.
