Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task's title line, **Model and effort**, **Depends on**, **Needs**, **Touches** and any **Waiting:** or **Blocked:** line are in its entry there, under "Open".

## Task

- **Why:** my request on 2026-11-15: replace all the audio, and above all the music, with my approval. The music must match the scenes, be quieter, and sit more in the background. Today there are 13 Kenney loops (T20, T71):
  - the title and the show
  - one for each of the seven cards: `dino_island`, `favor_father`, `glowing_briefcase`, `league_night`, `mine_bridge`, `pick_a_pill` and `space_plans`
  - Free Talk and The Cut
  - a win and a loss

  This task finds new music for each slot and gets my pick. T102 imports the picks and sets the mix.
- **Claude can't hear.** I am the only judge of how a track sounds. Claude finds candidates from what their sources say about them (genre, instruments, tempo, mood, whether there are vocals), puts them in front of me, and records my picks.
- **What every track must be:**
  - **It fits its slot.** A card's track fits its film-parody story and its place. It may suggest a film's genre, but it never quotes or imitates a real film's theme or score.
  - **Background music.** It sits under the talk. It is steady and sparse, with no vocals and no lead melody that competes with the lines. Its dynamics are soft, with no sudden swells or drops.
  - **It loops cleanly.**
  - **We may use it.** Its license allows use in a free game that we may sell later. Record the license and any credit line it needs.
- **Steps:**
  1. **Read first:**
     - "Music for each place and each scene (T71)" in `Docs/Assets.md`
     - `Docs/AudioShortlist.md` (how T20 chose)
     - `Docs/tasks/T71.md`: the slots, how `BPC_ShowAudio` picks a track (`TrackFor`, `PhaseKey`), the 40% duck under the drum roll, and the 1 s cross-fade
     - the seven cards in `Content/NHOS/Data/scenes.json`, for each story and its place
  2. **Two questions for me, one at a time:**
     - **The sources:** free licensed libraries (CC0 or CC BY, such as Kenney, OpenGameArt, Freesound and Incompetech), an AI music service that I sign up for, or both. An AI service needs my OK on its terms and its cost, and its key goes in `Local/` like the others. Claude never sees the key.
     - **What "more background" means to me:** music that sits under the talk, a theater ambience bed under the music as well (room tone and a quiet audience), or both. If it includes the bed, add a slot for it.
  3. **Candidates.** Find three candidates for each slot. Download them to `Local/audio/candidates/<slot>/` (git ignores `Local/`), each with a note: where it came from, its license, its length and the reason it fits. Cut each one to a 30 to 60 second preview at an even loudness, so I compare the music, not the volume.
  4. **Let me listen.** Put the previews in a private Artifact page with a player for each, grouped by slot, with each one's note under it. Load the `artifact-capabilities` skill to upload the audio. Ask me one slot at a time. When I reject all three in a slot, ask what to change and bring three more.
  5. **Record the picks.** Copy each picked track's full file to `SourceAssets/Audio/Music/` (in Git LFS), with a clear name. Write `Docs/AudioShortlist.md`'s music section again: each slot, its pick, the source, the license, the credit line and my words. Write the credit lines that `Docs/ItchPage.md` and the game's credits need.
- **Done when:**
  1. Every slot (the 13, and the ambience bed if I chose one) has a track that I picked.
