# T47 A headpiece for each role

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task's title line, **Model and effort**, **Depends on**, **Needs**, **Touches** and any **Waiting:** or **Blocked:** line are in its entry there, under "Open".

## Task

- **Why:** each role wears one of 13 clay headpieces, in its own color (the design doc's "Art Inspiration" tab, Costumes, and the "Stories" tab for each role's piece and color). T40 and T43 made the models. T79 and T80 build the heads they sit on (the new cast in [Docs/BotArt.md](../BotArt.md), which replaces T46's head): one round head shared by OpenAI, Anthropic and SpaceXAI, plus Meta's doll head and my cardboard box, each with a `HeadSocket`.
- **Steps:**
  1. **Data:** give each seat in `scenes.json` a `headpiece` (one of the 13 ids) and a `color` (hex), from the Stories tab. Add the 13 headpieces to `props.json` (or a new `costumes.json`) with their mesh, scale and offset on the head. `check_prompts.py` checks that every seat names a known headpiece. The game never sends these fields to the AIs.
  2. **`BP_Robot`:** a static mesh component on the robot's `HeadSocket` and `SetHeadpiece(Id, Color)`, which loads the mesh, sets a `M_Clay` instance's `Color`, and leaves the topper poking up beside it (OpenAI's halo, Anthropic's antenna light, SpaceXAI's bolt, my ping-pong-ball antenna). An empty id takes it off. One scale and offset per headpiece is set for the shared round head; T80 sized Meta's and my sockets so the same values fit there (its findings have the transforms). The `dark_shades` hide Meta's and Anthropic's own glasses while they are on. `Docs/BotArt.md`'s costume check shows the top hat, the bob wig and the shades on all five.
  3. **`BP_Show`:** at Scene Prep each robot first stands bare-headed, then puts on its seat's headpiece when the scene starts. A cut robot keeps its headpiece as it slumps.
  4. **Check:** screenshots of all five bots in costume in two different scenes; a free autoplay show reaches `NHOS: SHOW END`.
- **Done when:** the check passes, and Claude checks from screenshots of one free show in a game window that the costumes read.
