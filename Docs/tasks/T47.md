# T47 A headpiece for each role

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task's title line, **Model and effort**, **Depends on**, **Needs**, **Touches** and any **Waiting:** or **Blocked:** line are in its entry there, under "Open".

## Task

- **Why:** each role wears one of 13 clay headpieces, in its own color (the design doc's "Art Inspiration" tab, Costumes, and the "Stories" tab for each role's piece and color). T40 and T43 made the models; T46 made the head they sit on.
- **Steps:**
  1. **Data:** give each seat in `scenes.json` a `headpiece` (one of the 13 ids) and a `color` (hex), from the Stories tab. Add the 13 headpieces to `props.json` (or a new `costumes.json`) with their mesh, scale and offset on the head. `check_prompts.py` checks that every seat names a known headpiece. The game never sends these fields to the AIs.
  2. **`BP_Robot`:** a static mesh component on the head and `SetHeadpiece(Id, Color)`, which loads the mesh, sets a `M_Clay` instance's `Color`, and leaves the antenna topper poking up through it. An empty id takes it off.
  3. **`BP_Show`:** at Scene Prep each robot first stands bare-headed, then puts on its seat's headpiece when the scene starts. A cut robot keeps its headpiece as it slumps.
  4. **Check:** screenshots of all five bots in costume in two different scenes; a free autoplay show reaches `NHOS: SHOW END`.
- **Done when:** the check passes, and I say the costumes read in one free show in a game window.
