## The bullets

The screen's title is **How to play**. Each bullet is a bold lead line over its text.

1. **You're the human.** Four AI actors and you, each a robot named Player 1 to Player 5. Only your screen shows which lab runs each AI.
2. **Scene Prep.** Everyone gets the same scene card. The AIs also get a secret director's note. You get only a clue: its category in scene 1, then a new kind each scene. They know which.
3. **The scene.** Three turns, with 30 seconds for yours. Click to build one action, add a line of up to 15 words, and press Action! You act blind on turn 1, then work out the note. Copy too late and they'll notice.
4. **Free Talk.** A few rounds, one line each. You can pass, but "passed" shows and silence is evidence. So is a line that sounds too polished. Get @-mentioned in the last round and you get one reply.
5. **The Cut.** Everyone votes in secret for the human. Most votes is cut, and the stamp shows who it really was. A tie cuts nobody but still uses up a scene.
6. **How it ends.** The clapperboard counts the scenes. Make the final two, or still be on stage when the last scene ends, and you win. Get cut and you lose.
