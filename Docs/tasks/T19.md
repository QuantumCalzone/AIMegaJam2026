## Findings

### T19: robots act out their actions (2026-11-14)

- **How it hooks in.** The stage acts out each line's `does` text, the same text the AIs read in the shared log, so the stage never shows something the log doesn't say. `BP_Show.ActorLine` (the one place an actor's line enters the shared log) now also calls `BP_StageDirector.PlayLine(Seat, Entry, Robots, SeatName, SeatId)`, and `BP_Show.StagePrep` (run by every Scene Prep and by Restart) calls `ResetStage`. Those are the only `BP_Show` changes, plus a `NullDir` variable. Both calls skip quietly when the level has no director.
- **`BP_StageDirector`** (new, one in `L_Stage`): `PlayLine` splits `does` at "; " and reads each part back with the `does_templates` wording: "moves to", "looks at", "looks around", "grabs the", "throws the X at Y", "gives the X to Y", and "votes for" (the voter turns to look at its pick). "stands still" and "passes" play nothing. A target is a place (`BP_PlaceMark.PlaceName`, any case, "Table" also finds "Prop Table") or a Player name (`SeatName`, never the actor itself); a leading "the " or "@" is dropped. An unknown place, prop or target makes the robot look around and prints the reason. Each action prints `NHOS: ACT <id> <verb> <target> <item>`, with `-` for an empty field, for example `NHOS: ACT worrier give Player 1 cardboard sword`. It parses the template wording, so a change to `does_templates` in `prompts.json` needs the same change in `PlayPart`.
- **Places.** Seven `BP_PlaceMark` actors (outliner folder `Stage/Marks`), each with a list of spots. A move takes the first spot that no other robot stands on or is heading to. A spot counts as taken when another robot is within 95 cm sideways and 140 cm in depth, because a robot one step behind another hides it from the camera. A move to a Player name tries six spots around that robot. With no free spot, the robot looks at the place instead (`... look Center - (no free spot)`). Left is the left of the screen. Center, Front and Back are rows across the stage, the Riser puts one robot on top and the rest around it, and the Prop Table spots stand in front of and beside the table. Spots stay inside the curtains: the first Left and Right spots, at y = ±500, were half hidden, so they moved to ±440.
- **Props.** Eight `BP_Prop` actors on the prop table (folder `Stage/Props`), made of engine shapes and `M_Set` instances (five new: `MI_Cardboard`, `MI_PropYellow`, `MI_PropOrange`, `MI_ToyRed`, `MI_Paper`): the cardboard sword stands up at the back, the cape hangs off the table's front edge, and the paper crown, plastic rose, toy ray gun, teacup, rubber chicken and scroll sit on top. `PropName` picks the parts (component tags, as T17's accessories) in the construction script and at BeginPlay. Each prop is scaled 1.0 to 1.6 so it reads from the house camera. A prop flies in an arc (`TickFly`): into a hand, onto a spot, or back to the table. Every Scene Prep sends loose props back to the table; a held prop goes back when its robot walks home.
- **`BP_Robot`** gets an action queue: `MoveTo(Dest)`, `LookAt(Target)`, `LookAround`, `Grab(Prop)`, `Throw(Prop, Target, Hit)`, `Give(Prop, Bot)`, `PutDown(Prop, Target)` and `GoHome`. Each one adds to the queue, and `TickActs` (called from Event Tick) starts the next action when the last one is done. A move hops there in about a second (higher onto the riser). A look turns the robot for 2.5 s, then it turns back to the audience. A throw or give of a prop the robot doesn't hold grabs it first. A thrown prop lands near the target, and a robot it lands at shakes. A grab flies the prop into the right hand, then the left; with both hands full, the oldest prop drops at its feet. The paper crown goes on the head and the cape on the back. When the queue falls behind (free mode answers in 0.3 s), each action plays faster. A cut robot drops what it holds and plays nothing more, but it slides home at the next Scene Prep. The label keeps facing the camera (absolute rotation).
- **Free Talk has no actions to play.** Since T26 its ask says "No actions", and `PlayTalk` logs "stands still". The robots still bounce while they talk (T17).
- **PIE, free mode, played by hand** (I typed my scene turns, passed Free Talk and my reaction, and voted by click; `Local/switches.json` was not changed): two shows, scene 1 through The Cut and into scene 2. Every verb played and printed an `ACT` line, including give-after-grab, a snatch from another robot's hand, throws at Front and Back, the crown on a head, a robot on the riser, votes as looks, a cut robot dropping its teacup, and the reset at scene 2 (robots home, props on the table). - **Unreal MCP rules found in this task:**
  - **Inside a Blueprint, a call to its own function on another instance is `CallFunction|Name :self <other>`.** `Class|BPRobot|IsDown` doesn't exist inside `BP_Robot`.
  - **`(switch string ...)` can't name its cases** ("Unknown exec output move ... Available: Case_0 ..."). Use a chain of `if` with `return`.
  - **`Utilities|IsValid` in an expression becomes the exec macro** and the `if` is lost. For a pure check, compare with an object variable that is never set: `(!= x (V NullBot))` makes `Utilities|NotEqual(Object)`.
  - **Functions made with `add_function_graph` are impure.** Bind each call's result before an `if`, `return` or `select` uses it (as T05 found for `Json|HasField`).
  - **`write_graph_dsl` can't write `UserConstructionScript`.** `create_node` `CallFunction|<Name>` plus `connect_pins` from the entry node's `then` works; it reads back as `(fn ConstructionScript ...)`.
  - **`set_properties` can't shrink an array to new values in one call** ("elements changed alongside the size change"). Set it to `[]` first.
  - **`Utilities|String|Split` binds as `(bind (left right ok) ...)`.**
  - **A new Blueprint's components, added with `add_component`, show on instances placed after them**, so place new actors after their parts exist (T17's re-place rule applies only to instances placed before).
  - **`SlateInspectorToolset.Screenshot` of the editor's main splitter is full resolution** (3384 × 1336); `CaptureEditorImage` comes back at about 1280 wide. `Saved/T19/resize.ps1` crops and scales with `System.Drawing`.
