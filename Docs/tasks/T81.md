### The method (step 3, on Anthropic)

- **(a) The whole bot, then BANG:** `anthropic/whole/` (40,000 triangles, one piece) reads very close to `anthropic.png`. Its BANG split (`anthropic/whole_bang/`, "head, body, left arm, right arm") gave **five** pieces: a good head (with its glasses, ear pods and antenna), a body with gouges and smears where the arms had joined it, both arms in the concept's pose (the raised finger), and a loose fragment of one arm. Their models are not in git (a method test); the thumbnails, turntables and logs are.
- **(b) One part per crop:** clean every time: one closed piece, a flat closed neck and shoulders, a clean shoulder cut on the arm. **(b) was used for every part.**
- What else was learned: a painted-over patch on a reference crop comes back as geometry (a white blob, a box visor, a bib), so a part whose concept is hidden behind something (OpenAI's folded arms, Meta's glasses and chain) is better made from a **text prompt alone**; text-only results kept the cast's clay style. A crop also carries the concept's lighting into the texture: the first head shell had the curtain's red light baked into its lower half, and `texture_delight` with "uniform white" in the prompt removed it. A crop of a gloved hand gave a bare hand twice; text only gave the glove.

### How each part was checked (step 6)

- No 3D tool is installed on the Mac (no Blender), and the results have no preview image, so this task wrote two small tools in its scratchpad: a stdlib Python reader for binary FBX that writes an OBJ and counts triangles, separate pieces and open edges (`mesh_stats.json`), and a Swift SceneKit renderer that draws four turntable views offscreen with the diffuse texture (`turntable.png`; `thumbnail.png` is its front view). The sheet (`Local/art/T81_sheet.png`, not in git) tiles each thumbnail beside its crop or concept image, with a Swift AppKit script as T40 found.
- Agentic models are n-gons with holes: a fan triangulation fills the holes in a preview, so the reader ear-clips each polygon.
- **zsh gotcha:** a download link built as `$D:a…` in zsh is read as the `:a` path modifier when the signature starts with a modifier letter, so two downloads got a 403; write `${D}`.
- Redos: OpenAI 1 (body), Meta 2 (head, body), mine 2 (the arm twice), plus the shared head once. Each bot stayed at two or fewer.
