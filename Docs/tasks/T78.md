# T78 I can pick my robot's emotion

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task's title line, **Model and effort**, **Depends on**, **Needs**, **Touches** and any **Waiting:** or **Blocked:** line are in its entry there, under "Open".

## Task

- **Why:** since T77, the input LLM picks my robot's emotion from my line. My call (2026-11-15): I can also pick it myself, so I can act on purpose (look calm while I lie, or look as confused as the bots do). Nothing extra is asked of me: if I don't pick, T77's automatic pick stays.
- **Steps:**
  1. **An emotion chip in the action builder** (T64's `WBP_ActionBuilder`), styled from `UIStyle` like the other chips. It reads "Face: Auto" and opens a short list: Auto, then each emotion in `emotes.json` by its word (for neutral, "calm" or another word you pick). The list comes from `emotes.json`, so an emotion added there shows with no widget change. It never takes keyboard focus (T64's step 5), so what I type stays in the speech field.
  2. **In every phase where I speak.** It shows in my scene turns, Free Talk, the last word and the reactions, in each phase's input row as T66 left it.
  3. **The pick goes with my turn.** My sends (`SendHumanTurn` and the other phases' sends) carry the pick. Auto means T77's way (the input LLM, or the keyword table). A pick overrides it and prints `NHOS: HUMAN emotion=<id> from=pick`.
  4. **Back to Auto each turn.** The chip starts at Auto at the start of each of my turns, so a pick never sticks by accident and leaves my robot with one face all show. If a first-time player would expect it to stay, say so under "## Findings" and ask me.
  5. **The timer and the preview line.** T66's timer sends the chip as it stands when time runs out. T64's preview line shows a pick in words, for example `looks worried · throw rose at @Meta: "Oops."`, and shows nothing for Auto.
- **Done when:**
  - In PIE (Test for Free, autoplay off, a 1600 × 900 window), there are screenshots of: the chip at Auto at the start of a turn; its list open; a pick shown in the preview line; and my robot showing that emotion when my line plays.
  - Sending a pick prints `NHOS: HUMAN emotion=<id> from=pick`, and the next turn starts at Auto.
  - Typing, opening the list, picking and typing on puts every letter in the speech field.
  - A free autoplay show in a game window reaches `NHOS: SHOW END` with no `LogScript` line.
  - Under "## Findings", Claude judges from the screenshots, as a critical first-time player would, whether the chip is easy to find and whether it crowds the input row. T34 polishes that row, so list anything for it there too.
