## Findings

- **The look holds up.** The robots, backdrops, props, the gold and crimson house and the whole UI read the same on both Android previews. Vulkan and ES 3.1 look alike. What changes: the near stalls get much darker without Lumen's bounce light, the robots cast no floor shadow (Stationary lights have no shadow on mobile with static lighting off), the floor loses its sheen, and the engine's two touch joysticks show.
- **Materials:** all Substrate materials compile for mobile except `M_GlowStickSword` (T119's grayscale sampler bug).
- **Load:** in a scene turn, desktop draws about 880 calls and 5.1 M triangles a frame, the Vulkan preview about 317 calls and 2.9 M triangles. `L_Stage` has 1,412 mesh parts and 3.5 M triangles with no LODs; the audience is 1.5 M of it. 100 of the 117 textures are 2048 × 2048.

### Ways of working found here

- **The editor's mobile preview in a game window:** add `-featureleveles31 -faketouches OVERRIDESP=<shader platform>_Preview -DeviceProfile=<profile>` to the game-window command, for example `OVERRIDESP=VULKAN_ES3_1_ANDROID_Preview -DeviceProfile=Android_Preview_Vulkan`. This is what the editor's "Mobile Preview (Standalone)" passes (`Engine/Source/Editor/UnrealEd/Private/PlayLevelNewProcess.cpp`). Without `_Preview` the engine logs "it is not a preview platform" and uses the generic `PCD3D_ES3_1`. The editor passes `dp.Override=<profile>` as a bare word, which a game window ignores (it stays on `WindowsEditor`); `-DeviceProfile=` works. The first start compiles mobile shaders for about 2 minutes.
- **Driving a game window without the mouse:** `PostMessage` of `WM_KEYDOWN` and `WM_KEYUP` to the window's handle reaches the game even in the background. On the title, Play has the focus, so Down then Enter starts Test for Free (`NHOS: TITLE click free`, then `PREP shown`). This gives Scene Prep shots, which autoplay skips (it shows and starts Scene Prep in the same moment).
- **`stat unit` and `stat rhi` in a game window:** `-ExecCmds="stat unit,stat rhi"` on the command line, then a `PrintWindow` capture.
