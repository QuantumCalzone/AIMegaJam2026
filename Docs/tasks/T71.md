# T71 Music for each place and each scene

Back to [the plan](../Plan.md). This task's title line, **Model and effort**, **Depends on**, **Needs**, **Touches** and any **Waiting:** or **Blocked:** line are in its entry there, under "Open".

## Task

- **Why:** my request on 2026-11-15. Each story should sound like its own place, and each part of the show should sound different from the others. Today two loops cover the whole game (T20): the title loop, and one 12-second show loop that plays under every scene, Free Talk and the vote, for all seven stories.
- **Steps:**
  1. **The track list.** One loop for each item below, and no loop used twice:
     - **Each scene card** in `Content/NHOS/Data/scenes.json`, keyed by its card id, in the style of its story. It plays from Scene Prep through the scene's turns. The cards today: `space_plans` (a space western on a desert planet), `mine_bridge` (an old dwarf mine with a sleeping fire monster), `dino_island` (a jungle theme park on opening day), `favor_father` (a crime family's garden wedding), `glowing_briefcase` (a retro diner on dance-contest night), `league_night` (a bowling alley on semifinal night) and `pick_a_pill` (a gray office inside a computer program).
     - **Each part of the show outside a scene:** the title screen (keep `S_MusicTitle` or replace it), Free Talk (and the last word, once T58 adds it), The Cut until the drum roll starts, and the end screen, with one loop for a win and one for a loss.
  2. **Find the tracks.** This step needs no editor.
     - Start with Kenney's Music Loops pack (CC0, in the bundle the team bought). It has 24 loops, and T20 uses 2 of them. The whole pack is in `Local/audio/kenney/Music Loops/` on the Windows PC only (git ignores `Local/`). T20's picks are in `SourceAssets/Audio/kenney/`.
     - Some names suggest a fit (for example "Italian Mom", "Space Cadet" and "Retro Mystic"), but pick on more than the name: the length, the tempo, and whether the loop repeats cleanly.
     - Kenney's Music Jingles are too short to loop, but one can play as the win or lose sting before the loop.
     - If no Kenney loop fits a card, use another free library whose license allows a game (CC0 or CC BY), or an AI music tool whose terms allow game use. Get my OK before anything that costs money.
     - Copy each pick to `SourceAssets/Audio/`. Add a row to `Docs/Assets.md` with its source and license. A CC BY track needs a credit line, like T20's two.
  3. **Import** each track into `Content/NHOS/Audio/Music/` as `S_Music<Key>`, looping, on `SC_Music`. Use T20's auto-import rule (in "Unreal MCP rules found in this task" in `Docs/tasks/T20.md`). Set each wave's volume so the scene, Free Talk and Cut loops sit near T20's show loop (0.25 effective) under the lines. The title and end loops can be louder (T20's title loop is 0.35).
  4. **`BPC_ShowAudio` picks the track by key.** `SwapMusic` gets a map from key to sound: a `Name` to `SoundBase` map variable on the component, or a `select` as it uses now. Don't read asset paths from a JSON file: the cook packs only the assets that something references, so a track named only in JSON would be missing from a packaged build. A key with no track plays the old show loop, so a new card still has music. `NHOS: SOUND music <key>` prints as it does now.
  5. **The show asks for the right key.**
     - In `BP_Show.RunCue`, the `prep` cue asks for the card's id, and the `phase` cue asks for the key of its phase.
     - Take both from what the cue carries, not from the live `Phase` variable on `BP_Show`: the show's logic runs ahead of the line queue (T30).
     - Check that `CardId` already holds the new card when the `prep` cue plays. If `ScenePrep` sets it, ask for the music after `ScenePrep`.
     - The Cut's loop stops, or drops low, when `StartDrum` starts, so nothing plays under the drum roll and the reveal.
     - `EndCheer` starts the win or lose loop a few seconds into T20's applause or laughs.
  6. **A short fade between tracks** (about 1 s), if it can be done. T20 found that the DSL picks the Synth Component's `FadeOut` and `FadeIn`, so a fade needs `create_node` with `declaring_class` `/Script/Engine.AudioComponent`. If that fails, keep T20's hard cut, and write why under "## Findings".
- **Done when:**
  - A free autoplay show with `fast_lines` reaches `NHOS: SHOW END` with no new `LogScript` warning. Its log has, for each scene in order: `NHOS: SOUND music <card id>` for the card in play at Scene Prep, then the Free Talk key, then the Cut key. At the end, the log has the win or lose key.
  - Each card id in `scenes.json` has its own track in the map, and no track is used for two keys.
  - `Docs/Assets.md` has a row for each new track.
  - Claude can't hear the tracks. Under "## Findings", it writes why it picked each one (its name, length, tempo and what the pack says about it) and the volume it set, as T20 did.
