## Task

- **Why:** my words on 2026-11-19: "I don't like the audience on the sides of the theater. They're just standing there. They're not in a real balcony seat." T106 put two audience bots in each of the four theater boxes, standing on the balcony ledge (`'bots'` in `Docs/tasks/T106-build/s05_house.py`, z −38 from the box's pivot). From the house camera they look like figures on a shelf in front of the railing, not like people seated in an opera box. The bots in the stalls sit behind their seat backs and look right. This is the first of the art updates I want before the itch.io media (T113).
- **Steps:**
  1. **Look.** Read T106's findings on the boxes ("The theater boxes" in `Docs/tasks/T106.md`) and `Docs/tasks/T106-build/s05_house.py`. In PIE (Test for Free), screenshot the house camera at 1600 × 900 and a close-up of each box: the "before".
  2. **Seats.** Give each box real seats behind its railing, facing the stage, as an opera box has chairs. Reuse the stalls seat from T106 at the box's scale, or another piece already in `Content/NHOS/Stage/Set/`. If nothing fits, tell me what a new seat model would cost, and ask before any paid generation. If the space behind the seats reads as an empty shelf, give the box a floor and a dark back (a curtain or a wall), so it reads as a room.
  3. **Seat the bots.** Move each box bot off the ledge into a seat behind the railing. It sits as the stalls bots do: lowered so that the railing and the seat hide its lower body, with its head and shoulders above the rail, turned toward the stage. Keep each bot's color and hat. Use two bots per box, or three if a box looks empty with two. Nothing in a box may cover the stage from the house camera.
  4. **Keep it in the build.** Make the change in `s05_house.py`, or in a new script beside it that the folder's `README.md` lists, so that a re-run of T106's build keeps the seats. Save `L_Stage`.
  5. **Check.** In PIE (Test for Free), screenshot the house camera at 1600 × 900 and 1440 × 900 in every phase, and the box close-ups. Judge them as a critical first-time player would: do the boxes read as an audience seated in opera boxes? A free autoplay show in a game window on the saved level reaches `NHOS: SHOW END` with no `LogScript` line. The audience costs no more frame time than T106 measured (about 1.4 ms).
  6. **Show me.** Make `Docs/Art/Set/box_seats_compare.png` (before and after: the house camera and one box close-up) and ask for my OK. If the seated bots still look lifeless, show me, and ask whether they should also move (a small idle sway, a turn of the head, a clap at the cut), here or in a task of its own. That changes `BP_AudienceBot`, so it waits for my answer.
  7. **The design doc.** If "The stage" in `Docs/Design/ArtInspiration.md` needs a word for this (the box audience is seated), show me the edit and make it after my OK.
- **Done when:** every box bot sits in a seat behind its railing, facing the stage, in `L_Stage` as saved and in T106's build script; the house camera shows no bot on a ledge and nothing in a box that covers the stage; a free autoplay show reaches `NHOS: SHOW END` with no `LogScript` line; the before-and-after sheet is in `Docs/Art/Set/`; and I have approved it.
