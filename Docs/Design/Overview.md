# No Humans On Set - Game Design Document

Nov 13, 2026 · @George Katsaros

---

- No Humans On Set
  - A 15ish-minute social-deduction game for the 2026 Epic AI MegaJam. Theme: “Director’s Cut.”
- Premise
  - Tonight is opening night for the world’s first all-AI improv show. One problem: you’re human. Disguised as one of the bots, you must fool them all the way to the final two. The AI knows someone in the cast isn’t one of its own, and the cast votes after every scene to cut one actor. Make it to the final two, or to the end of the last scene, and humanity wins the stage.
  - Each show’s AI cast is three or four petty robot parodies of AI lab leaders, each on its own provider (see Bot Prompts). You’re secretly one more robot among them, with no persona. On stage everyone goes by a Player number, so the AIs can’t spot you by name.
- Fantasy & peak
  - Fantasy: you’re the only human in an all-AI cast, and you win by fooling them.
  - Emotional peak: an actor is cut, the reveal says AI, and you watch the machines realize they voted out one of their own.
- How a show works
  - Scene Prep: everyone gets a scene card, and yours shows a clue to the director’s note: its category in scene 1, then a new kind of clue each scene. Only the bots get the note itself, and they know you don’t have it.
  - The Scene: act it out by clicking what your bot does and typing what it says.
  - Free Talk: the cast argues about who the human is.
  - The Cut: the cast votes, the actor with the most votes is cut, and every bot reacts. A tie cuts nobody.
  - Repeat until two actors remain or the last scene ends. If you’re cut, you lose.
