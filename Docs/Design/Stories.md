# Stories

Seven film parodies replace the six old cards. This is the draft for review (2026-11-15): one agent wrote each card, and nothing is in the game yet. Parody rules hold: new names, no trademarked terms, no quotes, no logos. Each card's banned-word list is in its draft file in `Docs/StoryDrafts/` and moves into `Tools/check_prompts.py` with the card.

## Who Hid the Plans in the Robot?

Parody of Star Wars: Episode IV – A New Hope. Card id `space_plans`.

Planet Dust, a desert world with two suns. A rebel hid the stolen plans to a moon-sized battle station inside a tin-can robot, and a masked villain who breathes very loudly is coming to get them back. The Riser is the cockpit of a battered space truck.

Backdrop (`desert_planet`): A desert at dusk under two suns, with a rusty dome hut, a battered space truck and a suspicious grey moon.

Props: tin-can robot, glow-stick sword, toy blaster; spare: piggy bank.

![Renders of the space saga props: tin-can robot, glow-stick sword, toy blaster, and the spare piggy bank](images/Stories/space_plans.jpg)

| Role | Want | Headpiece |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A farm kid | Climb into the space truck on the Riser and leave this dusty planet today, taking the tin-can robot with you if you can. | wide-brim hat (sandy straw yellow) |
| A masked villain | Breathe very loudly while you hunt for the tin-can robot, then grab it and march it to the Back, where your shuttle waits. | saucepan helmet (black) |
| An old desert hermit | Teach anyone who will listen your ancient art of waving the glow-stick sword, then give it to your best student. | hood (earthy brown) |
| A smuggler pilot | Guard your space truck on the Riser with the toy blaster, and let nobody aboard until they hand you something valuable up front. | dark shades (black lenses) |
| A rebel princess | Grab the toy blaster and keep the tin-can robot out of the wrong hands until you can give it to the one actor you trust. | tiara (silver and white) |

## The Ring Is Mine, I Mean Ours

Parody of The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring. Card id `mine_bridge`.

Deep in an old dwarf mine, a band of travelers must cross a narrow stone bridge before a big, grumpy fire monster who hates noise wakes up. The cursed gold ring they are hauling to a volcano tempts everyone who touches it. The Riser is the narrow bridge, and the monster snores at the Back.

Backdrop (`dwarf_mine`): A huge old dwarf mine: carved stone pillars, a deep chasm with a thin stone bridge, and a warm orange glow rising from far below.

Props: cursed ring, glowing staff, water bucket; spare: frying pan.

![Renders of the ring quest props: cursed ring, glowing staff, water bucket, and the spare frying pan](images/Stories/mine_bridge.jpg)

| Role | Want | Headpiece |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A barefoot homebody | Grab the cursed ring, cross the Riser bridge to Right, and talk anyone who gets hold of it into giving it back. | bob wig (chestnut brown) |
| A bossy old wizard | Grab the glowing staff, plant yourself on the Riser, and keep everyone off the bridge until you decide it is safe. | wizard hat (dusty grey) |
| A brooding ranger | Get the cursed ring into your own hands for just a moment, from the Prop Table or from whoever has it, and swear you were only checking it for scratches. | hood (moss green) |
| A loud, proud dwarf | Prove this old mine has the best echo anywhere by throwing the water bucket at Back as hard as you can. | horned helmet with beard (iron grey helmet, ginger beard) |
| A vain elf | Keep your hair perfect in the footlights at Front, and talk anyone holding the water bucket into handing it to you. | tiara (pale silver) |

## Dino Island Is Mostly Safe

Parody of Jurassic Park. Card id `dino_island`.

A billionaire cloned dinosaurs for an island theme park, and today is opening day. The fences just lost power, a dino egg rolled out of the hatchery, and the tour group is still out in the jungle. The Riser is the park's lookout tower.

Backdrop (`jungle_park`): A steamy island jungle with a sagging electric fence, a tilted warning sign, giant muddy footprints and a smoking volcano far away.

Props: dino egg, meaty drumstick, safety waiver; spare: dino plush.

![Renders of the dino park props: dino egg, meaty drumstick, safety waiver, and the spare dino plush](images/Stories/dino_island.jpg)

| Role | Want | Headpiece |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The billionaire park owner | Carry the dino egg to every guest in turn and insist it proves the park is perfectly safe. | top hat (cream white) |
| A dinosaur expert | Get every guest up on the Riser before something big shows up, and scold anyone who climbs back down. | wide-brim hat (olive green) |
| A runaway dinosaur with tiny arms | Get your dino egg back from wherever it ends up, and roar at anyone who touches it. | dino hood (grass green) |
| A kid on the tour | Feed the meaty drumstick to a dinosaur, or to whoever looks most like one, no matter what the grown-ups say. | ball cap (cherry red) |
| The park's nervous lawyer | Hand the safety waiver to every guest to sign, then hide at the Back far from anything with teeth. | dark shades (black) |

## The Favor Father

Parody of The Godfather. Card id `favor_father`.

The family boss's daughter is getting married in the garden. Tradition says nobody may say no to a favor on her wedding day, so every guest is asking for one. The Riser is the boss's big chair at the head table.

Backdrop (`garden_wedding`): A sunny garden wedding: a striped party tent, paper lanterns, lemon trees, a tiered cake and a pink stucco villa.

Props: plush horse head, cannoli tray, wedding envelope; spare: bowl of oranges.

![Renders of the crime family props: plush horse head, cannoli tray, wedding envelope; the spare bowl of oranges has no model yet](images/Stories/favor_father.jpg)

| Role | Want | Headpiece |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The family boss, father of the bride | Sit on the Riser like a king, say yes to every favor anyone asks, and seal each deal by handing over the cannoli tray. | fedora (charcoal black with a red band) |
| The hot-headed eldest son | Take offense at every tiny insult to the family, throw the cannoli tray at whoever said it, then storm off to sulk at the Back. | ball cap (tomato red, worn backwards) |
| The reluctant youngest son | Stay out of the family business: hide at the Front by the footlights and dodge anyone who comes over to ask a favor, because today you cannot say no. | hood (olive green) |
| The family adviser | Fix every problem the same quiet way: grab the plush horse head and throw it at whoever is causing trouble. | dark shades (black) |
| A washed-up crooner | Win a starring movie role: croon a sad song at the Front, then give the wedding envelope to whoever looks most in charge and beg for help. | top hat (glittery purple) |

## What's in the Glowing Briefcase?

Parody of Pulp Fiction. Card id `glowing_briefcase`.

A retro diner on dance-contest night, and everyone inside is up to something. A glowing briefcase that nobody may open sits on the Prop Table, which is the diner counter. The Riser is the dance-contest stage.

Backdrop (`retro_diner`): A retro diner: a chrome counter with cherry-red stools, a checkered floor, plump booths, a big wall clock and wide windows onto a sunny parking lot.

Props: glowing briefcase, gold pocket watch, giant milkshake; spare: giant cheeseburger.

![Renders of the diner props: glowing briefcase, gold pocket watch, giant milkshake, and the spare giant cheeseburger](images/Stories/glowing_briefcase.jpg)

| Role | Want | Headpiece |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A chatty errand man in a black suit | Carry the glowing briefcase to the boss's booth at the Back, chatting about burgers all the way, and let nobody open it. | dark shades (glossy black) |
| A nosy errand man in a black suit | Get the glowing briefcase to yourself at the Front and peek inside it while nobody is looking. | fedora (dark gray with a skinny black band) |
| The boss's bored wife | Win the dance contest on the Riser, pull anyone you like up to be your partner, and claim the giant milkshake as your prize. | bob wig (jet black) |
| A runaway boxer who refused to lose | Grab your lucky gold pocket watch before anyone else can and keep it safe at Left, dodging anyone who asks about the big fight. | hood (red with gold trim) |
| A smooth fixer in a tuxedo | Clean up the chaos in record time: grab any prop left lying loose and throw it back onto the Prop Table. | top hat (black with a white band) |

## League Night, Bathrobe Optional

Parody of The Big Lebowski. Card id `league_night`.

League night at the neighborhood bowling alley, the night of the big semifinal. A slacker in a bathrobe has been mistaken for a millionaire with the same name, and now somebody has to drop off a ransom bag at the Back before the last frame. The Riser is the winners' podium.

Backdrop (`bowling_lanes`): A retro bowling alley on league night: shiny wooden lanes, rows of white pins, a ball return and a starry ceiling.

Props: rolled-up rug, bowling ball, ransom bag; spare: milk carton.

![Renders of the bowling props: rolled-up rug, bowling ball, ransom bag, and the spare milk carton](images/Stories/league_night.jpg)

| Role | Want | Headpiece |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A laid-back slacker in a bathrobe | Replace your ruined rug with the rolled-up rug, throw it down at Center, and stay relaxed no matter who yells at you. | hood (oatmeal beige) |
| A hot-headed teammate who knows every rule | Take charge of the ransom bag, throw it at the Back yourself, and call a foul on anyone who questions your plan. | saucepan helmet (olive green) |
| A quiet bowler nobody listens to | Get the bowling ball and finally take your turn, rolling it at the pins at the Back, before anyone cuts you off. | ball cap (pale sky blue) |
| A flashy rival bowler | Claim the Riser as your winners' podium before a single frame is bowled, and hold up the bowling ball like you already won. | top hat (hot pink) |
| An avant-garde artist | Get the rolled-up rug back, since it was your mother's, and throw it onto the Riser as your newest work of art. | bob wig (cobalt blue) |

## Pick a Pill, Any Pill

Parody of The Matrix. Card id `pick_a_pill`.

Midnight in a gray office that is secretly a computer program. Someone here is about to be offered a red pill or a blue pill, agents in dark suits are closing in, and the ringing phone is the only way out. The Riser is the boss's big leather armchair.

Backdrop (`midnight_cubicles`): A gray office after midnight: rows of clay cubicles, one buzzing ceiling light, a rainy window and old computer screens dripping with green light.

Props: red pill, blue pill, ringing phone; spare: bent spoon.

![Renders of the simulation props: red pill, blue pill, ringing phone, and the spare bent spoon](images/Stories/pick_a_pill.jpg)

| Role | Want | Headpiece |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A sleepy office worker | Refuse to choose: give away any pill or prop that reaches you, and keep sneaking back to the Riser for a nap. | beanie (dull office gray-blue) |
| A grand leader in a long coat | Grab a pill, settle into the Riser, and give it to whoever looks most confused, swishing your coat dramatically. | wide-brim hat (deep maroon) |
| A rebel in black leather | Grab the ringing phone and give it to whoever seems ready to escape, and dodge in slow motion at Center whenever anything is thrown. | bob wig (glossy black) |
| A stern agent in a dark suit | Gather up every loose pill and the ringing phone, throw them back onto the Prop Table, and glare at any actor who looks suspiciously like a pill. | dark shades (black) |
| A fortune-teller baking cookies | Stir your cookie dough at the Back, then give a pill to whoever you predict will pick the wrong one. | wizard hat (soft lavender) |
