; WBP_Stage.SetPhase(Phase): the phase header's text (the "phase" cue, BP_Show.RunCue).
; T64: also opens or closes the action builder (PhaseShown: open right after BuilderTurn, closed at any other header)
; and fits the lower panel to it (FitBuilder).
; T66: Pass hides in my scene turns, and the last turn's timer gives up its space (ShapeRow).
; T34 (row 4): the header shows only the phase, the part before the first ":" ("THE SCENE: SCENE 2, TURN 1 OF 3" ->
; "THE SCENE"); a scene turn's "TURN n OF 3" shows as pips under it (SetPips); the scene number is on the clapperboard (T53).
; BP_Show's header texts don't change: BPC_Analytics.CuePhase reads them (T68).
(fn SetPhase (Phase)
  (bind (_l _r) (CallFunction|HeaderSplit :Text Phase))
  (Class|Text|SetText :Text (Utilities|Text|ToText(String) _l) :self (Variables|WBP_Stage|GetPhaseHeader))
  (CallFunction|SetPips :Detail _r)
  (bind _typed (Utilities|String|ToString(Text) (Class|TextBox|GetText :self (Variables|WBP_Stage|GetInputBox))))
  (Class|WBPActionBuilder|PhaseShown :self (Variables|WBP_Stage|GetActionBuilder) :Text _typed)
  (CallFunction|ShapeRow)
  (CallFunction|FitBuilder))
