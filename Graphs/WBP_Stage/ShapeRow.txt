; T66: the input row per phase, at every header (SetPhase). In my scene turns the builder shows and Pass doesn't: No action
; is how a scene turn says nothing (the spec's row: the field, the counter, Action! and the timer). Every other header shows
; Pass, enabled only on my Free Talk line, last word or reaction (SetPassEnabled, as now). The last turn's timer gives up
; its space and the wide panel here; the next turn's TimerStart decides again.
(fn ShapeRow ()
  (Widget|SetVisibility :self (Variables|WBP_Stage|GetTimerSize) :InVisibility "Collapsed")
  (set TimerWide false)
  (bind _on (Class|WBPActionBuilder|IsOn :self (Variables|WBP_Stage|GetActionBuilder)))
  (if _on
    (Widget|SetVisibility :self (Variables|WBP_Stage|GetPassButton) :InVisibility "Collapsed")
    (else
      (Widget|SetVisibility :self (Variables|WBP_Stage|GetPassButton) :InVisibility "Visible"))))
