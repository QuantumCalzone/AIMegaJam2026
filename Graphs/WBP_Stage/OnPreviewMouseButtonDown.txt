; T65: any click in the game window passes here first (a preview event tunnels from WBP_Stage down to the widget under the
; cursor). It only notes the click, so HoldFocus gives the keyboard back to the speech field if the click took it; the
; click itself goes on as before. An override of UserWidget's OnPreviewMouseButtonDown (add_function_graph).
(fn OnPreviewMouseButtonDown (MyGeometry MouseEvent)
  (set ClickAsked true)
  (return (Widget|EventReply|Unhandled)))
