; T34 (row 2): a shown tag hides (Hidden, keeping its place) while another robot's bubble covers it, or shows again.
(fn TagHide (S Hid)
  (if Hid
    (Widget|SetVisibility :self (get (V TagFrames) S) :InVisibility "Hidden")
    (else
      (Widget|SetVisibility :self (get (V TagFrames) S) :InVisibility "HitTestInvisible"))))
