; T68: the end screen, from RunCue's "end" branch: the last call counts, then show_end with T54's WinReason
; (caught, final_two, last_scene). show_end is the show's last event. result is win or lose (NHOS: SHOW END).
; Left for the chain's part: BPC_LLMClient.FlushCalls doesn't exist yet. Once it does, add as the first line:
;   (Class|BPCLLMClient|FlushCalls :self (V Client))
(fn ShowEnd (Win Reason)
  (set TmpStr (select Win "win" "lose"))
  (bind _f (Json|LoadJsonfromString "{}"))
  (Json|SetField _f "result" (V TmpStr))
  (Json|SetField _f "reason" Reason)
  (CallFunction|Send :Event "show_end" :Fields _f))
