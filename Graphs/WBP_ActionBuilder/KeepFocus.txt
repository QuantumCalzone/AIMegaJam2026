; T64: after a click on an action or a chip, the keyboard focus goes back to the input box that SetFocusBack named,
; so what I type next still lands there (step 5). Without SetFocusBack it does nothing. NullWidget is never set.
(fn KeepFocus ()
  (if (== (V FocusBack) (V NullWidget))
    (return))
  (Widget|SetKeyboardFocus :self (V FocusBack)))
