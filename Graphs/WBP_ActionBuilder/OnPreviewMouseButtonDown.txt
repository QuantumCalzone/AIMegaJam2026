; T69: every mouse-down inside the builder passes here first, a greyed (disabled) action button's too: a preview event
; tunnels from the root down to the widget under the cursor (T65's WBP_Stage override sees every click the same way).
; GreyClickAt finds whether it was on a greyed action. Always Unhandled, so every click works as before.
; An override of UserWidget's OnPreviewMouseButtonDown (add_function_graph).
(fn OnPreviewMouseButtonDown (MyGeometry MouseEvent)
  (bind _pos (Input|PointerEvent|GetScreenSpacePosition MouseEvent))
  (CallFunction|GreyClickAt :Pos _pos)
  (return (Widget|EventReply|Unhandled)))
