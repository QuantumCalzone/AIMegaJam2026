; T64: the typed line, live, for the preview line (WBP_Stage's OnTextChanged on the input box). After I send, the
; preview keeps the turn I sent, so the box being cleared doesn't change it.
; T65: the preview line is drawn word by word (DrawPreview), so a mention is a chip.
(fn SetLine (Text)
  (if (V Sent)
    (return))
  (set Line Text)
  (bind _p (CallFunction|MakePreview))
  (CallFunction|DrawPreview :Text _p))
