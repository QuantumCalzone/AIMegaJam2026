; T38: the bubble styles itself from UIStyle when it is built, and hides unless a line already shows: a screen-space
; WidgetComponent builds its widget a frame after SetWidget, so a ShowLine called right after SetWidget comes first.
; While a line or the dots show, it places itself each frame.
(event UserInterface|EventConstruct
  (CallFunction|ApplyStyle)
  (if (not (V Shown))
    (CallFunction|HideNow)))

(event UserInterface|EventTick (MyGeometry InDeltaTime)
  (if (V Shown)
    (CallFunction|Place :Geo MyGeometry)))
