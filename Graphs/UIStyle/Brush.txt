; UIStyle.Brush(Fill, Outline, Width, Radius) -> SlateBrush. A rounded box with an outline: every panel, card and button state.
(fn Brush (Fill Outline Width Radius)
  (return (Utilities|Struct|MakeSlateBrush
    :TintColor (Utilities|Struct|MakeSlateColor :SpecifiedColor Fill)
    :DrawAs "RoundedBox"
    :OutlineSettings (Utilities|Struct|MakeSlateBrushOutlineSettings
      :CornerRadii (Math|Vector4|MakeVector4 Radius Radius Radius Radius)
      :Color (Utilities|Struct|MakeSlateColor :SpecifiedColor Outline)
      :Width Width
      :RoundingType "FixedRadius"))))
