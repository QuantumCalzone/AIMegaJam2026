; BPC_Stagehand.EndLine(Win, Reason) (T61): the end screen's line: "win", or on a loss T60's top reason when given, else "lose".
(fn EndLine (Win Reason)
  (if (or Win (Utilities|String|IsEmpty Reason))
    (CallFunction|Say :Key (select Win "win" "lose"))
    (return))
  (add (V Shown) "lose")
  (CallFunction|Enqueue :Key "lose" :Line Reason :Spot "end"))
