; BPC_Stagehand.EndShow(Win, Reason) (T61): one node at the end screen. On a teaching show it says the win or lose line
; over the end screen, then the next show won't teach (until the title's toggle). Reason is T60's top-reason line on a loss,
; or "" (then stagehand.json's lose line, the doc's example, is said). TipsOn stays true until the next BeginShow,
; so the end line still shows after the file says false. Autoplay never writes the file. A show that taught nothing writes nothing.
(fn EndShow (Win Reason)
  (if (not (V TipsOn))
    (return))
  (CallFunction|EndLine :Win Win :Reason Reason)
  (if (V Autoplay)
    (return))
  (CallFunction|SetTipsNext :On false))
