; T87: the face part of StageLine (T77), in its own function: Seat's robot shows emotion Id (BP_Robot.Emote). In
; StageLine, this Utilities|IsValid branch at the end of an if body made the DSL put the stage director call in that
; if's else path, so from T77 to T87 no line's action played (a seat is always in range). Here nothing follows it.
(fn EmoteSeat (Seat Id)
  (if (and (>= Seat 0) (< Seat (len (V Robots))))
    (set EmoBot (get (V Robots) Seat))
    (Utilities|IsValid (V EmoBot)
      (:"Is Valid"
        (Class|BPRobot|Emote :self (V EmoBot) :Emotion Id))
      (:"Is Not Valid"))))
