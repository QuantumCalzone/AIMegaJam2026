; T77: the one place my emotion is settled, for every line of mine: HumanEmo (a line through SpellAsk) and TakeEmo
; (a pass). It prints NHOS: HUMAN emotion=<id> from=<llm|keywords|none|pick> and returns the id that goes on my line.
; T78: a pick on the face chip overrides T77's pick (ApplyFace): from=pick.
(fn HumanFeels (Id From)
  (bind (_fid _ffrom) (CallFunction|ApplyFace :AutoId Id :AutoFrom From))
  (print (cat "NHOS: HUMAN emotion=" _fid " from=" _ffrom))
  (return _fid))
