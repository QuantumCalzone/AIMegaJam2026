; T38: the bubble's anchor rides on the name label, 26 cm over the label's location (the bottom of its text, WorldSize 24):
; in PIE at 1600 x 900, 14 cm (Part 1's canvas number) put the tail's tip into the letters, and a tall bubble's box
; over them. T57's "(passed)" line sits above the name, so the anchor goes up one more line (24 cm) while it shows.
(fn BubbleLift ()
  (if (not (V HasBubble))
    (return))
  (Transformation|SetRelativeLocation :self (V Bubble) :NewLocation (Math|Vector|MakeVector :X 0.0 :Y 0.0 :Z (select (V PassedOn) 50.0 26.0))))
